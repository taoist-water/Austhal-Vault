# Duchy: Stonereach (The High Shields)
#location/region #faction/state #status/draft

> "Corvus fell and Stonereach's tolls tripled in a season. Down on the coast they call it ash-money. Up here we call it the only good year we ever had." — Kettlehold toll-clerk

> "The dwarves built the walls that tax them. Think on that before you call either side a fool." — Keeper Merrit Dray, who was born up there

---

## 🗺️ Lay of the Land
- **Position:** the high passes of the north-eastern [[The Jagged Spine|Jagged Spine]], above the Concord Road's last leg. The mouth of the [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast#5. 💀 The Scarred Land: Duchy of Corvus (The Fallen Crown)|Corvus Scar]] lies to the south and west. Along the crest to the west is **[[Ubaraz Kingdom|Ubaraz]]**, at the valley's inland head. De Vonce lies below, and Valerius's cliff coast is to the south-east (see [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast#🧭 Lay of the Coast|Lay of the Coast]]).
- **Terrain:** sheer granite, pass-bowls and scree.
  - **Thraw**, the peak, gives its name to **Thraw's Pass**, which carries the Concord Road up to Granite Spire.
  - **Kettlehold** sits in a pass-bowl around **Kettle Tarn**.
- **The passes:**
  - **Thraw's Pass** — the north-eastern trade artery, from the Road to Granite Spire. Guarded by **Thrawgate**.
  - **The Long Graft** — the high road west along the crest to Ubaraz. It is the longest, the lowest and the most heavily tolled pass in the Spine. **Since 8 A.A. it has been Ubaraz's only lawful road to the coast.**
  - **Sleetgate** — closed most of the Winter Moons.
  - **The Notch** — a goat-track, for smugglers only.
- **Rivers:** **The Tinwater** runs off the mines, and below Deepgraft the tailings make it undrinkable.
- **Weather:** the Winter Moons close the passes. Villages like **Sleetfall** are snowed in four months a year and govern themselves because nobody else can reach them. The year begins when **Pass-Opening** is declared.
- **Travel to Port Nevarellon:** ~6–8 days by horse on the Concord Road through Thraw's Pass, longer or impassable in deep winter; 10–14 days for a loaded granite train. The Road runs through De Vonce the whole way down.

## 🏰 House Scutarius
The ruling house is **Scutarius**, an old Landed name the coast shortens to *the Shields*, as it does the duchy. The seat is **[[Granite Spire]]**.

### Duke Brennus Scutarius
58. Hard, frugal and careful. He counts everything twice and trusts the second count.
- **His father** held the coronet when Corvus fell. In 8 A.A. the old Corvus road to Ubaraz died, and the Long Graft's tolls tripled. The house has been rich ever since, and knows exactly what it was rich from.
- **Brennus** has kept the tolls high, the passes open and the dwarves content. He has never once said the word *ash-money* aloud.

### The Family
- **Cato Scutarius** — 36, the heir, and a widower. The prize [[Tythius De Vonce]] wants for Sheandri (see [[Duchy of De Vonce]]).
- **Aemilia Scutarius** — the Duke's niece, married to Calvus Hordeus. Aerthos got to Stonereach first (see [[Duchy of Aerthos]]).

**The match.** Brennus is weighing the two lowland houses against each other. Aerthos feeds the passes, and through them Ubaraz. De Vonce holds the Road's fork at Iron-Spire and the largest levy on the coast, and every load of granite goes down through its country. A niece already sits at Millhaven. The heir is still unspoken for.

## 🧱 The Dwarves
Stonereach is a human duchy built by dwarven hands.
- **The engineering.** Granite Spire, the toll-keeps and the deep mines are all Ubaraz work. Ubaraz **Keystone crews** live in Granite Spire's lower works and keep the load-bearing stone. They answer to Ubaraz, not to the Duke.
- **The mines.** At **Deepgraft** the shafts are ducal and the crews are Ubaraz contract labour. No shaft opens without the stamp of the master-surveyor, [[Brekka Stonegraft]].
- **The road.** Ubaraz's trade comes east along the Long Graft and down Thraw's Pass, and pays Stonereach's toll at every gate. Ubaraz has not opened its down-valley gates since 8 A.A. It pays the tolls without complaint and says nothing about why.
- **The humans of the passes** grew up next to dwarves and take them for granted, the way lowlanders take the sea for granted. Keeper [[Keeper Merrit Dray|Merrit Dray]] of the Plumb Court is a Stonereach hauler's son.

> **GM-facing — who owns the citadel.** A dwarven citadel is built with its own undoing in it. The Keystone crews can bring down Granite Spire's upper works — the hall, the keep and the house — and leave the lower works standing. Brennus suspects it. His father knew, and did not tell him. It is why the Duke has never raised the toll on Ubaraz's own goods, only on everyone else's.

> **GM-facing — the shut gates.** Ubaraz watched the 8 A.A. breach from the far end of its own valley. It sealed its down-valley gates that season and has never opened them, and it would rather pay a human duke's tolls for ever than send a cart down the Corvus valley again. What it saw is for the Corvus pass.

## ⛏️ Economy & Logistics

| What | Where | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| **Tolls** | Thrawgate, Kettlehold, the Long Graft keeps | The house. The duchy's real wealth, and ash-money since 8 A.A. |
| Granite | **Grimscree**, a scree-slope quarry with appalling attrition | Down the Road to the city, where there is no masons' guild: the stone-work goes through Ubaraz factors (see [[Council of Five]]) |
| Ore, and the deep seams | **Deepgraft** | Ducal ore; and **Sun-Iron**, which the [[The Iron-Anchor Syndicate\|Iron-Anchor]] smuggles out over the Notch |
| Ubaraz's trade | The Long Graft and Thraw's Pass | The coast — tolled at every gate |

- **Stonereach cannot feed itself.** Its grain comes up the Road from Aerthos, and part of it goes on west along the Long Graft to Ubaraz. A bad Aerthos harvest is a hungry winter in the passes, and a hungrier one in Ubaraz.
- **The Road runs through De Vonce.** The Stonereach toll-keep and the De Vonce garrison detachment on the Road's north-east leg have been quarrelling over winter provisioning all season. [[Jeerdan Darcy]] is trying to keep it from becoming a killing.

## 🎉 Customs
- **Pass-Opening.** The first safe crossing of Thraw's Pass is declared, the gates open, and trade resumes. In Stonereach the year starts here, not on First Keel.
- **Storm-right.** Every keep in the passes must keep its lamp lit and its door unbarred when a storm comes down. Anyone caught on the road is taken in, and **in a storm the toll is waived.** No Duke of Stonereach has ever repealed it, including the ones who would have liked to.

## 🕯️ Faith
- **The Rime-Fang** keeps the shrine at Pilgrim's Notch, the coast's only free roof on the Long Graft (see [[The Cult of the Rime-Fang]]).
- **The Iron Horizon.** The passes claim Saint Senecus: every keep says his doomed pass was one of theirs (see [[The Cult of the Iron Horizon]]).
- **The Ashen Veil** buries the passes' dead at Featherfall. In Deepcold the dead wait in the snow for Pass-Opening, and the grave-keepers come up with the first carts (see [[The Cult of the Ashen Veil]]).
- **The dwarves'** own faith is theirs, and they do not discuss it with the Shields.

## ⚖️ Leverage & Power Dynamics
- **Over Ubaraz:** the only road to the coast.
- **Ubaraz over Stonereach:** the foundations, the mines and the engineering — and the shut gates.
- **Over the lowlands:** granite; the passes; the north-eastern trade.
- **The lowlands over Stonereach:** bread from Aerthos; the Road through De Vonce.
- **The Council** has never given a dwarf a seat, though Ubaraz is among the city's oldest trading partners (see [[Council of Five]]).
- **Vulnerabilities:**
  - Hunger.
  - Winter.
  - A road that runs through someone else's duchy.
  - Engineers who answer to a foreign king.
  - Being the house that got rich the year Corvus died — and everyone on the coast remembers it.

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 1**, a Ducal power.
- **Chokepoint:** the passes — Ubaraz's road, and the north-eastern trade.
- **Enforcement:** terrain and tolls. The law is enforced by the impracticality of going around.
- **Geographic anchor:** Granite Spire and Thraw's Pass.
- **Bypass:** the Notch, for smugglers; the sea, for anyone who can reach it; and for Ubaraz, the Corvus valley, which it will not use.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is Grimscree, the Tinwater, and ash-money.
  - **The mote** is storm-right, and the free roof at Pilgrim's Notch.
