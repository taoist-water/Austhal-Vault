# Duchy: Aerthos (The Breadbasket)
#location/region #faction/state #status/draft

> "Ten children. Six names and four numbers. Count them like sacks, the tenants say, and he does — and he writes to every one of them, every month, in his own hand." — a Sheafcross factor

> "The steward takes a third. The factor takes the seed back with interest. The Duke takes nothing, and thanks you. That's how you know he's the one to be afraid of." — Bram Hollick, tenant near Millhaven

## 🗺️ Lay of the Land
- **Position:** inland, west of [[Port Nevarellon]], along the River Aer. De Vonce's foothills rise to the north-east. To the north the plain runs up to the south-western edge of the [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast#5. 💀 The Scarred Land: Duchy of Corvus (The Fallen Crown)|Corvus Scar]]. To the west it fades into the wild inland, toward the [[The Kald Mountain Territory|Kald]] (see [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast#🧭 Lay of the Coast|Lay of the Coast]]).
- **Terrain:** flat, open and fertile, and almost impossible to defend.
  - **The Barley Flats** are the coast's real breadbasket.
  - **The Wether Downs** are old sheep-country, full of boundary stones older than the duchy.
  - **The Fallow Wood** is the ducal hunting reserve, and is poached constantly.
  - **The Wide Fallow** is the plain under the Scar's south-western edge, which was Corvus farmland until 8 A.A.
- **Rivers:**
  - **The Aer.** The duchy is named for the river, not the reverse. Below **Millhaven** it breaks into **the Nine Sisters**, a braided reach whose channels shift every year, and from there the barge-water runs down to the bay head.
  - **The Quern** is a tributary: mill-dense, over-fished and silting.
- **Travel to Port Nevarellon:** ~2–3 days by horse on the Concord Road's spur, which runs west along the river as a towpath; 4–5 days for a loaded barge riding the current, which rides the last ebb into the bay head. The closest ducal seat to the city, and the only one an army could reach and take inside a fortnight (see [[Framework - The Three Layers]]).

## 🏰 House Hordeus
The ruling house of Aerthos is **Hordeus**, an old Landed name that the Docks hear as *hoard* and call the Hoarders. The seat is **[[Millhaven]]**.

**Every child is a treaty.** Aerthos has no mountains, no walls worth the name and a levy it cannot spare at harvest. What it has is grain, and children. Duke Felician has married his children outward, one at a time, to every power that could hurt Aerthos. Each marriage is a guarantee that someone else's army has a reason to stand between Millhaven and harm.

**The return clause.** Every Hordeus marriage contract carries one clause the Duke has paid dearly for, every time. A child who is widowed or ill-used may come home to Millhaven with their dowry, and no one may stop them. It has never been invoked. Every Hordeus child knows it is there.

### Felician Hordeus, Duke of Aerthos
63. Soft-spoken, courteous and patient. His father signed the Accord, and he has never once said no to the Council to its face. He retains a Golden Company Envoy at his table, and to the city this is the most frightened man at Layer 1 behaving accordingly (see [[The Golden Company]]). He writes to every one of his children, every month, in his own hand.
- **His one hard act.** In 43 A.A. the Low Moons fell in the Opening and the trade season failed. Felician opened **Grainlock** to the city at cost and fed it through the summer. The Council remembers it as a kindness, and the Docks remember it as bread. Whether he did it out of decency or to remind the city what Aerthos is worth, nobody can say — including Felician.

### The Duchesses
- **Prisca** *(dead)* bore the first seven children and died bearing the seventh, in 33 A.A.
- **Renata**, of a Sheafcross factor family, married Felician in 35 A.A. The grain factors' money sits behind the ducal chair, and every factor in the duchy knows it.

## 🌾 Land, Tenancy & Grain
- **Bound to the furrow.** Aerthos tenants hold their strips from the Duke and owe labour-days on the ducal demesne. They may not leave the land without the steward's leave. Everyone knows the steward's share: **a third** of what a tenant grows.
- **The factors.** Millhaven's grain factors advance seed against the coming crop and buy the harvest forward. Most tenants are in debt to a factor by the first year of their tenancy and stay there; **Barleywick** has not been out of debt in living memory. A tenant who cannot pay goes to the debtors' hulks off the Foundry Slips (see [[The Foundry Slips]]).
- **The stewards** run the ducal estates. Some of them collect private levies on top of the Duke's, outside any lawful assessment. [[Jeerdan Darcy]] has seen it and looked away. The steward on the river spur, **Rafe Hobbius**, Latinised his name when he bought his deed, and the spur has never let him forget *Hobb*.
- **[[Jeerdan Darcy]]** was born on the spur. He ploughs for short-handed villages at harvest, and in Aerthos that is very nearly sedition.

## 🎉 Customs
- **Sheaf-Day** closes the harvest in Sheafturn. It is the one day a tenant eats what he grew, and the Duke eats at the tenants' tables at Millhaven, by old custom.
- **The Quern Blessing.** The millstones are blessed and the miller's cut for the year is announced. The cut has gone up in nine years of the last ten.

## ⚖️ Leverage & Power Dynamics
- **The city starves in a month without the barges.** It is the one monopoly the city cannot replace, and the reason Aerthos has never been eaten. It is a shield, not a weapon. Aerthos has never used it to threaten anyone, and could not survive the reply if it did.
- **The marriages** are the duchy's real army. Every power that could take Aerthos has a Hordeus at its table.
- **De Vonce:** the Wide Fallow, where Aerthos ploughs and De Vonce patrols, and both have moved the stones since 8 A.A. Livia's offer is waiting. Aerthos also feeds De Vonce's border levy, and Tythius knows exactly what that means.
- **The Golden Company:** an Envoy at the table, and a daughter married to a captain.
- **[[Jeerdan Darcy]]:** the tenantry's Warden, and the stewards' nuisance.
- **Vulnerabilities:**
  - Flat ground and a small levy.
  - A heir who would sell the reserve.
  - A Low Moons in the Opening, which strands the barges and the seed in the same season.
  - Wether Bridge — the only way across the lower Aer for anyone coming from the south.

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 1**, a Ducal power.
- **Chokepoint:** grain — the city's bread, the Company's rations, De Vonce's border levy and Ubaraz's road.
- **Enforcement:** scarcity, never used; and marriage.
- **Geographic anchor:** Millhaven and the Barley Flats.
- **Bypass:** none for bread. Imported grain by sea would take a season to arrange and cost the city its treasury.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is the tenants, bound and indebted, and the children, married off like sacks.
  - **The mote** is the return clause, which every Hordeus child knows is there. It is also 43 A.A., when Grainlock fed the city — and Sheaf-Day, when the Duke sits at the tenants' tables.
