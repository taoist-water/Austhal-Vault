# Duchy: De Vonce (The Iron Court)
#location/region #faction/state #status/draft

> "Iron for the Duke, oak for the city, charcoal for the Guild, and the smoke for us." — Ord Bracken, charcoal-burner of Ashcopse

> "My father is eighty-six and looks forty. I am fifty-one and look it. Do the sum." — Eldrick De Vonce, in his cups, at the Levy Muster

---

## 🗺️ Lay of the Land
- **Position:** directly north of [[Port Nevarellon]], in the foothills of [[The Jagged Spine]]. The Ducal Concord Road and the Spine Aqueduct climb through it side by side. To the north, beyond the Adderwater, the mouth of the [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast#5. 💀 The Scarred Land: Duchy of Corvus (The Fallen Crown)|Corvus Scar]] opens onto De Vonce ground. Aerthos lies to the west and Valerius's cliff coast to the east (see [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast#🧭 Lay of the Coast|Lay of the Coast]]).
- **Terrain:** rolling hill-country climbing to **the Sisters**, the foothill ridge where the iron is mined. **The Iron Wood** covers the slopes below them: the oak stands that keep the city's coopers and shipwrights in work. In the lower valleys lie the **war-fields** around Longbarrow, where the plough turns up stone bone every spring.
- **Rivers:**
  - **The Slowmarrow** runs south out of the war-fields and joins the Aer above Port Nevarellon. It carries the oak rafts down to the bay head, and the bone-silt with them.
  - **The Adderwater** is the Corvus border in law. It has not been the border in practice since 8 A.A.
- **Weather:** the Winter Moons come straight down off the Spine. The upper Sisters are snowbound through Deepcold, the mines go to half-shifts, and the border towers are cut off for weeks at a time.
- **Travel to Port Nevarellon:** ~3–4 days by horse from Castle Iron-Spire on the Concord Road; 7–9 for an ore or timber train. Oak goes faster by water, down the Slowmarrow on the spring flood.

## 🏰 House De Vonce
The oldest of the four standing houses, or so it says, and it counts its own years: a De Vonce steward who says "the year of the house" in front of a Council factor is making a point (see [[Framework - The Coastal Reckoning]]). The seat is **[[Castle Iron-Spire]]**.

**The blood.** There is elf in the house, and the vassals count ears. [[Tythius De Vonce|Tythius]] is half-elven and wears his hair over his. His children are a quarter elven and show it unevenly. Two of them carry the liquid given names the house uses for the elf-blooded (see [[Reference - Name Tables]]), and the oldest knights still say that a Duke who will not die is a Duke who will not make room.

### Tythius De Vonce, the Iron Duke
86, and looks forty. He signed the Ducal Accord, saw the last King executed, and has outlived every merchant who hired the Golden Company. See [[Tythius De Vonce]].

### Cassia De Vonce, of House Valerius *(dead)*
Tythius married her in the war, in −4 A.A., and her house's coin paid his levies through the last years of the rebellion. She died of a winter fever in 41 A.A., and he has not remarried. The Duke who calls the Council shopkeepers paid for his war with a syndicate's dowry, and the Valerius side of the family has never let him forget it. Her brother Gratian wore the Valerius coronet for thirty years, and lent the Iron Court the money it still owes (see [[Valerius Family]]).

### Ellenst Rulieone, née De Vonce
54. The eldest. In 28 A.A. her father married her into a steppe-lord house of Tuwal Ghorun, one that sits at the Gathering (see [[The Twelgorn Kingdom]]). The coast writes the house's name *Rulieone*; its own spelling is nearer *Al Ruliyun*.
- **The reason given was horses.** Every knight blooded at Iron-Spire since has ridden a steppe destrier bought through her husband's fief.
- **The reason the vassals give** is that the Duke sold a daughter to slavers.
- She writes home twice a year, sealed, by [[Palla Vantry]]'s southern ships.
- **On the coast her marriage does not exist.** It was written in a polity no register lists, so on paper she is still Ellenst De Vonce, unmarried, of the Iron Court. Her children have no coastal standing at all.

> **GM-facing.** Tythius made the match on purpose, as a window into the court that keeps the exiled royal line. It has worked. Ellenst has seen the Unaging Heir at the Gathering, and her letters for the last two years have described the omen: the Low Moons falling in the Opening, around 59 A.A. Tythius can count the moons as well as any Guild table. **He has told no one** — not his heir, and not [[High Captain Marco|Marco]]. It is why the Stonereach match cannot wait.

### Eldrick De Vonce, the heir
51. **Marshal of the Levy**: he commands the Iron Court and the border levy under his father.
- **As a soldier:** brave, well loved by the knights, poor at sieges and worse at supply.
- **Two quarrels with his father.** He would clear the Scar-Holders and move the border stones openly. And he says, in drink, that the city drinks at De Vonce's pleasure.
- **The weapon tests** run under his captains (see below).
- **The wait.** He is half Valerius by blood and hates it. He holds his mother's Valerius shares, votes them by proxy, and draws the dividend (see [[Valerius Family]]). His father looks younger than he does, and the whole court can do the sum.

### Imaihil De Vonce
45. The second son, and the one the vassals mean when they count ears. He has the stillness, the slow ageing and the sharp ears; he looks thirty.
- **The library.** He keeps the house's books at Iron-Spire and corresponds with the [[The Guild of Alchemists|Guild]]'s Chapter-House about its moon-tables.
- **The Scar.** Twice he has gone to the edge of the Scar with the castellan's leave, to bring back soil and water in stoppered jars.
- The knights call him *the Elf*, and not kindly.

### Sheandri De Vonce
34. The younger daughter, and she looks younger still.
- **Service.** She served six years with the border levy under [[Castellan Senna Cade]], the only De Vonce who has stood a winter at Adder's Gate.
- **The match.** Her father wants her married to Cato Scutarius, the Stonereach heir (see [[Duchy of Stonereach]]). She refuses, and not for love. The match would put her six to eight days into the passes, and leave nobody at Iron-Spire who will tell Eldrick no.

### Serrian De Vonce, of the cadet line
Tythius's nephew, kept far from the succession and close to the forges: **master of Coldhelve**, where the High-Steel is made. Nobody in the house has ever asked what Serrian wants. Nobody thinks they need to.

## ⚔️ The Iron Court & the Levy
- **The Iron Court** is the house's sworn knights, from which the duchy takes its name. They hold manors from the Duke and owe him service in the field, each with his own lance of armed men.
- **The Blooding** makes a knight. The candidate keeps the Held Pass vigil the night before. At dawn he is cut across the palm with the **[[The Shard-Blade|Shard-Blade]]**, and he swears with the bleeding hand.
  - **It is deliberately archaic and deliberately expensive.** The candidate must bring his own steppe destrier, a High-Steel harness, and the feast.
  - **Half the Iron Court is mortgaged** to pay for it, most of them to Valerius bond-houses.
- **The Levy Muster** opens the High Turns. Every vassal brings his levy to the Muster Field below Iron-Spire to be counted and inspected, and the year's border rotations are drawn.
- **The border levy** is the largest standing levy on the coast. Companies rotate out to the Corvus-facing towers for a season at a time. It is the single heaviest line in the Duke's accounts, and the Council's subsidy has never covered it.
- **Adder's Gate** stands at the end of the Concord Road's military spur, above the ford at Bittern's Ford. It is the last garrison before the ash, and its castellan is **[[Castellan Senna Cade]]**. Knights were offered the post and refused it. **Wardstone Watch**, the second tower, answers to her as well, and everyone knows it is undermanned.
- **The weapon tests.** The Duke permits his garrison captains to test certain "controlled" weapons — alchemical, and Undertow-touched — on ground in the upper Sisters. The tests run under Eldrick's captains. Senna Cade will not have them on her ground.

> **GM-facing.** Where the weapons come from is not settled: the Guild, the Unsealed, or someone closer to home (see Open Questions). They work. They are also exactly the kind of essence-touched thing the Low Moons destabilise, and the tests have been stockpiling them a day's ride from the Scar.

## ⚒️ Economy & Logistics
**Rich in ore, poor in coin.** The duchy feeds four of the city's guilds and pays a border levy no other duchy carries. Its silver goes out as fast as its iron does.

| What | Where | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| Iron ore | Mines in the Sisters; smelted at **Hollowforge** | [[Framework - The Guilds of Port Nevarellon\|The Smiths and the Shipwrights]]; refined iron through the Basin |
| **High-Steel** | Refined only at **Coldhelve**, under Serrian | The Golden Company, nobles, sanctioned guild-masters — the monopoly is decreed jointly by the Council and the Duke because the steel is his (see [[Law - The Council's Edicts]]) |
| Oak | **The Iron Wood**; rafted down the Slowmarrow | The Coopers (staves) and the Shipwrights (timber) |
| Charcoal | **Ashcopse** and the oak stands | Hollowforge's furnaces; [[The Guild of Alchemists]] |
| Bone-meal | **Longbarrow** ploughs it up; **Marrowmill** grinds it | Fertiliser for the duchy's own fields |
| Water | The aqueduct's headwater at **Spine-Head**, kept by the crews at **Cistern Camp** | [[Port Nevarellon]], for which the Council pays De Vonce every year |

- **The mines** are worked hard and badly. Shafts flood in the Winter Moons, and the mine-captains are paid by the ton, not by the man. The Duke knows and looks away, because the ton pays for the border.
- **Once a year the Weepers walk the mine-towns** and heal for nothing (see [[The Cult of the Weeping Martyr]]). The mine-captains welcome them, because a healed miner goes back down.

> **GM-facing — the war-bone.** The bone Longbarrow ploughs up is the petrified remains of the War of Creation (see [[Cosmology - The Celestial Graveyard and The war of Creation]]). Ground to meal it is almost entirely inert, and the fields it feeds are the richest in the duchy. *Almost*: now and then a fragment is still wrack. Marrowmill's miller sells those, quietly, to the Guild's wrack-assayers. The locals who will not eat the bread are wrong about the bread and right about the mill.

## 🕯️ Faith
- **The Iron Horizon** is the officers' faith. Maxim Night initiates them, and every knight keeps the Held Pass vigil before his Blooding (see [[The Cult of the Iron Horizon]]).
- **The Weepers** walk the mine-towns once a year.
- **The Rime-Fang** is kept by the miners of the upper Sisters, who are snowed in through Deepcold (see [[The Cult of the Rime-Fang]]).
- **The Zenith** keeps no house in the duchy. The Duke's justice is given in his own hall, and the Register touches De Vonce only where an instrument touches the city.
- **The Ashen Veil** buries De Vonce's dead, as it buries nearly everyone's: Featherfall in every parish (see [[The Cult of the Ashen Veil]]).
- **The Tidespoken** have little hold this far inland.

## ⚖️ Leverage & Power Dynamics
- **Over the city:** the aqueduct, the High-Steel, and the iron and oak behind four guilds. Tythius has used none of it as a weapon, and Eldrick talks about all of it.
- **The city over De Vonce:** the Council's subsidy pays for the border, and the only market for the ore is Port Nevarellon's.
- **[[Jeerdan Darcy]].** The Duke demands his paper, and the Warden and the castellan at Adder's Gate get on very well.
- **[[High Captain Marco]]** wants the border reports from Adder's Gate. Tythius will not share them.
- **Stonereach:** the match the Duke wants and his daughter refuses. Duke Brennus has not answered either, and the Road's north-east leg is a running quarrel over winter provisioning.
- **Valerius:** blood kin through Cassia, and creditor to half the Iron Court.
- **Aerthos:** feeds the border levy. Duke Felician offered his daughter Livia to Eldrick two years ago, and Tythius has not answered (see [[Duchy of Aerthos]]).
- **Tuwal Ghorun:** a daughter at the Gathering.
- **The Scar.** The Adderwater is the border in law and nowhere else. De Vonce has been grazing and cutting north of it since 8 A.A. Eldrick wants it done openly, and the Scar-Holders at the ford stand in the way.
- **Vulnerabilities:**
  - The coin. The levy costs more than the ore earns.
  - The succession. A Duke who will not age, an heir who resents it, and a second son the knights will not follow.
  - The stockpile in the upper Sisters.

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 1**, a Ducal power.
- **Chokepoint:** iron, High-Steel, oak, and the water.
- **Enforcement:** violence; the largest levy on the coast.
- **Geographic anchor:** Castle Iron-Spire and the Sisters.
- **Bypass:** Ubaraz iron through Stonereach; Iron-Burl in place of oak; and for the water, nothing.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is the mines, the border, and the stockpile.
  - **The mote** is the widows' bread at Iron-Spire (see [[Castle Iron-Spire]]), and Senna Cade's ford.
