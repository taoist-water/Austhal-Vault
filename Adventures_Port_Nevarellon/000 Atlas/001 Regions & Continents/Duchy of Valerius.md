# Duchy: Valerius (The Gilded Coast)
#location/region #faction/state #status/draft

> "In Valerius you pay for the salt in your bread. Then they lend you the money to pay for it." — Brinewick pan-worker's saying

> "The Road closes, Iron-Spire starves of coin, Millhaven sits on its sacks, and Saltmere sends a galley. That is the whole of our foreign policy." — Duke Junia Valerius

---

## 🗺️ Lay of the Land
- **Position:** the cliff coast north of [[Port Nevarellon]], running up the Inner Sea shore toward the Spine. De Vonce's foothills lie inland to the west. The mouth of the [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast#5. 💀 The Scarred Land: Duchy of Corvus (The Fallen Crown)|Corvus Scar]] touches its inland edge (see [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast#🧭 Lay of the Coast|Lay of the Coast]]).
- **Terrain:**
  - **The cliffs.** Sun-bleached limestone — the same stone as the High Quarter's — broken by shallow fishing bays.
  - **The pans.** Behind the cliffs lie lagoons and flats. **Coldspar Mere**, brackish, feeds the salt-pans.
  - **The Cropped Downs.** Wind-stunted and thin-soiled. The vineyards are terraced here for drainage and walled against the wind. With light coming from everywhere, nobody plants for sun (see [[Framework - The Coastal Reckoning]]).
  - **The headlands:** Gull Cape, with its cliff colonies, and Palefast Head.
- **Rivers:** **The Colding** runs down off the Spine and freezes at its mouth in the deep Winter Moons.
- **Weather:** the Winter Moons bring the Spine's sleet onto the northern waters, and the pans close. **The High Turns are the salt season**, and the turn of Saltdraw is named for it.
- **Travel to Port Nevarellon:** ~1 day by galley along the Coastal Meridian in fair weather, 2 in poor. Overland is 4–5 days by the Concord Road and the cliff roads, which punish wagons. **Valerius is the one duchy that does not need the Road** (see [[Framework - The Three Layers]]).

## 🏰 House Valerius
The house is a syndicate with a coronet, and it means it. The family holds the duchy as **shares** and chooses its Duke at **the Table**. See **[[Valerius Family]]** for the house: the Table, the bond-houses, and the people.

- **Seat:** [[Saltmere]].
- **The Duke:** **Junia Valerius**, chosen by the Table in 47 A.A.

## 🧂 Salt, Wine, Fish & Bonds

| What | Where | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| Salt — white for the table, grey for curing | The pans at **Saltmere** and **Brinewick**, fed by Coldspar Mere | The city: the [[Framework - The Guilds of Port Nevarellon\|Victuallers]] ([[Verrine Sallow]]), the Foundry Slips smokehouses, and every kitchen on the coast |
| Cured fish | **Brinewick** | The city — one of its two chief exports |
| Wine | The Cropped Downs | The High Quarter, and anyone else who can pay for being seen to drink it |
| Eggs and feathers | **Gullstand**, under Gull Cape | Local |
| Shipping | Valerius galleys on the Coastal Meridian, resupplied at **Netherquay** | Everyone |
| **Credit** | The Valerius bond-houses at Saltmere | Half the Iron Court, House Hordeus, and a good part of the city (see [[Valerius Family]]) |

- **The pans are worked by bond-labour.** A pan-worker comes in on a **Debt-Bond** (see [[Law - The Council's Edicts]]). Board, lodging and tools are charged against the wage — and so is the salt in the worker's bread. Most bonds never close.
- **Free salt** is boiled on the beaches by runaway bondsmen and sold to the smugglers. It is the trade the house hates most, because it costs nothing to make and undercuts everything.
- **Salt-Draw.** The pans are opened in the turn of Saltdraw, and the duchy's yield for the year is made public in Saltmere's market square. The Table sits the same week.

## 🏜️ The Dry Pans
North of Brinewick lie the old pans. Two generations ago a storm cut the channel that fed them from Coldspar Mere, and the house never paid to re-cut it. What is left is a white crust a mile across, cracked into plates, with the sluice-gates standing open on nothing.
- **Runaways go there.** A bondsman who runs can scrape free salt off the crust and boil it on the beach for the smugglers. The Dry Pans are a day from the nearest water.
- **The Thirsty Woman.** The pan-workers say a woman waits in the Dry Pans with a cup, beside a well that is not there. She is beautiful, and she is always thirsty. Some runaways come back. Some come back changed.
- **What the bond-rows whisper:** *Give her your name, and the Bonds book forgets you.*
- **What the Tongues say:** the Brinewick Tongues call her an abomination, as they call every hag (see [[The Tidespoken Clergy]]). They have not gone in after her. Nobody has ever paid them to.
- **What the house says:** nothing. The Dry Pans are not on any lodged map of the duchy's holdings.

> **GM-facing — the Mirage-Hag (adventure hook).** The Thirsty Woman is a **Mirage-Hag**, rooted in the Dry Pans: a place where still water used to be (see [[Lore - Hags]]). Everything in the Hags note applies — she must be asked, she always agrees, and the price is paid in full.
> **The trade she does here** is in runaways. Most bring her nothing she can work, because a shard costs more than a bondsman will ever hold. So most pay for the cup alone, and the cup is real. The bond-rows' whisper is also true, after its fashion:
> - **A runaway who pays her a name is forgotten.** The Bonds book still carries the name, and the Register still holds the copy, but nobody who reads it can bring the face to mind. A Bond-Master cannot hunt someone he cannot picture.
> - **The price is total.** The runaway's family forgets them too.
> - **They are a little fey after**, and cold iron bites them.
>
> **Hook seeds:**
> - **A recovery job.** House Valerius hires someone to recover a runaway the ledger says exists and nobody can describe.
> - **A pyre.** The Brinewick Tongues want her burned before the Low Moons, while she is still rooted and can be found.
> - **A freed worker.** A worker bought free by the Crock went into the Dry Pans anyway. Why?
> - **The Low Moons (~59 A.A.).** She can uproot, or a sister can arrive. Abandoned pans are exactly the ground a Mirage-Hag needs, and a coven forming a day's walk from the coast's largest bond-pans is a problem a city has to answer.

## 🕯️ Faith
- **The Tidespoken** are strong along the bays. The Tongues keep kitchens at Brinewick and Netherquay, every Valerius boat pays the Mother's share, and Widow's Reach sends more names to Thalass's Count than any village of its size (see [[The Tidespoken Clergy]]). The faithful are called *the Salt*, and in Valerius the joke makes itself.
- **The Zenith.** The Table loves the Register. Every bond, every share and every Duke's appointment is lodged and counter-copied in it. Valerius is the Plumb Court's most reliable client on the coast.
- **The Crooked Coin** rides with the free-salt smugglers (see [[The Cult of the Crooked Coin]]).
- **The Iron Horizon, Rime-Fang and Ashen Veil** have little hold here. *(To be set in the regional-cult pass.)*

## ⚖️ Leverage & Power Dynamics
- **The exit.** If the Road closes, Valerius keeps trading.
- **The debt.** Valerius is owed by De Vonce's knights, by House Hordeus, and by half the city. Its weakness is the same fact: **it is owed more than the coast can pay.** If Aerthos or the Iron Court defaults, someone has to foreclose on a duke's son or a blooded knight, and nobody can.
- **Kin.** Through Cassia, Valerius is blood to the De Vonce heir. Through Faustina, it married into the Hordeus heir (see [[Valerius Family]]).
- **The Council.** Its closest ties on the coast: Valerius salt passes through Sallow's Victuallers, and the Table and the Council speak the same language.
- **The Golden Company:** a standing Envoy at Saltmere (see [[The Golden Company]]).
- **Vulnerabilities:**
  - Free salt.
  - A runaway bondsman with a good Arbiter.
  - Wreckers at Coldspar.
  - Pirates on the Meridian.
  - A Table that can remove its own Duke.

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 1**, a Ducal power.
- **Chokepoint:** salt, coastal shipping, and credit.
- **Enforcement:** debt, lodged in the Register.
- **Geographic anchor:** Saltmere, the pans and the Meridian.
- **Bypass:** free salt on the beaches; foreign salt by sea; default.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is the bond-workers in the pans.
  - **The mote** is the Crock: one bond a year, bought out by the pan-gangs themselves (see [[Bond-Master Tallis Gorrius]]).
