# Duchy: Corvus (The Fallen Crown) — the Scar
#location/region #status/draft

> "Seven went down, and the mountain came after them. We say their names on the Vigil. Nobody else bothers." — a Cinder Row elder

> "There's no Duke here, no levy, no roll. There's the ash, the dead that walk in it, and us. Two of the three are honest." — a Scar-Holder at Ashenford

---

## 🗺️ Lay of the Land
- **Position:** the long valley cut into the southern flank of [[The Jagged Spine]], between all four standing duchies. Its mouth opens south-east onto De Vonce at the Adderwater. Its head runs deep into the range, to the shut gates of [[Ubaraz Kingdom|Ubaraz]] (see [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast#🧭 Lay of the Coast|Lay of the Coast]]).
- **What it was:** a rich valley of black soil, dense pine and ancient stone spires. Its road carried Ubaraz's trade down to the coast, and its deep works were the richest on the Whispering Coast.
- **What it is:** **the Scar.**
  - **The Ash Plain** is the dead heart of the valley.
  - **Broken Ward** is the stump of the fallen peak. Corvus Spire is buried beneath it (see [[Corvus Spire]]).
  - **Lake Ashen** — nothing lives in it, and nothing has for fifty years.
  - **Blackcopse** is standing dead timber that will not burn and cannot be felled.
  - **The Blackrun** runs with acidic sulphur.
  - Under it all lies the **Ash-Blight**: Undertow-touched soot from the collapse, thick on the plain and thin in the wind-shadows.
- **The clean pockets:** wind-shadowed valleys and seeps below the blight-line, where the ground will take a crop if you know which ground (see [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast]]).
- **Travel:** there is no lawful road. The Concord Road's military spur ends at Adder's Gate, facing the valley mouth.

## ⚔️ What the Coast Remembers — the Demon Wars (6–8 A.A.)
Every family in the Sunken Ward tells it the same way:
- **Something came up out of the deep works under Corvus Spire.** It was six years after the Accord, at the Low Moons. The pious call it demons, and the soldiers who fought it never found a better word.
- **For two years Corvus fought them in its own valley and lost it** village by village.
- **The others held the edges.** De Vonce held the Adderwater with the levy it still keeps there, and Ubaraz shut its gates at the valley's head. The refugees came down to Port Nevarellon and never went home.
- **The end.** In 8 A.A., at the next Low Moons, **seven went down into the Spire**, and the mountain came down after them. The Ash-Blight fell across the valley the same night, and choked everyone it caught.

**What the coast believes:** the weight of what came through brought the peak down — that is how the ducal surveyors wrote it. **What the refugees believe:** the Seven brought it down, on purpose, on themselves. The refugees keep the Seven's names and speak them at the Ash Vigil. Nobody uphill has ever asked for them.

**The Seven, as the Vigil names them:**
1. **Hadrian Corvus**, the last Duke.
2. **Dorrun Halt-Adze**, the Ubaraz engineer who cut the deep shafts.
3. **Corran Ossius**, a Corvus knight — a minor house's third son, sworn to the Duke.
4. **Maryn Aske**, a midwife of Corvus Low who took the Ashen Veil.
5. **Tamsin Vole**, a miner of the deep works. *Vole* was an insult, and she kept it.
6. **Hallam Drey**, a De Vonce levy sergeant from the Adderwater line, who kept the Iron Horizon.
7. **Marren Loweb**, an elf three centuries old, who could read the writing in the deep works.

> **GM-facing — the temple and the door.**
> **The temple.** House Corvus's wealth came from wrack mined deep under Corvus Spire, in shafts cut by Ubaraz engineers. In the deepest drift the miners broke into **a temple older than the coast's settlement** — built by a people nobody on Austhal remembers, over the seam where the Undertow runs closest under the rock (the same seam that runs north-east to [[The Inner Sea|the Broken Spires]], and toward which Stonereach's deep drift is now heading — see [[Brekka Stonegraft]]). At its heart stood a **door**: a portal into the Undertow, long dead and long forgotten.
> **The Undercourt.** What the temple taught made a cult. The house's own deep-works master, **Quintus Corvus** — the Duke's younger brother — gathered it from Corvus miners and Ubaraz engineers. They called themselves the **Undercourt**: a court for what waits below. Their aim was to pull the Undertow up into the Slack Water and remake the material world in its image. The Corvus valley was to be the **beach-head**.
> **The war.** At the Low Moons of ~6 A.A., when everything essence-touched loosens, the Undercourt opened the door. What came through made the valley its foothold and fought outward from it for two years, with the cult as its guides.
> **The Seven.** At the next Low Moons, ~8 A.A., seven went down through the Spire's deep works to the temple: **Duke Hadrian Corvus** himself, who knew his own house had opened the door; **Dorrun Halt-Adze**, who had cut the shafts; **Marren Loweb**, who could read the temple's script well enough to know the door could not be shut from this side; and **Corran Ossius, Maryn Aske, Tamsin Vole and Hallam Drey**, to get them there. Dorrun did what a dwarven work is built to allow (see [[Granite Spire]]): he brought the mountain down on the temple. None of the Seven came back.
> **Buried, not closed.** The door is under Broken Ward, beneath millions of tons of rock, and **at every Low Moons it strains**. The Dust-Wretches walk further and the Scar's deep valleys stir. [[High Captain Marco]] has noticed the pattern in his map-room and cannot explain it. The next Opening-season Low Moons falls around 59 A.A.
> **The mirror.** The Fourth's breach opened "where the grandest house stood" (see [[Reference - The Wondrous Markets]]). The Fourth has no temple like this one. **TheTao's direction (2026-10-08):** its breach may be an attempt to *rebuild* one, or something else entirely — a narrative still to be supplied.

## 🏰 House Corvus
Functionally extinct, the coast says.
- **Duke Hadrian Corvus** — the last Duke, lost in the fall. The refugees say he went down with the Seven. The coast says he died in his seat.
- **Quintus Corvus** — the Duke's younger brother, master of the deep works. Remembered as lost early in the war, in the first fighting below the Spire.
- **The raven child.** The Sunken Ward has always whispered that an infant of the house was carried out through the ash before the end. Nobody uphill believes it, and nobody in Cinder Row will say who.

> **GM-facing — the claimant.**
> **The whisper is true.** Before the Seven went down, the Duchess sent the Duke's infant out with a nurse. The child is **fifty now** and lives in Cinder Row under a refugee name, as an ordinary refugee, and does not know.
> **Who does know:** one old member of the Cinder Row elder-councils, who keeps the **raven seal** the nurse carried (see [[The Sunken Ward]]).
> **The proof:** the child's birth was lodged in the Register before the fall.
> **Why it can never be settled:** the claim can be voided and never confirmed (see [[The Cult of the Zenith]]). A proven heir of Corvus would not end the annexation problem. It would make it a succession crisis with no court able to settle it.
> **The poison** is that the claimant is the child of the man who closed the door, and the nephew or niece of the man who opened it.
> **A hook to use or ignore:** Keeper [[Keeper Merrit Dray|Dray]]'s eleven-week delay concerned a Cinder Row street of Corvus refugees. He may have seen the name.

## 🪶 The Ashen Veil in the Scar
- **The Scar holds more Wretches than anywhere on the coast.** The Ash kept everyone it caught (see [[Lore - Wretches]]).
- **The Veil's grave-keepers go in.** Their rite is **the Long Carry**: find a Dust-Wretch, lay it down, and carry the body out past the blight-line to fire or dry earth, where the Scar cannot reach it.
- **What the Veil say it does:** the Carry loosens the place's grip and guides the spirit up to the High Reach.
- **Why they do it:** one of the Seven was theirs. The Veil say every Carry finishes the work **Maryn Aske** went down to begin.
- **Nobody pays them.** [[Castellan Senna Cade]] lets them through the ford. The Scar-Holders feed them, and leave the Wretches they find for them.

> **GM-facing.** The Carry works, and the Veil are wrong about why: nothing goes up to the High Reach. What answers them, and what the Carry really ends, is in [[The Cult of the Ashen Veil]]. The miracles are real.

## 🏚️ The Scar-Holders
The only people who live in the Scar, on the only land on the coast that nobody can lawfully take from them.
- **The Quiet Steading** — one family, four years in, farming a clean pocket: the most successful Scar-Holding on record. They draw water at Bittern's Ford and bring the first of their beans to [[Castellan Senna Cade]]. They keep the Rime-Fang's Bare-Teeth Night (see [[The Cult of the Rime-Fang]]).
- **Ashenford** — abandoned in the collapse and re-occupied nine years ago. The ford still works, which is the whole reason.
- **Cinderhollow** — a blight-scavenger camp stripping metal from the buried Spire's outworks. Crews rotate, and nobody overwinters twice.
- **Blackrun** — a ruined mill village on the acid stream. The wheel still turns, and nobody turns it.
- **The sulphur** goes out across the Adderwater to [[The Guild of Alchemists]]. It is the Scar's only trade in coin.
- **The Dust-Wretches** are the dead the Ash kept (see [[Lore - Wretches]]). The Scar-Holders know them the way marsh-folk know the tide.

## 🪧 The Border-Holders
Four duchies have been eating the Scar's edges for fifty years. None of it can ever be made lawful.
- **De Vonce** grazes and cuts north of the Adderwater, with the levy at Adder's Gate watching both ways (see [[Duchy of De Vonce]]).
- **Aerthos** ploughs the Wide Fallow under the valley's south-western rim, and moves the stones at night (see [[Duchy of Aerthos]]).
- **Valerius** sends bond-gangs to cut dead timber at the valley's mouth for the salt-pans' fires. It is the only use anyone has found for Blackcopse that does not need it to burn (see [[Duchy of Valerius]]).
- **Stonereach** keeps a toll-keep on the upper rim, where the Long Graft looks down into the valley, and tolls whatever comes up out of it (see [[Duchy of Stonereach]]).

## 🕯️ The Refugees
Fifty years on, the Corvus refugees and their children are the underclass of [[The Sunken Ward]].
- **The Ash Vigil** — the anniversary of the fall, kept in the Sunken Ward and never in the Scar. The Seven are named.
- **Homing Night** — the second generation names the villages they have never seen.
- **The elder-councils** of Cinder Row are fading as the last survivors die.
- **The dead charters.** The Corvus trades still sit on the city's Charter Roll, held by nobody (see [[Framework - The Guilds of Port Nevarellon]]).

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**No Layer 1 power.** The fifth chair is empty. The Scar-Holders hold ground at **Layer 2**, and nobody can lawfully hold it at all.
- **Chokepoint:** sulphur — and the buried door, which nobody but the GM knows is there.
- **Enforcement:** the Ash; the Dust-Wretches; the absence of any law.
- **Geographic anchor:** Broken Ward.
- **Bypass:** the Adderwater fords; the night.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is a dead valley, a buried door, and fifty years of refugees.
  - **The mote** is the Seven, who went down on purpose; the Quiet Steading's beans; and smoke rising from chimneys that are not supposed to exist.
