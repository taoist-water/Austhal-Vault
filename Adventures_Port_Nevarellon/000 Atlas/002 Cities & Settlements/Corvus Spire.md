# Settlement: Corvus Spire
#location/settlement #status/draft

> "You can see the stump from Adder's Gate on a clear day. Broken Ward, we call it. There was a house in there once, and a duke, and whatever else." — a De Vonce levyman

---

## 🏙️ Profile
- **Duchy:** [[Duchy of Corvus]] — the Scar.
- **Tier:** T— — buried. Nothing is available, and nothing is for sale (see [[Reference - Name Tables]]).
- **Position:** under **Broken Ward**, the stump of the fallen peak, in the heart of the Corvus valley above the Ash Plain.
- **What it was:** the seat of House Corvus, cut into the spire that gave it its name (see [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast]] on the "-Spire" convention). Below the hall ran the deep works, the richest shafts on the coast, cut by Ubaraz engineers.
- **What it is:** millions of tons of shattered rock, buried since 8 A.A. It has never been excavated or resettled. Scavengers from Cinderhollow strip metal from its outworks, where the outworks still show.

---

## 📍 What Is Known
- **The outworks** — gatehouses and curtain-wall at the stump's foot, half-buried and full of what the ash left.
- **The deep works** — the Spire's shafts, under the rubble. Everyone on the coast knows that whatever came up in 6 A.A. came up out of them.

> **GM-facing.** Under the deep works lies **the temple**, and in it **the door**: the portal into the Undertow that the Undercourt opened in ~6 A.A. The Seven brought the mountain down on it in ~8 A.A. It is buried, not closed, and it strains at every Low Moons (see [[Duchy of Corvus]]). Anyone who digs here is digging toward it.

## 🧱 Layer & Mote
**No Layer.** A ruin.
- **The mote:** none, deliberate. Whatever hope there is in the Scar is out on its edges, not here.
