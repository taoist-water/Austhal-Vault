# Settlement: Millhaven
#location/settlement #status/draft

> "Twenty wheels on one weir, and every one of them turning for somebody else." — Millhaven saying

---

## 🏙️ Profile
- **Duchy:** [[Duchy of Aerthos]]. The seat of House Hordeus.
- **Tier:** T2 — one of the coast's four ducal seats (see [[Reference - Name Tables]]).
- **Position:** on the River Aer, where it breaks into **the Nine Sisters**, the braided reach below. It is ~2–3 days from [[Port Nevarellon]] by horse on the Concord Road's spur, and 4–5 days by barge downstream. This is the closest ducal seat to the city.
- **What it is:** a fortified grain town built around a great weir. There are mills on both banks, barge-quays below the weir, and a curtain wall that was built to keep out raiders, not armies.
- **Who lives here:** millers, bargemen, the grain factors and their clerks, the Duke's household, and the tenants of the home farms.

---

## 📍 Key Locations
- **The Weir House** — the ducal residence, built over the mill-race. The water runs under the hall and the floors hum.
- **The weir and the wheels** — twenty mill-wheels on one weir, the largest milling works on the coast.
- **The barge-quays** — where the flat-bottomed grain barges load for the run down to the city.
- **Factors' Row** — the grain factors' counting-houses, every one of them newer and better kept than the Weir House.
- **The Envoy's lodging** — a Golden Company Envoy, retained at the Duke's table (see [[The Golden Company]]). Comfortable, in sight of the quays, and two streets from the Duke.

---

## 👥 Who Holds It
- **Duke Felician Hordeus**, and his household (see [[Duchy of Aerthos#🏰 House Hordeus|House Hordeus]]).
- **The factors**, in practice, of everything that is not the Duke's.

---

## 🧱 Layer & Mote
**Layer 1 ground** — the seat of a Ducal power, and the loading-point of the city's bread.
- **The mote — the Weir Bell.** When the Nine Sisters shift and a barge channel silts shut, the Weir Bell is rung. Every household in Millhaven turns out to re-cut the channel. Nobody is paid and nobody is excused — the Duke's sons included, and Decimus has blisters to prove it.
