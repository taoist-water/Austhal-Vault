# Settlement: Castle Iron-Spire
#location/settlement #status/draft

> "It rusts when it rains. The whole hill. You'd think a family would take the hint." — Valerius saying

---

## 🏙️ Profile
- **Duchy:** [[Duchy of De Vonce]]. The seat of House De Vonce.
- **Tier:** T2 — one of the coast's four ducal seats (see [[Reference - Name Tables]]).
- **Position:** in the De Vonce foothills, ~3–4 days north of [[Port Nevarellon]] by horse. The **Ducal Concord Road forks here**: the main line runs north-east toward Stonereach's Granite Spire, and a short military spur runs north to **Adder's Gate** and the Scar (see [[Castellan Senna Cade]]). The aqueduct's headwater at Spine-Head is a day's ride west.
- **The spire:** a tower of red ironstone standing out of the foothills. The keep is cut into it, not built against it (see [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast]] on the "-Spire" convention). After rain the rock weeps rust down its own face, and the castle takes its name from that.
- **The burgh:** the town at the foot of the spire, with the Road's garrison post, a market, the forges, and the knights' houses.

---

## 📍 Key Locations
- **The Hall of the House.** Cut deep into the rock, cold all year, and hung with the house's banners by year of the house. The Duke gives justice here himself.
- **The Muster Field.** The flat ground below the spire, where the Levy Muster is held each year at the opening of the High Turns. The knights take their Blooding at its edge, at dawn.
- **The library.** [[Duchy of De Vonce#Imaihil De Vonce|Imaihil]]'s. The house's books, its maps, and its correspondence with the Guild's Chapter-House. The knights do not go in.
- **The armoury.** Where [[The Shard-Blade]] hangs when the Duke is not wearing it, which is rarely.
- **The Road post.** One of the Concord Road's four garrison posts. [[Jeerdan Darcy]] keeps a warden's cot here, under the Duke's windows, and the Duke has never once asked him to dinner.

## 👥 Who Holds It
- **[[Tythius De Vonce]]**, and his household.
- **[[Duchy of De Vonce#Eldrick De Vonce, the heir|Eldrick]]**, when he is not with the levy.
- **The Iron Court**, in rotation between their manors and the border.

---

## 🧱 Layer & Mote
**Layer 1 ground.** The seat of a Ducal power, and the fork of the coast's only shared road.
- **The mote — the widows' bread.** Every levyman killed on the border leaves a family, and the Spire feeds it from the Duke's own granary for life. In the lean years the garrison has gone unpaid. The widows' bread never has. Tythius has never explained it, and has never let it lapse.
