# Settlement: Granite Spire

> "The Duke lives on top. The dwarves live underneath. Ask yourself which floor the building would rather lose." — Kettlehold saying

---

## 🏙️ Profile
- **Duchy:** [[Duchy of Stonereach]]. The seat of House Scutarius.
- **Tier:** T2 — one of the coast's four ducal seats (see [[Reference - Name Tables]]).
- **Position:** at the head of Thraw's Pass, in the north-eastern Spine, where the **Ducal Concord Road ends**. It is ~6–8 days from [[Port Nevarellon]] by horse, and longer or impassable in deep winter. The Long Graft leaves from its western gate along the crest toward Ubaraz.
- **The spire:** a granite horn standing out of the pass. The citadel is cut into it — the hall, the keep and the house above, the lower works below (see [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast]] on the "-Spire" convention). It is the only ducal seat on the coast built by dwarves.
- **Who lives here:** the Duke's household, the pass garrison, toll-clerks, carters, and the **Keystone crews** of Ubaraz in the lower works.

---

## 📍 Key Locations
- **The Toll Hall** — the Duke's hall. The toll-books are kept on open shelves along its walls, so that anyone who comes to the Duke can see what he is paid for.
- **The lower works** — Ubaraz's quarter, under the citadel. The stone is cut cleaner here than anywhere above. The Duke's writ runs at the stair-head, and nobody tests whether it runs further.
- **The western gate** — where the Long Graft begins, with Ubaraz's carts waiting in a line for the toll.
- **The storm-lamp** — on the highest tower, lit whenever a storm comes down the passes. Under storm-right every keep in Stonereach answers it with its own.

---

## 👥 Who Holds It
- **Duke Brennus Scutarius**, above (see [[Duchy of Stonereach]]).
- **The Keystone crews**, below. They answer to Ubaraz.

---

## 🧱 Layer & Mote
**Layer 1 ground** — the seat of a Ducal power, and the gate on Ubaraz's only road.
- **The mote — storm-right.** When the storm-lamp burns, the western gate opens to anyone on the Long Graft, toll or no toll, dwarf or human. The Keystone crews come up the stair to help carry the half-frozen in, and for one night nobody counts anything.
