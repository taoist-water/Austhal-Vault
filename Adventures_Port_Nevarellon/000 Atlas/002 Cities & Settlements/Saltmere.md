# Settlement: Saltmere
#location/settlement #status/draft

> "White on the cliffs, white in the pans, white on the ledgers. The only colour in Saltmere is the ink." — Netherquay boatman

---

## 🏙️ Profile
- **Duchy:** [[Duchy of Valerius]]. The seat of [[Valerius Family|House Valerius]].
- **Tier:** T2 — one of the coast's four ducal seats (see [[Reference - Name Tables]]).
- **Position:** on the cliff coast north of [[Port Nevarellon]], ~1 day by galley up the Coastal Meridian. The walled town stands on the cliff-top, the harbour sits in a cleft below it, and the salt-pans lie inland behind it, fed from Coldspar Mere.
- **What it is:** a walled port town built in white limestone, cleaner and quieter than any other seat on the coast, and richer than all three of the others together.
- **Who lives here:** shareholders and their households, factors and clerks, galley crews, and the pan-workers, who live in bond-rows below the walls on the pan side.

---

## 📍 Key Locations
- **The Table Hall** — where the Table sits each Salt-Draw. There is no throne in it. The Duke sits at the head of the table, and the chair is the same as all the others.
- **The strongroom** — beneath the Table Hall. Every bond the house holds, and a counter-copy of every lodging it has ever made in the Register.
- **Salt-Draw Square** — the market square where the year's yield is read out.
- **The harbour** — the Fleet book's galleys, and the bell.
- **The bond-rows** — the pan-workers' housing, below the walls on the pan side, where their board is charged against their wages.
- **The Envoy's lodging** — a Golden Company Envoy, retained by the house (see [[The Golden Company]]).

## 👥 Who Holds It
- **Duke Junia Valerius**, and the Table (see [[Valerius Family]]).

## 🧱 Layer & Mote
**Layer 1 ground** — the seat of a Ducal power, and the coast's second counting-house after the Plumb Court.
- **The mote — the fog bell.** When the fog comes in off the Inner Sea, the Tongues ring the harbour bell all night for any boat still out there. That includes the free-salt smugglers, and the Table has asked them more than once to stop. The Tongues say the sea does not check a manifest (see [[The Tidespoken Clergy]]).
