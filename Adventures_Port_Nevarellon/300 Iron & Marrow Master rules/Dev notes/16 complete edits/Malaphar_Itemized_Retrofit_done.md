# Itemized Modifiers: Retrofit and Going-Forward Guidance

## Why This Matters (Design Rationale)

Smite the Unclean (and any future "strip a trait" Miracle, spell, or Momentum spend) needs a real number to subtract. A fused modifier like "Defense +7 (Precognition & Hell-Forged Plate)" reads beautifully but gives a tag-stripping effect nothing concrete to grab. Itemizing fused Boss modifiers into their named components fixes that — and it does double duty: it tells the GM exactly what a piece of looted Boss equipment is actually worth if the party takes it home, rather than requiring the GM to invent a number on the spot after the fight is already over.

## Malaphar, The Ashen Sovereign — Retrofitted Defense

> **Defense +7:** Precognition +4, Hell-Forged Plate +3. *(Explicitly breaking the +3 mortal cap on the Plate component alone — Precognition's +4 is itself already at the edge of what a mortal Boss should carry, representing supernatural foresight rather than trained skill.)*

**Why this split, specifically:** Precognition is the larger share deliberately — it's the *un-strippable* half of Malaphar's Defense, representing an innate, narrative-fictional power rather than a physical object a sword can cut off him. Hell-Forged Plate gets the smaller, removable +3, which is still a meaningful chunk (roughly the size of a Masterwork armor bonus) without being the majority of his total Defense. This preserves the core design intent you flagged: Smite the Unclean should feel like a genuine, satisfying tactical win — "the party identified and stripped a real, named weakness" — without single-handedly trivializing the fight by removing the *entire* Defense score in one cast. A Priest who successfully strips the Plate brings Malaphar from Defense +7 down to **Defense +4**, which is still a real Boss-tier number, just no longer an unbeatable one.

**Loot Implication (the upside you flagged):** If Malaphar is ever defeated or the party manages to physically claim the plate mid-fight (narratively unusual, but mechanically now well-defined), the Hell-Forged Plate is now a concrete lootable item: **+3 Armor, likely with a drawback tag given its infernal origin** (a GM could reasonably attach Cursed, Heavy, or a similar narrative-cost tag — that's a separate design pass, but the foundation number now exists to build on).

## Strike +8 (Ashen Greatsword) — Flagged, Not Yet Itemized

Worth noting for completeness: Strike has the identical fused-modifier shape (how much is Malaphar's own training versus the Greatsword's Power rating?), but nothing in the current ruleset can strip a weapon mid-combat the way Smite the Unclean strips armor — there's no "disarm" or "sunder" effect that would need this number yet. I'm flagging it as a deferred item rather than itemizing it now, since splitting it today would be speculative work with no mechanic to consume it. If a future Miracle, spell, or Momentum spend targets weapons specifically (a "Sunder" maneuver, for instance), this is the natural next stat block to retrofit.

## Going-Forward Guidance for Future Bosses

When writing a new Boss (or retrofitting an existing Elite/Boss), any modifier with a parenthetical citing **more than one named source** should itemize the split at the point of writing, not after a player tries to interact with one piece of it mid-fight. A simple rule of thumb: the innate/narrative-fictional component (precognition, demonic blood, ancient magic) should generally carry the larger share and be the un-strippable part; the equipment/physical component (armor, a specific weapon, a worn relic) carries the smaller, strippable share and doubles as the eventual loot table entry.
