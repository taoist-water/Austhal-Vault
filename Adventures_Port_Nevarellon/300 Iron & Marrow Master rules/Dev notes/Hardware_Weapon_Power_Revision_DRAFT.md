# DRAFT REVISION — Weapon Power Rescaling
*Replaces the "Weapons," "Weapon Tags," and "Advanced Specialized Weaponry" sections of Hardware (Equipment).md. Armor, Inventory, Quality, Gear Condition, and Expedition Gear sections are unaffected and unchanged.*

**Design intent:** Fodder, Grunt, and Elite-tier enemies should go down to Wounds noticeably more often when a competent martial character connects — a Clean Success should reliably wound, not just the top half of one. Boss-tier Wound Thresholds are untouched by this pass; the goal is *not* to let mundane weapons threaten a Boss with a Major Wound, only to fix the lower and mid tiers. Spell Power is rescaled on the identical tier structure in a companion document so weapons and Arcana stay anchored to each other.

## Weapon Tags — one addition

All existing tag definitions are unchanged. One tag is being **formalized** here because it was already load-bearing elsewhere in the ruleset but never actually defined:

**Heavy** *(New)*
- Alongside Siege, required to inflict Wounds on Gargantuan Scale (+3) creatures — per the existing rule in *Metal meet Flesh*'s Scale section, which references this tag but never defined it until now.
- **Not available on the standard market table.** No off-the-shelf weapon should carry this tag — a mundane Greatsword should never be capable of wounding something that levels buildings, regardless of Power value. Heavy is earned through a Commission-gated artisan build, a named relic, or a feat/spell that explicitly grants it for a scene (e.g., a future Domain of Law or Winter miracle empowering a weapon temporarily).
- Distinct from the existing **Heavy Hitter** tag (which suppresses Fates' Bounty in exchange for treating natural 6s as 7s) — different tag, unrelated function, name collision is unfortunate but both terms are now doing separate jobs on the books.

## Reserved: Black Powder Ordnance *(placeholder — not designed yet)*

Flagging space for this rather than designing it now. Tentative bucketing, to be revisited:
- **Pistols/hand cannons:** likely slot into the existing Ranged progression alongside Crossbows (Power 2–3 range), possibly trading Reload for a Concealable/Sidearm angle.
- **Wall guns / cannons / siege ordnance:** likely carry **Siege** by default, and are where the still-unbuilt "Siege Engines" subsection belongs — these were always meant to be literal artillery, not a mundane weapon upgrade path.
- No numbers are proposed here; this section exists purely to mark where the category will attach once designed.

## Flagged, not fixed, in this pass
- **Armor-Piercing vs. Precise:** the Light Crossbow, Heavy Arbalest, and Estoc use the tag name "Armor-Piercing," but the Weapon Tags glossary only formally defines "Precise" (ignores 1 point of armor), which appears to be the same effect under a different name. Worth reconciling into one tag in a later pass.
- **"Cruel" (Barbed Spear):** referenced in that weapon's tag line but not defined anywhere in the Weapon Tags glossary. Likely meant to point at an existing effect (possibly Vicious, from the Bestiary traits) rather than a new undefined tag.
