## Cantrips redesign (raised 2026-09-10)

**TheTao's proposal:** Rework Cantrips into very minor magic that is effectively free — no roll, no cast action required. A step below the current Novice tier's "roll Arcana vs a TN" structure entirely.

**Current state, for context when this gets picked back up:**
- Exactly **one** Cantrip exists in the whole corpus: **Elemental Manipulation** (Common Arcane Magic list). No Paradigm has any Cantrip-tier spells of its own — Cantrip only exists on the Common list; every Paradigm's own tier structure starts at Novice.
- Elemental Manipulation's current implementation directly contradicts the "free, no cast" proposal: it's an **Activation**, resolved as **Unopposed Arcana vs. TN 6**, with a Margin Scaler (0–4 grants Advantage on one relevant check; 5+ sustains for the scene). It is a rolled, action-costed spell today, not a freebie.

**Open questions to resolve when this is picked up:**
1. Does "no cast required" mean no Action Type at all (a passive/flavor effect, not even a Free Action), or just no roll (auto-succeed, but still costs an Activation)?
2. If Cantrips become truly free, does that risk stepping on Elemental Manipulation's current niche (a scene-long Advantage-granting utility effect) — does that effect get demoted to flavor-only, or does it need a replacement at Novice to keep that utility niche filled?
3. Should Paradigms get their own flavor Cantrips (a Pyromancer can light a candle without rolling; a Necromancer can wilt a flower), or should Cantrip-tier stay Common-only, shared across all Arcanists regardless of Paradigm — consistent with how the Common list already works for Novice+ ("no Paradigm Mastery, standard DP cost")?
4. If Cantrips stop costing DP or an Action, do they still belong on the Arcane Awakening / Advancement menus at all, or do they move to background flavor a character simply "can do" once they have Arcana 1+, with no DP cost and no listing needed?

Not resolved. Do not implement without explicit confirmation of direction first — this note exists so the context isn't lost, not as a green light.

## Permanent injuries/mutations/diseases system (raised 2026-09-14)

Needed to properly resolve Apex Chimera's (Biomancer, Tier 3) Fumble clause: "the transformation destabilizes, resulting in a permanent, gruesome physiological penalty (GM's discretion)." Currently just GM fiat with no mechanical shape. Worth a real design pass rather than a single-feat fix — a proper system here would also give teeth to any other "permanent consequence" clause elsewhere in the corpus.

## Per-turn Momentum-spend cap (raised 2026-09-14)

Raised while reviewing The Red Mist (Berserker, Tier 2): should there be a hard limit on how many separate Momentum spends a character can make in one turn/activation? Nothing caps it today — a character with several Momentum-spend triggers live in the same round (Twin Strike, The Chain, a Riposte-style reaction, Red Mist's retaliatory Strike, etc.) could chain several in one turn as long as the Momentum is banked. Not necessarily broken, but worth a deliberate yes/no. Touches every Momentum-spend feat across multiple documents, so scope as its own pass rather than folding into a single-document edit.
