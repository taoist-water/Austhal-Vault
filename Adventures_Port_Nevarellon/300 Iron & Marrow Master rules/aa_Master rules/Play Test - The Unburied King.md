# Iron & Marrow — Playtest Session: "The Unburied King"

*A one-night attrition test for a Green party — party size itself is one of the two open questions this session is built to answer. Nothing here is committed to the core documents — this is a testing pass. If a piece plays well, fold it into the Bestiary / GM Tools afterward.*

---

## Session Overview

**Premise:** A killing frost has swallowed a mountain village for three nights running — livestock frozen solid in locked barns, a watchman found kneeling in the snow with frost inside his lungs. The local shrine-keeper points the party at an old barrow sealed into the mountainside: home, tradition says, to a warlord-king who never accepted his own death. If his bones aren't scattered or his tomb re-sealed by the next sunrise, the village won't see a fourth morning. There's no time to make camp between fights tonight — whatever rest the party gets, they take standing in the dark, thirty minutes at a time.

**Party:** Two different rosters exist for this module and this doc doesn't pick between them — whoever runs it should choose one and read the results below with that choice in mind.

- **As originally drafted (Duo/Trio):** Helga Stonewright (Faith, Law Domain — the only shield/Block build) and Faelan Rook (Arcana, Shadow Sorcery — the weakest Dodge of the roster). This pairing puts a genuine Brace/shield defender and a genuine Dodge defender in the same party, which the Frost-Cave Troll fight is specifically built to test. For a third player, add **Pillit Thistlewood** (Halfling, ranged skirmisher) for a distinct action-economy role without touching the Block-vs-Dodge test.
- **As actually dry-run tested (Trio/Quartet):** the simulated sessions below used **Bram, Helga, and Faelan** (Trio) and **+ Brynja** (Quartet) instead — not Pillit. All are valid Green pregens; the sim data further down only speaks to this cast.

All pregens should be pulled directly from the current pregen roster rather than re-copied here, so this doc can't drift out of sync with them.

**Structure:** Cold Open (Grunt+Fodder skirmish, with an environmental Hazard Check on approach) → **Breather 1** → Elite (Frost-Cave Troll, in a Heavily Obscured/Difficult-Terrain cave) → **Breather 2** → Boss (Halgrim the Unburied, in a Heavily Obscured chamber).

A Playtest Log checklist is at the end — fill it in as things happen, not from memory afterward.

**The central thesis this session is actually testing:** the Breather empties every participating creature's Momentum Bank to 0. *Decisive Blow* (3 Momentum, force exactly 1 Wound past a Boss's Wound Threshold) is likely the only reliable way a party dents Boss-tier Wound Threshold without heavy prep. Force two Breathers before Halgrim, and the party walks into his lair with empty Banks, needing to rebuild to 3 apiece before they can reliably cross his WT 9 at all. That tension — rest costs you your only real answer to the boss's durability — is the whole point of the night, not a side effect.

> **Dry-run note:** three simulated runs of this exact fight chain went **Trio 0-for-3** (a full party wipe across three separate honest runs, each from a different failure cause) and **Quartet 2-for-2** (one clean win, one costly win with two deaths). Read as: this encounter, as built, wants a minimum of four PCs — the Trio losses weren't a fluke of bad dice, they were a structural mismatch. These runs pre-date the Momentum-unification conversion below (Halgrim's kit is now stronger — his control abilities fire for free instead of being rationed, and his Bank refills every round), so treat the 4-PC-minimum finding as directional and re-run it before trusting it under the current rules.

**The second thesis:** the corpus has no stated party-size axis for enemy budgeting — only party Standing. Halgrim is deliberately built *under* the flat Green Boss floor as a live test of whether a small party needs that discount to have a fair fight, or whether it undershoots and the fight goes limp. Log this explicitly; it's an open rules question, not a settled one.

**Lethality note:** this chain of fights is genuinely dangerous — that's the ask. But don't manufacture a TPK to prove the point. If Wounds are stacking faster than expected, the party knowing they can disengage (they scouted a side passage on the way in) is a legitimate valve, and a fled/interrupted fight is still useful data.

## Scene 1 (Cold Open): The Threshold Wards

The approach to the barrow crosses open, wind-scoured ground before the fight even starts.

**Environmental beat — the crossing:** Before the party reaches the seal, call for a **Hazard Check** (Survival or Athletics, 2d6 vs. TN 8). On a failure, the killing frost inflicts **1d6 Locked Stress** before the fight begins — Locked, not Dissonant, so it doesn't clear on a Breather. Worth tracking whether this compounds uncomfortably fast against a Green Stress Limit.

The barrow's outer seal has a construct guardian bound to it, flanked by two risen thralls.

**Terrain:** the threshold itself is glazed black ice for 10ft in every direction — **Difficult Terrain** (halves Movement, Disadvantage on Athletics/Acrobatics within it). Anyone flanking or disengaging fights the ice as much as the enemy.

**Starting Momentum: Standard Engagement.** The Grave-Warden (highest tier present) starts with **2** Momentum in its own Bank; the Grave-Wretches start empty. The wards are alert to intrusion but not expecting a fight this large.

**Trio/Quartet adjustment:** add a third Grave-Wretch rather than boosting anyone's Starting Momentum — keeps the fodder-per-PC ratio roughly even without inflating the enemy side's opening resources.

### The Grave-Warden (Grunt)

*A rusted, robed construct bound to guard the barrow's threshold.*

- **Tier:** Grunt
- **Type:** Construct
- **Size:** Standard | **Move:** 30ft
- **Attributes:** Reflex +0 *(derived only — never added to a roll)*
- **Skills:** Melee +4
- **Derived Stats:** Wound Threshold **6** | Wound Slots: 2 | Stress Limit: 4 | Activation Order: 6 (6 + Reflex 0) | **Momentum Bank: 4** (4 + Reflex 0)
- **Equipment:** Ancient bone blade (Power 2)
- **Traits (1): Fear Inducing** — Resolve check vs. TN 8 or gain Fear. Cleared by taking Regroup out of sight of the source, or automatically if the source is destroyed.
- **Special Actions (1): Grinding Assault (free, no Momentum cost)** — *Trigger:* wins a Melee Clash with Margin 3+. *Effect:* 1 Dissonant Stress in addition to the normal Impact.

**Phases**
- **Unbroken:** Advances in a straight line toward whoever is closest to the crypt door. No tactics, no hesitation.
- **Broken:** At max Stress, it seizes up mid-swing and is removed from the encounter rather than destroyed.

### Grave-Wretch (Fodder) — ×2 (Duo/Trio) / ×3 (Trio/Quartet adjustment)
- **Now statted in _Beasts Monsters Mutants_ as _The Wretch_, Grave-Wretch skin.** Run it from there. The inline block that used to sit here is superseded, and it carried **three fossils** worth recording: `Prowess +1` where its own stated Strike of `2d6+1` derives from **Melee**; `Stress Limit: 0`, the pre-Gate-Test fossil, against the Fodder floor of 4; and no Momentum Bank or Activation Order at all — while the Grave-Warden block directly above it states both correctly.
- Quick reference: WT 5 _(4 + Brawn 1)_, 1 Wound Slot, **Stress Limit 4**, Activation Order 6, Momentum Bank 4, claws (Power 1) striking at `2d6+1` (**Melee +1**), **Vicious**.
- **Grave-Wretch skin:** frost-cracked claws, ignores difficult terrain from ice and snow — including this scene's refreezing meltwater, which it wades through while the party does not — and takes **Advantage on its Clash against a target suffering Rigor**. Immune to the blizzard's own Hazard Check (_Iron World_).

### What to watch for here

If the party clears this without needing a Breather, that's real data too — don't retroactively harden it. If they're still comfortably under 50% Stress afterward, nudge the Frost-Cave Troll's Starting Momentum up a band instead of going back to inflate this fight.

**Trigger for Breather 1:** whenever the fiction calls for it — GM's read on the table, not a hard rule.

## Breather 1 (and the Attrition Tax — decide before running)

Standard Breather rules apply in full: 30 uninterrupted minutes, Momentum Banks empty to 0, all Dissonant Stress clears, Locked Stress and Wounds are untouched, then roll the Community Supply Die.

**Open call — the Attrition Tax:** the original design wanted a cost for resting (the GM gaining +2 Threat the moment the party commits to the Breather — Halgrim's wards feeling the breach, the mountain leaning in closer). That was written against a shared GM Threat pool that no longer exists under the current Momentum rules. Pick one before running this scene:
- **(a) Retire it outright** — resting is free, full stop.
- **(b) Reshape it** — seed the next scene's highest-tier enemy with +2 Starting Momentum on top of its normal band, instead of feeding a pool.

Whichever you pick, log whether it actually registered as a felt cost at the table or evaporated unnoticed.

## Scene 2 (Mid, Elite): The Frost-Cave Troll

**Environmental beat — the cave itself:**
- **Heavily Obscured:** thick ice-fog, lit only by whatever the party carries (Attacker Disadvantage, target +2 to Defense). Cuts both ways — it also hampers the Troll's own Strikes.
- **Difficult Terrain:** refreezing meltwater and old ice (halves Movement, Disadvantage on Athletics/Acrobatics). Gives a ranged/kiting PC a mechanical reason they can't just kite the whole fight.

**Starting Momentum: High Alert.** The Troll's Bank is 5, so it starts with **2**. It doesn't hunt cleverly — it wakes up already furious that something is in its den.

**Open call — Trio/Quartet adjustment:** the original "+1 Starting Threat" lever doesn't translate directly (there's no pool to add to, and High Alert is already a fixed formula off the Troll's own Bank). If a third or fourth PC needs a bump, the candidates are seeding the Troll with a full Bank (4) instead of half, or leaving it alone and letting the extra body absorb the difference on its own. Genuinely open — this is the experiment.

### The Frost-Cave Troll (Elite)
- **Tier:** Elite | **Type:** Beast | **Size:** Large (Scale +1)
- **Skills:** Melee +6, Prowess +4
- **Derived Stats:** Wound Threshold **9** (4 + Brawn 3 + Scale +2) | Wound Slots: 3 | Stress Limit **5** (4 + 1 Elite) | Activation Order: 7 (6 + Reflex 1) | **Momentum Bank: 5** (4 + Reflex 1)
- **Equipment:** none — natural weapon only, Tree Trunk (Power 3, Reach, Brutal)
- **Traits (1): Troll-Blood Regeneration** — at the start of its activation, heals 1 Wound Slot and clears 1 Stress. *Weakness:* suppressed until the end of the next round if it takes Impact from a fire source.
- **Special Actions (2), both free — no Momentum cost:**
  - **Vicious Frenzy:** *Trigger:* wins a Strike's Clash with Margin 3+. *Effect:* immediate secondary Strike at an adjacent target (claws, Power 1, Vicious).
  - **Sweeping Uproot:** *Trigger:* declared instead of a single-target Strike. *Effect:* the Tree Trunk Strike gains Cleave, forcing everyone in its frontal arc to defend against the same roll; losers are knocked Prone.

**Phases**
- **Unbroken:** Leans on Regeneration to shrug off attrition, alternating Vicious Frenzy with Sweeping Uproot.
- **Broken:** Frenzy — loses Block/Dodge entirely but gains Advantage on all Strikes until it dies.

### The actual test — read this before running it

Make sure the party's shield/Block build takes at least one hit they Block, and the party's Dodge build takes at least one hit they Dodge, specifically from the Tree Trunk. Difficult Terrain already makes pure kiting costly, but position a ranged PC inside the frontal arc at least once regardless, rather than letting the fiction quietly let them avoid the fight. If running a third or fourth PC, get them into that arc at least once too — a fourth data point the two-PC test can't produce on its own.

**Trigger for Breather 2:** after the Troll is dealt with — the party has to patch up before descending to the vault proper.

## Scene 3 (Finale, Boss): Halgrim the Unburied

**Environmental beat — the chamber:** lit only by failing, centuries-old grave-lanterns. **Heavily Obscured** applies throughout, for both sides.

**Starting Momentum: Kill-Zone.** Halgrim starts the fight with **4** Momentum already banked — a full Bank.

> "He does not raise his voice. He has had three hundred years to learn that silence frightens the living more than any war-cry ever did."

## Vital Statistics
- **Tier:** Dread Entity/Boss
- **Type:** Undead (a dwarven warlord-king, centuries dead and unwilling to lie down)
- **Size:** Standard
- **Attributes:** Will +2, Wits +0, **Reflex +0** *(slow and mentally uncomplicated — illusions, Arcana debuffs, and anything requiring him to out-think rather than out-endure a target land on him easily; he's never racing anyone to act, so it costs him nothing tactically)*
- **Skills:** Melee +5, Resolve +4, Prowess +3
- **Derived Stats:**
  - Wound Threshold: **9** (4 base + Prowess 3 + Armor 2)
  - Wound Slots: **4**
  - Stress Limit: **8** (4 base + Will 2 + Wits 0 + 2 Boss-tier bonus)
  - Activation Order: **6** (6 + Reflex 0)
  - Momentum Bank: **4** (4 + Reflex 0)
- **Equipment:** Ancient grave-plate (+2 Armor, Medium, Bulky) | The Frost-Bitten Maul (Power 4, Brutal)
- **Budget:** 12 Skill points (Melee 5 + Resolve 4 + Prowess 3) — top of the Green Elite band (9–12), one point short of the flat Green Boss floor (13). Deliberate, logged experiment: the flat Boss band assumes an action economy ("acted on 3–5 times for every one of its own actions") that a small party doesn't supply. The bet is that his Special Action kit and durability make up the difference — that bet is exactly what this session should confirm or deny.
- **Traits (3):**
  - **Fear Inducing:** Resolve check vs. TN 8.
  - **Sovereign's Malice:** Halgrim banks **1 Momentum to his own Bank** every time a PC spends Momentum from theirs. The mechanism that taxes the party for rebuilding toward a Decisive Blow — capped at 4 now instead of open-ended.
  - **Grave-Locked Resilience:** once per round, Halgrim may spend up to **2 Momentum from his own Bank (3 once Broken)** to reduce incoming Impact by 2 per point spent.
- **Special Actions:**
  - **Grip of the Barrow (free):** *Trigger:* wins a Melee Clash with Margin 3+. *Effect:* target is Anchored (0 movement) until they spend a full Aggressor action tearing free.
  - **Winter's Judgment (Cost 2 Momentum, from his own Bank):** *Trigger:* declared on Halgrim's activation. *Effect:* Direct Dissonant Stress (2) to one target in his Threat Zone, bypassing Wound Threshold entirely.
  - **Reaver's Bite (free):** *Trigger:* wins a Melee Clash with **Margin 5+**. *Effect:* Direct Wound (bypasses Wound Threshold, 1 Wound Slot automatically).

## Phases
- **Unbroken:** Deliberate and patient — no wasted motion. Opens toward whoever is more mobile. **Grip of the Barrow fires on any Margin 3+ win; Reaver's Bite needs Margin 5+** — control on a good hit, execution only on a great one, so the kit no longer runs dry but the automatic Wound is no longer routine (against a typical Green defence: 44.4% of his attacks Anchor, 23.9% also Wound). Only Winter's Judgment and Grave-Locked Resilience are Bank-limited, so those are the two calls that actually require GM judgment mid-fight.
- **Broken:** N/A as a separate state — the Stress-break *is* the phase change for a Boss, not a Rout/Surrender/Frenzy in between.
- **Boss Phase change:** The moment his Stress Track maxes out, the frost binding him cracks audibly. He **loses Fear Inducing** and **loses his Prowess bonus on Brace** (post-break, Brace rolls a flat 2d6), but **gains Advantage on all Melee Strikes**, and **Grave-Locked Resilience's per-round cap rises to 3** for the rest of the fight. He is not weaker after breaking — he is spending everything he has left rather than accept a second death.
