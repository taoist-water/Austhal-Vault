# Green

## Tass Blackrun — Human Female, Day-Hand (Generalist)

*"Ask for whoever's best. If they don't turn up, ask for me."*

### Vital Statistics
- **Species:** Human
- **Standing:** Green (Milestone 0 — 0 DP earned; pure creation build)
- **Size:** Standard | **Move:** 30 ft / 6 squares
- **Attributes:** Brawn 1 | Reflex 1 | Wits 1 | Will 1 *(Flat array)*
- **Skills:** Melee 1 | Athletics 1 | Prowess 1 | Acrobatics 1 | Notice 1 | Crafting 1 | Medicine 1 | Influence 1 | Resolve 1 *(9 ranks, 9 DP with Adaptable. Every ceiling is 4 — the one thing only Flat can do)*
- **Wound Threshold:** 6 *(4 base + 1 Brawn + 1 Leather)*
- **Stress Limit:** 7 *(4 base + 1 Wits + 1 Will + 1 Indomitable Spirit)*
- **Wound Slots:** 3 | **Momentum Bank:** 4 *(4 + Reflex 1, −1 Steady Not Sharp)* | **Activation Order:** 7 *(6 + Reflex 1)*
- **Inventory Slots:** 9 *(8 + 1 Brawn)* — 8 used, 1 free

### Species Traits (Human)
- **Adaptable:** +1 Skill **DP** at creation (already applied — a Human's creation Skill budget is 9, not 8).
- **Indomitable Spirit:** +1 Stress Limit (already applied above).
- **Steady, Not Sharp (Drawback):** −1 to your Momentum Bank cap (already applied above).

### Feats
- **Trench Fighter** *(Brawn 1 — Creation)*: ignores the Disadvantage Difficult Terrain imposes on checks, and drawing a weapon while engaged takes no penalty. She grew up in Cinder Row's ash-mud.
- **Callous Pragmatism** *(Will 1 or Wits 1 — Creation)*: when she witnesses an ally take a Wound or fall Incapacitated, she may use a Free Action to bank 1 Momentum. The same instinct that got her out of the smokehouse.

### Equipment
- **Armour:** Leather (+1 Armour, Light)
- **Weapons (two 1H items):** Hand Axe (Power 2, Brutal, Thrown, Sidearm) — a cooper's hatchet — + Knife (Dagger/Knife: Power 0, Concealable, Close-Quarters, Finesse, Thrown, Sidearm). Qualifies for the **Twin-Blade Stance**.
- **Starting Purse: 80 sp (borrowed — see Beneath the Numbers)** — Leather 12 + Hand Axe 8 + Knife 5 + Crowbar 3 + Hemp Rope 2 + Grave-Dust Poultice 8 + Artisan's Tools (cooperage) 5 + Bedroll 2 + Common Lamp 2 + Waterskin 1 = **48 sp spent, 32 sp held back.** The 32 sp is not savings: it is her first payment, due at the Long Ledger.
- **Belt:** Hand Axe, Knife, Grave-Dust Poultice. **Pack:** Crowbar, Rope, Tools, Bedroll, Lamp.

### Combat Math Quick-Ref
Strike (Hand Axe) 2d6+1, Impact = Margin+2, **Brutal** (+1 Impact per natural 4) | Parry 2d6+1 (**Off-Hand Parry** with the Knife: −1 Impact) | Dodge 2d6+1 | Brace 2d6+1 | Thrown Hand Axe 2d6+0 *(no Ranged)* | **Twin Strike:** 1 Momentum for Knife Power 0 + 1 Stress | Tactical Assessment 2d6+1 | every other trained Skill 2d6+1 | Activation Order 7

### Beneath the Numbers
- **Motivation to Survive (3):** She owes the Iron-Anchor eighty silver. If she dies, the debt walks down Cinder Row to her younger brother, Lassen.
- **Connection to the World (2):** The Tongues' kitchens fed her family through three winters. They have never mentioned it. They will.
- **Deeply Held Secret or Shame (1):** She ran from the smokehouse fire. Rilla Bask went back in instead and didn't come out. The Slips think it was the other way round.
- **How They See the World (4):** Hope gets people killed. Plan for the worst; you will rarely be disappointed.

### Backstory
Tass's grandmother walked out of Blackrun in the year the Spire came down, and got as far as Cinder Row, which is as far as most of them got. Tass was born there in 31 A.A., in a tenement that floods ash-grey every time it rains. Her mother named her for the wave, because the Tidespoken's kitchens on the lower piers fed the family when nothing else did.

She has gone to the Boot-Change eleven years running and come away without a year-token every time. Nobody has ever said no to her; they have always said yes to someone else first — the stronger hauler, the faster gutter, the rigger with better hands. So she works on day-tokens: cargo in the Basin when a regular doesn't show, the rope-walk for Suse Kellard in a busy season, gutting in the smokehouses, a bucket chain when the Slips catch fire. She knows how most work in this city is done. She has never been the best at any of it.

At the last fire, when the smokehouse roof went, Tass ran. She came out black with soot, and the street decided she had stayed to the end. She has never corrected them.

This year the Union passed her over again. The same Syndicate that wouldn't hire her for a year lent her eighty silver — most of a dockhand's year — to kit out for paid work beyond the walls, and Maeve has the entry in her ledger. Tass was twelve at the last Opening Low Moons; like most people under thirty, she has never worked through one. The next is coming.

### Table Notes
- **The floor of the creation space, and the roster's first character with no rank above 1.** Every roll she owns is 2d6+1: 58.3% against TN 8, against 41.7% untrained and 83.3% for a specialist's +3. What she buys is breadth: nine live skills where a Spike build has four or five, and +1 where they roll +0.
- **Momentum is where "no strength" really costs.** At +1 a Massive Success against TN 8 needs a natural 12 (2.8%), against 16.7% at +3 and 41.7% at +5, so every feat keyed to Margin 5+ (Scavenger's Eye, Ledger of the Deep) is close to dead on her. Her routes are the ones that don't need a good roll: **Tactical Assessment** (an Activation, 58.3% to bank 1), **Callous Pragmatism**, and the natural 12. **Gallows Humour** (Influence 1 ✓) is the swap if the table wants her support-flavoured instead, at the cost of her only feat-based earner.
- **She is not a fighter, and the sheet doesn't pretend otherwise.** The Bestiary's Grunt budget (4–6 Skill points vs a Green party) is a total, so a Grunt's fighting Skill sits around +2 to +3: her Strike wins 24–34% of those Clashes. Against Fodder at +1 she wins 44% and ties 11%. WT 6 is mid-pack for a Green character.
- **Cheap, wide growth.** Flat puts every ceiling at 4, and ranks 2–4 cost 1 DP each, so one Milestone buys three +1 bumps anywhere on the sheet. Breadth bought at creation is also the cheapest breadth in the game: nine Rank 1s cost her 9 DP, where Advancement charges 2 DP for each new Skill.
- **The Edicts of Armament bite her inside the walls.** In Port Nevarellon the Hand Axe is legal only at work or on the way to it (Contextual Carry — in a tavern it is Sedition); the Knife is legal under the Palm-Length Edict. The rules do not model either; GM call.

## Dilly Marrowick — Halfling Female, Thief (Arcane Dabbler)

*"If it isn't nailed down, it's mine. If it is, I'll have the nail by morning."*

### Vital Statistics
- **Species:** Halfling — **Size:** Small (Scale −1)
- **Standing:** Green (Milestone 0 — 0 DP earned; pure creation build)
- **Move:** 30 ft / 6 squares
- **Attributes:** Brawn 0 | Reflex 2 | Wits 1 | Will 1 *(Broad array)*
- **Skills:** Thievery 3 | Stealth 2 | Acrobatics 1 | Ranged 1 | Arcana 1 *(8 ranks, 8 DP. Ceilings: Thievery/Stealth/Acrobatics/Ranged/Ride 5 (Reflex 2); Arcana/Notice/Insight/Medicine/Crafting/Lore 4 (Wits 1); Influence/Resolve/Faith/Survival 4 (Will 1); everything under Brawn 3)*
- **Wound Threshold:** 4 *(4 base + 0 Brawn + 1 Leather − 1 Scale)*
- **Stress Limit:** 6 *(4 base + 1 Wits + 1 Will — Halflings are exempt from the Scale Stress penalty)*
- **Wound Slots:** 3 | **Momentum Bank:** 6 *(4 + Reflex 2)* | **Activation Order:** 8 *(6 + Reflex 2)*
- **Inventory Slots:** 8 *(8 + 0 Brawn)* — 8 used

### Species Traits (Halfling)
- **Underfoot:** Advantage on Stealth checks with cover, while obscured, or moving through a larger creature's space.
- **Halfling Luck:** Once per session, ignore a Fumble's Stress penalty entirely (the action still fails).
- **Small Stature (Drawback):** Cannot wield weapons carrying the Cumbersome tag. Exempt from the Scale −1 Stress Limit penalty (The Marrow).
- **Scale −1 (Small):** WT −1 (already applied). Anyone attacking her takes −1 to the Clash for the Scale step (Metal meet Flesh).

### Feats
- **Arcane Dabbler** *(Arcana 1 — Creation)*. A Grimoire holding Novice spells equal to her Arcana rank — **one**, below. No Paradigm, so never Paradigm Mastery: every Messy result costs her the Dissonant Stress. Raising Arcana is how she learns: each rank adds a Novice spell, to a ceiling of 4 spells at Wits 1. Never above Novice.
- **Scavenger's Eye** *(Wits 1, Stealth 1 — Creation)*: a Massive Success (Margin 5+) on an exploration or scouting check banks 2 Momentum instead of 1.

### Equipment
- **Armour:** Leather (+1 Armour, Light — no Arcana or Stealth penalty)
- **Weapons:** Sling (Power 0, 1H, Sidearm, max Medium / 50 ft) + Knife (Dagger/Knife: Power 0, Concealable, Close-Quarters, Finesse, Thrown, Sidearm)
- **Grimoire** (granted by Arcane Dabbler — outside the purse). Casting needs it in one hand and the other hand free.
- **Starting Purse: 80 sp** — Leather 12 + Sling 2 + Knife 5 + Thieves' Tools 20 + Hemp Rope 2 + Grappling Hook 3 + Smokestick 15 + Caltrops 4 = **63 sp spent, 17 sp remaining.** The 17 sp is already promised to Wenna's bond.
- **Belt:** Grimoire, Sling, Knife. **Pack:** Thieves' Tools, Rope, Grappling Hook, Smokestick, Caltrops. Sling stones come off the Community Supply Die.

### Grimoire (Arcana = **2d6+1**)
- **Gravity Well** *(Astromancy, Novice — no Mastery)* — Unopposed Arcana vs TN 8, Activation, Medium Range, instantaneous. One willing ally or **unattended object** (anything one person could carry) is yanked to an empty space beside her. Margin 0–2: it works, and she takes 1 Dissonant Stress from the recoil. Margin 3–4: smooth, no cost. Margin 5+: an ally pulled beside an enemy gets a free Aggressor Strike. Cannot target unwilling creatures.

### Combat Math Quick-Ref
Thievery 2d6+3 | Stealth 2d6+2 (Advantage with cover — Underfoot) | Shoot (Sling) 2d6+1, Impact = Margin, **Sidearm** (no Point-Blank Disadvantage) | Knife Strike 2d6+0, Impact = Margin, **Finesse** | Dodge 2d6+1 | Arcana (Gravity Well) 2d6+1 | Attackers −1 to hit her (Scale) | Activation Order 8

### Beneath the Numbers
- **Motivation to Survive (1):** Her sister Wenna is rowed ashore from the debtors' hulks every dawn to work the Slips and rowed back every dusk. Dilly is buying the bond down one silver at a time. The price went up again.
- **Connection to the World (3):** Lidda Shoon buys whatever Dilly brings to the Rusty Tankard and asks no questions. She knows exactly where every piece came from.
- **Deeply Held Secret or Shame (6):** She informed for the Blue-Cloaks to have a year struck off Wenna's bond. Somewhere a Watch ledger has her name beside the people who hanged.
- **How They See the World (1):** Everyone has a price. You just haven't found theirs yet.

### Backstory
Dilly was born on a hull in the Muddy Docks, to a family named for reefs she has never seen. She learned the Low-Tide Market from underneath. The mud hides small feet, and a halfling on the boardwalks learns early to be wherever nobody is looking.

Her sister Wenna caught the family's debt instead: a Syndicate loan for marsh-fever medicine that turned into a Debt-Bond, and the bond turned into the hulks. Dilly pays it down with whatever Lidda gives her for what she brings to the Tankard.

Two winters ago she got into the Basin without a work-token and out of a foreign carrack with a sea-chest's worth of other people's things. Most of it went to Lidda. She kept the book. Nobody in the Docks can read its hand, but one page held a diagram she could follow, and the first time it worked, a purse on a table slid across the room into her palm. She has learned nothing else from it yet. She has not stopped trying.

### Table Notes
- **A thief first, a caster by accident.** Her signature rolls are Thievery 2d6+3 (83.3% against TN 8) and Stealth 2d6+2 with Underfoot's Advantage (89.4% against TN 8). Gravity Well at 2d6+1 lands 58.3% of the time, and **72% of her successful casts are Messy**, so the one trick usually costs her a point of Stress. With no Paradigm there is no Mastery to upgrade it.
- **Gravity Well is the thief's spell.** It lifts an *unattended* object from up to 60 ft — the key on the guard's table, the ledger on the desk — with no Thievery roll and no need to get close. It cannot touch anything held or worn, so it is a theft tool, never a disarm.
- **Dabbler grows through rank, cheaply.** Arcana 2 costs her 1 DP and adds a second spell and +1 to every cast; Arcane Awakening pays 2 DP for each extra Novice spell, +1 off-Paradigm. Her ceiling is 4 spells at Wits 1. Darksight or Light/Darkness is the obvious second pick for a thief.
- **Her Momentum comes from her trade.** Scavenger's Eye pays 2 Momentum on a Massive scouting or exploration check. With Thievery at +3 that is a 16.7% chance on a TN 8 lock, and 19.9% on Stealth with Advantage. A Momentum Bank of 6 lets her hold it.
- **The sling harasses; it does not kill.** At Power 0, Impact is the Margin alone, so against a WT 5 Grunt she needs to win by 5 to Wound. Most hits are Glancing (1 Dissonant Stress). Against NPCs' binary Stress, each tick still moves them toward a Break.
- **The hands problem.** Casting needs the Grimoire in one hand and the other free; the sling also needs a hand. Drawing from the Belt is a Free Action, but the rules have no clear cost for putting a held item away. Run a cast-then-shoot turn at the table and see how it plays.
- **WT 4 is the floor of the new roster.** One Grunt hit at Margin 2+ with a Power 2 weapon Wounds her. Her defence is Dodge 2d6+1, the −1 Scale penalty on attackers, and not being seen.
