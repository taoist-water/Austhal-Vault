## Design Notes

**4. Focus vs. spread — the actual numbers behind the tradeoff you were checking.** Same three characters, same species/class/domain choices, built twice: once maxing the core Attribute at 3 (above, the standing rule), once capped at 2 with the freed point spent elsewhere (the version generated last message, now superseded). Side by side:

| Character | Signature roll (focus, above) | Signature roll (spread) | What the spread version bought instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helga | Tithe of Will 2d6+6 | 2d6+5 | Melee 2/Block 1 instead of 1/1, WT 9 instead of 8 — more of an actual frontline priest |
| Faelan | Arcane Clash 2d6+6 | 2d6+5 | Dodge 2d6+3 instead of 2d6+2, Dagger Strike 2d6+2 instead of 2d6+1 |
| Pip | Ranged Strike 2d6+6 | 2d6+5 | Dodge 2d6+4 instead of 2d6+3, one extra point of Prowess (WT 5 instead of 4) |

The pattern holds across all three regardless of class identity: **one point off the signature roll buys roughly one point of improvement somewhere else load-bearing** — a defensive stat, a backup weapon, a secondary skill. That's a clean, legible tradeoff for a player to actually feel at the table, which is what makes "cap at 3, let people commit" worth keeping as the default rather than a special case.
