## Trap Mechanics (formalized)

### Scene 2 (entrance room): Temple Wall-Spikes

> **Temple Wall-Spikes** (Hazard Power +3)
> - **Specifics:** Pressure-plate triggered spikes punch out from the corridor walls — a direct reuse of Iron World's Corpse-Rust Dart Trap template.
> - **Spot:** Passive Notice check (TN 8) to become Aware before triggering it.
> - **Aware Requirement:** Block or Dodge only — the spikes are too fast and numerous to Parry individually.
> - **Unaware:** Standard Unaware resolution (Iron World) — a Disadvantaged Dodge-only Clash against the Hazard Roll; Block and Parry are off the table regardless.

### Scene 2a (pass branch): The Long Drop

> **The Long Drop** (Hazard Power +4)
> - **Specifics:** A hallway-wide pit lined with spikes. Its Hazard Power (+4) already accounts for the fall and the spikes together — don't stack the general Falling table (Iron World) on top of it, that's what this rating already is.
> - **Spot:** Passive Notice check (TN 8) to become Aware before approaching it.
> - **Crossing it (Aware):** This trap specifically calls for a TN 8 Athletics check to leap the gap, instead of the usual Dodge-vs-Hazard-Roll Clash — Iron World allows a trap to dictate its own required reaction, and "make the jump" fits this pit better than an opposed roll.
>   - **Success:** Clears the pit entirely, generates 1 Momentum (per the standard "win the Clash" reward).
>   - **Failure:** Falls in. Resolved as Unaware against the pit's +4 — a disadvantaged, flailing Dodge, not the clean Athletics check they already missed.
> - **Situational Modifier — ambush mid-leap:** If the Skink ambush springs while a PC is mid-jump, apply GM Tools' **Difficult (-2)** modifier to that Athletics check.
> - **Unaware:** A PC who never spots the pit at all resolves it as a standard Unaware Hazard Roll.
> - **The Small-Scale Bypass:** A PC who passes a TN 10 Notice check spots the Skinks' own narrow ledge along the pit's edge — safe for a Small-Scale creature, no Athletics check needed. Anyone else attempting it triggers the pit immediately, no roll offered.

### Scene 3 (main chamber): Two Openings

Same roster both ways — 4 Skinks, 2 Lizardmen, 1 Lizardman Shaman, plus 1 Giant Spider (temple lair fauna, assumed 3–4 PC Green party). Only the opening state differs, since nothing on this roster spends Threat post-retune — the danger dial for this fight lives in what's already resolved when initiative starts, not in GM Tools' Starting Threat Scale.

> **Setup A — PC-Sprung Ambush** _(arrived via a clean pass through 2a)_
> - The Shaman is at the pedestal, unprepared: Ward of Scales not raised, Call of the Deep Green not cast. The Giant Spider is present as neutral lair fauna, not yet bonded. Lizardmen are at rest, not holding their Spear/Shield line. Skinks are idle, not in Stealth.
> - **Resolution:** Roll the PCs' approach as an opposed Stealth vs. the room's best Notice — same shape as the Long Drop's "passive notice V skinks stealth." Whoever they beat is **Surprised** (Iron Core: Disadvantage on rolls) for the opening exchange.
> - A clean Stealth win lets the party burst the Shaman before it raises Ward of Scales or bonds the spider — the mechanical payoff for succeeding at 2a.

> **Setup B — Alert** _(arrived via a fail branch)_
> - The Shaman opens with Call of the Deep Green already resolved — the Giant Spider is its bonded ally from the moment initiative is rolled — and with Ward of Scales already raised. Lizardmen are already holding their Spear/Shield line at the chamber's approach; Skinks are already at range.
> - No one is Surprised. Full mutual awareness — Activation Order resolves normally from round 1.

### Scene 3 (main chamber): The Sunken God's Toll

> **The Sunken God's Toll**
> - **Trigger:** Disturbing the golden statue on its pedestal without first disarming it (TN 8 Thievery or Lore to disarm).
> - **Design note:** This is deliberately *not* a snap Hazard Roll. An instant Unaware compare can zero out a low-Wound-Threshold character's agency in one bad roll with nothing of their own to throw at it — exactly the failure mode flagged as an open problem in the Ledger House playtests. Instead, this is a telegraphed countdown that runs on its own clock alongside the Skink/Lizardman/Shaman fight already in the room: the tension is racing the water while still fighting, not a single binary save-or-suffer roll.
> - **Round 1 after trigger:** Water floods in ankle-deep — Difficult Terrain across the chamber.
> - **Round 2:** Knee-deep. Anyone who ends their turn without reaching the exit or high ground must pass a TN 8 Athletics check or gain **Drowned** (Iron Core: Disadvantage on all rolls, 1 Dissonant Stress at the start of each turn until cleared).
> - **Round 3+:** Fully flooded. The Athletics check above applies every round to anyone still in the chamber, Drowned or not.
