## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Kaleb / "The Lock-Smith" (Old alias from her younger days).
- **Current Occupation:** Barkeep and Manager of [[000 Atlas/The Rusty Tankard|The Rusty Tankard]].
- **Social Class / Standing:** Un-Landed Citizen (Living on a managed commercial lease).
- **Affiliations:** [[100 Society/The Cobalt Feather Syndicate|The Cobalt Feather Syndicate]] (Liaison / Safehouse Keeper).

---

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** Human, 51 years old. Her body is heavily worn by a lifetime of hard labor and street-level survival, but she possesses a quiet, deceptive physical density.
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** She walks with a pronounced stiffness in her left knee—the result of it being shattered by an iron truncheon during a Dock-Watch raid twenty years ago. Her hands are thick, heavily calloused, and missing the tip of her right index finger.
- **Financial Status:** Officially broke; her daily ledger shows she barely makes enough coppers to pay the property lease to the High Quarter clerk. In reality, she receives a steady, untraceable income of **Silver Pieces** from the Cobalt Feather for maintaining the cellar drop and keeping her mouth shut.

---

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** Keep the peace in the Tankard. If a fight breaks out and draws the attention of the Golden Company, it risks exposing the cellar dead-drop.
- **The Core Fear:** The [[100 Society/The Iron-Anchor Syndicate|Iron-Anchor Syndicate]] discovering her true affiliation. She knows Garrick would gladly hang her from a harbor crane to send a message to the Cobalt Feather leadership.
- **The Front:** Kaleb plays the role of a tired, slightly deaf, grumbling old barkeep perfectly. She hears every rumor that passes through the Basin—which merchant galleons are carrying unregistered gold, which Golden Company captains are taking bribes, and which scribes are deep in debt—and passes it upward through the Feather's network.
