# Character: Mother Marra
#cast/active #status/draft #scale/local

> "Mother Marra has never lost a patient she meant to keep." — said at the House of Mercy, as praise

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Mother Marra
- **Occupation:** A senior Weeper of [[The Cult of the Weeping Martyr]]; teaches the laying-on to novices
- **Primary Residence:** The House of Mercy, Artisan Quarter, [[The Trade Plazas]]
- **Register:** A — devotional (*Marra*, from Mother Elara of the Mud)

## 📜 What Is Known
- She trained Elrid Fallow, a runaway tenant from Barleywick, in the laying-on.
- When an Aerthos steward's man came to the House asking after him, she told the man which ward to search. Elrid escaped over the roof.
- She is revered at the House, and still teaches there.
