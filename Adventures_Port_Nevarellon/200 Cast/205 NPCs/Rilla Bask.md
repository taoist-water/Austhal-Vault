## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Rilla Bask
- **Occupation (in life):** Fish-smoker in the smokehouses of [[The Foundry Slips]]
- **Social Class / Standing:** Un-Landed
- **Register:** A — Coastal Common (Name Tables #12)
- **Status:** Dead. Died in the most recent smokehouse fire in the Slips.

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Physical Flaws:** Chronic lung damage from the smoke-racks — the Slips' trade cough (Name Tables).

## 📜 What Is Known
When the smokehouse roof went, Rilla Bask did not come out. The Slips say a Cinder Row day-hand, Tass Blackrun, stayed on the bucket chain to the very end — she came out black with soot — and that Rilla had already gone back inside.

> **GM-facing.** Tass did not stay: she ran when the roof went, and Rilla went back in instead. This is Tass's Deeply Held Secret (her PC backstory), and only Tass knows it — unless someone else saw.
