## 📜 What Is Known
- Halfling; older sister of the thief Dilly Marrowick.
- The bond began as a Syndicate loan for marsh-fever medicine. Like most bonds on the hulks, its balance only ever goes up.
- Dilly pays it down a silver at a time, through what she sells to [[Lidda Shoon]].

> **GM-facing.** Dilly once informed for the Blue-Cloak Watch to have a year struck off this bond. People hanged on what she gave them. Nothing yet says whether Wenna knows.
