> "Folly's name came out of the bag. Good. Forty-nine years — it was his turn." — Thala Skerry, the night of the Salt-Draw

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Thala Skerry
- **Occupation:** Pan-raker in the Brinewick salt-pans, in the gang Iolo Neapwater called for
- **Social Class / Standing:** Un-Landed; under a **Debt-Bond** to the Bonds book of [[Valerius Family|House Valerius]] ([[Law - The Council's Edicts]])
- **Primary Residence:** The Brinewick bond-rows ([[Duchy of Valerius]])
- **Register:** A — Coastal Common, Tidespoken devotional given name (*Thala*, from Thalass's Omen)

## 📜 What Is Known
- Human. She rakes in the gang that sang to Iolo Neapwater's calling, and she knows every one of his verses.
- She puts her coppers in the gang's crock like everyone else, and her token went into the bag at the last Salt-Draw like everyone else's.
- She was glad when the elf's name was read out, and she said so.

> **GM-facing.** Iolo's hand closed on Thala's token in the lot-bag. He palmed it and read out his own name instead. The Crock should have freed her. Nothing yet says whether anyone saw.
