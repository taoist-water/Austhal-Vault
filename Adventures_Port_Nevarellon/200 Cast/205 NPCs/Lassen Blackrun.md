# Character: Lassen Blackrun
#cast/active #status/draft #scale/local

> "Lassen? He's the one that still says his prayers at the pot." — a Cinder Row neighbour

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Lassen Blackrun
- **Occupation:** Open — like most of Cinder Row, presumably day work in the Docks or the Slips.
- **Social Class / Standing:** Un-Landed; third generation of a Corvus refugee family
- **Primary Residence:** Cinder Row, [[The Sunken Ward]]
- **Register:** A — Coastal Common. *Lassen* is a Tidespoken devotional name (Thalass's Omen); *Blackrun* is the Corvus village his grandmother walked out of in the year the Spire fell.

## 📜 What Is Known
- Younger brother of Tass Blackrun.
- The family was named for the Tidespoken because the kitchens on the lower piers fed them through three winters.
- **The debt.** Tass borrowed 80 sp from the [[The Iron-Anchor Syndicate|Iron-Anchor Syndicate]], entered in [[Maeve the Scribe|Maeve]]'s ledger. If Tass dies, it passes to Lassen.
