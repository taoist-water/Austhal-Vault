# Character: Castellan Senna Cade
#cast/active #status/draft

> "She lets them water at the ford. She lets them sell their yellow rock across it. She writes none of it down. I asked her once by what right. She said, 'Same as you, Warden. None.'" — [[Jeerdan Darcy]]

> "Hold the gate. That's the whole of the maxim. Nobody ever tells you what the gate's for." — Senna Cade

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Senna Cade / "the Castellan" / "Cade of the Ford" (in the Scar)
- **Current Occupation:** Castellan of **Adder's Gate**, the fort at the end of the Concord Road's military spur, and of **Wardstone Watch**, the second Corvus-facing tower (see [[Duchy of De Vonce]])
- **Social Class / Standing:** Un-Landed. She holds a ducal commission under the Duke's own seal — real paper, unlike the Warden's — and no manor, no Blooding and no knight's rank. The post was offered to three knights first. All three refused it.
- **Primary Residence:** The gatehouse at Adder's Gate, above the ford at Bittern's Ford.
- **Affiliations:** The De Vonce border levy, under [[Duchy of De Vonce#Eldrick De Vonce, the heir|Eldrick De Vonce]] as Marshal of the Levy. She keeps the Iron Horizon.

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** Human, 47. Thirty years on the border, the last eleven in command. Hard, spare and tired.
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** A forearm burn-scarred to the elbow from a grass-fire on the Scar's edge, which pulls when she draws. Every Ashfall, when the wind turns and comes off the Scar, she coughs for a month.
- **Financial Status:** The garrison is paid late or not at all. She has paid her men out of her own stipend in four of the last eleven years, and she owns almost nothing.
- **Equipment & Upkeep:** A plain levy sword, a good bow, and a winter cloak patched in three colours. She has no High-Steel and has never asked for it.

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** Get Adder's Gate the twenty men it is owed before the Winter Moons close the spur.
- **The Core Fear:** An order she will not obey. Either to clear the ford, or to let the weapon tests onto her ground. She knows which one is coming first.
- **Moral Compromises:**
  - **The ford.** She lets the Scar-Holders draw water at Bittern's Ford, the last clean water before the ash. She lets them carry sulphur across the Adderwater to the Guild's buyers, untaxed and unrecorded.
  - **Imaihil.** She has let him into the Scar twice.
  - She takes nothing for any of it and writes none of it down, because a written record is the first thing that will hang her.

## 📜 Backstory & Current Role
Senna was born at Bittern's Ford, to a family that kept the Iron Horizon. Her name is a Senecus devotional, and she has always taken it more seriously than her parents meant her to. She came into the levy at sixteen and never left. She was the sergeant who actually ran Adder's Gate under two knight-castellans who rarely came north of Iron-Spire. When the second one died, three knights in turn refused the post. Tythius gave it to the woman who had been doing it, and sealed the commission himself.

She holds the last garrison before the ash with too few men and the wrong orders. Her reports go to Iron-Spire; she would send them to [[High Captain Marco]] if she were allowed, and she is not. [[Duchy of De Vonce#Sheandri De Vonce|Sheandri De Vonce]] served six winters under her and is the closest thing she has to an ally in the house. [[Jeerdan Darcy]] stops at the gate every circuit, and the two of them sit up late, saying nothing much.

**Why she is losing.** Eldrick wants a blooded knight at Adder's Gate, and a castellan who will clear the ford. Her best men are being rotated south. The captains running the weapon tests in the upper Sisters have twice asked for her ground. She cannot win any of this, and she is still holding the gate.

**What she has instead.** The Scar-Holder children know her name. There is one family that came to the ford four years ago with a handcart and has been farming a clean pocket ever since. They bring her the first of their beans every year, and she eats them.

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 2** — she holds ground between the seats, not a seat.
- **Chokepoint:** the ford, the last clean water before the Scar; and the only lawful road toward it.
- **Enforcement:** a garrison of levymen who would follow her into the ash, which is the only reason anyone at Iron-Spire hesitates to move her.
- **Geographic anchor:** Adder's Gate and Bittern's Ford.
- **Bypass:** the Scar's other edges, which belong to the other three duchies; or replacing her.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is that the border is held by someone with no rank, no money and no future.
  - **The mote** is the ford, and the beans.
