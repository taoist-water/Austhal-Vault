# Character: Reeve Colm Hollick
#cast/active #status/draft

> "The steward wants a third. I write down what I count. If I count badly, that's between me and the beans." — Colm Hollick

> "Hollick's tally is short every year, and every year it's short by exactly what the village needs to get through the Winter Moons. Either he can't count or he counts better than I do." — Rafe Hobbius, steward of the river spur

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Colm Hollick / "the Reeve" / "Short-Tally" (by the stewards, and not kindly)
- **Current Occupation:** Reeve of **Wether Bridge**, the only stone bridge on the lower Aer (see [[Duchy of Aerthos]])
- **Social Class / Standing:** Un-Landed, a bound tenant of the Duke's like everyone he speaks for. The reeve is chosen by the tenants every Sheaf-Day. They have chosen Colm twenty years running.
- **Primary Residence:** The bridge-house at the south end of Wether Bridge, on the village side.
- **Affiliations:** The tenants of Wether Bridge. His brother Bram farms near Millhaven.

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** Human, 58. Broad and slow, and stronger than he looks.
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** A knee that was crushed between a barge and a bridge pier at thirty. It locks in the cold, and he walks the bridge every morning anyway.
- **Financial Status:** Owes the factors like everyone else, and a little more than most, because he has paid other people's seed-debts out of his own strip twice.
- **Equipment & Upkeep:** The village tally-sticks and the bridge-book, a mason's hammer, and a lantern.

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** Get the scoured north pier shored before the spring flood, out of a bridge-penny the steward keeps trying to take for Toll-on-Aer.
- **The Core Fear:** That the steward puts his own man in the bridge-house, and the tally comes out true.
- **Moral Compromises:**
  - **The tally.** He keeps it, and he keeps it short. Every year the village's count comes in a little under the harvest — by about what the village needs to see out the winter. The steward's third is a third of the tally, not of the barley.
  - **The bridge-penny.** By old custom every crossing pays a penny, and the penny mends the bridge. He has never spent it on anything else, and he has never let the steward see the book.

## 📜 Backstory & Current Role
Colm was born at Wether Bridge, worked the barges as a young man until the pier took his knee, and came home to his father's strip. At thirty-eight the village chose him reeve because no one else would stand up to the stewards. Nobody has stood against him at a Sheaf-Day since.

His village is the one in [[Jeerdan Darcy]]'s story: the Warden came through in a bad wet season and ploughed for four days, and a fortnight later the steward came and took a third. Colm wrote that third down short as well.

**Why he is losing.**
- The factors' auditors come at the Draw-Down, and this year they are coming to Wether Bridge first.
- **Rafe Hobbius**, the steward of the river spur, wants a reeve of his own choosing. A reeve chosen by the tenants is not, strictly, anything the Duke's law requires.
- The north pier is scouring faster than the bridge-penny can mend it.
- Wether Bridge carries everything that crosses the lower Aer, so the Duke's council has started to take an interest in who holds it.

He cannot win any of this. He walks the bridge every morning anyway.

**What he has instead.** Once a year, on the bridge-mending day, the whole village turns out with picks and baskets to work the piers. Nobody is paid. The children carry stones. It is the best day of the year in Wether Bridge, and Colm makes sure there is beer.

> **GM-facing.** Wether Bridge is the only stone crossing of the lower Aer. An army coming north out of the [[Silted Marshes]] toward Millhaven or De Vonce crosses here or swims. If Tuwal Ghorun moves at the 59 A.A. Low Moons (see [[The Twelgorn Kingdom]]), the Duke's council will have to decide whether to hold the bridge or break it. Breaking it would leave the southern villages, Colm's among them, on the wrong side of the river.

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 2** — he holds a crossing between the seats, not a seat.
- **Chokepoint:** the bridge, and the tally.
- **Enforcement:** twenty years of Sheaf-Day votes. A village that will not hand over its reeve.
- **Geographic anchor:** Wether Bridge.
- **Bypass:** ford the Aer in the dry turns; or replace him.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is that he is cheating the Duke every year, and one audit will end him.
  - **The mote** is the bridge-mending day, and a village that has eaten through every winter he has been reeve.
