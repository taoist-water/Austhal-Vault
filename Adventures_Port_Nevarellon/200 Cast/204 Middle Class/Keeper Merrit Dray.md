# Character: Keeper Merrit Dray
#cast/active #status/solid

> "The Arbiters rule and the Keeper files. That's the shape of it, and you'll not find a man in that building who'll tell you it's the truth." — a Notary of the Plumb Court, declining to be named

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Merrit Dray. *The Keeper*. No honorific beyond the post; he has refused three
- **Current Occupation:** **Keeper of the Register** — singular post, total custody of the vault at [[The Cult of the Zenith|The Plumb Court]]
- **Social Class / Standing:** Landed by office, not by birth. Born to a hauling family in a Stonereach mining village; *dray* is the cart, and the name is the trade
- **Primary Residence:** Two rooms adjoining the vault stair. He has lived within forty feet of the Register for thirty-one years
- **Affiliations:** [[The Cult of the Zenith]]

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** Dwarf, 118. Broad, slow, and entirely unhurried. He will outlive the current High Arbiter by a margin nobody in the building discusses
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** His hands are ruined — three decades of vellum, ink, and stone-cold air have left the joints thickened and stiff, and he can no longer cut a quill without help. Worse, and quieter: **a chronic cough from lamp-black and vellum dust in a sealed room.** The vault is kept cold and dry to preserve the records, and it is slowly making him unable to be in it. **His entire power is physical custody, and the room is evicting him.** He has begun sending Keepers down in his place for the long sections and has not told the High Arbiter
- **Financial Status:** Salaried, modest, entirely clean. He has never taken a bribe. **The Cobalt Feather stopped approaching him eleven years ago, and he finds that insulting rather than reassuring** — a man nobody bothers to corrupt is a man somebody has decided is irrelevant, or has found another way around
- **Equipment & Upkeep:** **The key.** Singular, iron, unlovely, and the only one. A plate of comparison seals and a set of fine steel needles for testing wax, both of which he still uses himself despite the hands. A rack of stamped brass tallies for tracking which section a Copyist is working

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** **A successor, and there isn't one.** The Copyists are lay and unordained. The Notaries are competent and twenty-six. The one Keeper senior enough for the post is High Quarter-born and would hand the vault to whoever asked nicely and dressed well. Dray has been quietly training a Notary from the Foundry Slips for two years without telling her what for
- **The Core Fear:** **Being asked directly.** Not exposure — he has no fear of being investigated, because he has left nothing to find. But he has spent a hundred and eighteen years on the accuracy of records, and if an Arbiter stands in front of him and asks plainly whether he delayed that verification, **he will say yes.** He will not construct a lie. Everything after that is arithmetic
- **Moral Compromises:** Precisely one, and it took eleven weeks. See below

## 📜 Backstory & Current Role
A hauler's son from a Stonereach village who came down to the coast at forty with a good hand and no prospects, took a Copyist's bench because it was indoor work, and was promoted for the only quality the Zenith reliably rewards in the low-born: he was never once wrong. Thirty-one years later he holds the vault.

**The eleven weeks.** A High Quarter house brought a matter that, if the Arbiter ruled as expected, would have voided the title on an entire street in [[The Sunken Ward]] — second-generation Corvus refugees holding papers whose provenance would not survive scrutiny. The ruling needed one verification from the Register. It is a two-day job.

Dray took eleven weeks. He never refused, never obstructed, and never once said anything untrue. He requested a clarification. He requested a second clarification. A Copyist was reassigned. A section was found to require re-shelving. By the time the verification landed on the Arbiter's bench the house's principal was dead, the heir had other priorities, and the political weather had turned.

**Several hundred people are still in their homes, and not one of them knows why.** Dray considers this the single worst thing he has ever done, because he was not appointed to decide anything and he decided. The Zenith's whole claim to exist is that rulings come from hearings. He held a hearing of one, in his own head, and won it.

Every day since, he has been more rigorous than anyone in that building — which is not penance so much as the only available alternative to thinking about it.

**The fault line he embodies:** doctrinally the Keepers are subordinate to the Arbiters. In practice the Register is the only real power in the Plumb Court, because a Keeper who slow-walks a verification can strangle a ruling without ever disobeying one. Dray proved it works. **He is also, therefore, the man best placed to understand that a Keeper could unmake one of the remaining [[Reference - The Wondrous Markets|Wondrous Markets]] the same way** — no motion, no fee, no hearing, no record of a decision. He has thought about this at some length and finds it the most frightening fact in his own post.
