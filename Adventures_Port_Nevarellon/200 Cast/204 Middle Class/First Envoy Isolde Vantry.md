# Character: First Envoy Isolde Vantry
#cast/active #status/solid

> "Ask her which fork to use, and she'll tell you without blinking. Ask her where she's really from, and watch the room go quiet." — Overheard at a High Quarter dinner

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Isolde Vantry / "The Rented Duchess" (a street nickname, half sardonic and half admiring)
- **Current Occupation:** First Envoy of [[The Golden Company|The Golden Company]] — head of the Envoy Corps, "The Second Contract."
- **Social Class / Standing:** Un-Landed-born; Landed by Commission. Moves fluidly through Landed and noble society without ever fully belonging to it.
- **Primary Residence:** No fixed personal residence — she lives embedded in whichever client household currently retains her, with a small private room at **The Sovereign's Gate** for the gaps between contracts.
- **Affiliations:** The Golden Company (First Envoy); a rotating roster of private clients among the nobility and guild-masters, kept confidential as a matter of professional discretion.

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** 37 years old. Exceptional conditioning kept quietly beneath tailored dress — she moves like a court lady and fights like a soldier, and prefers clients never see the second half of that until they need to.
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** An old dueling scar along her left forearm, always hidden under long sleeves — less out of vanity than professional pride; she never wants a client to see a mark that suggests a situation once slipped out of her control.
- **Financial Status:** Genuinely wealthy by Un-Landed-origin standards, though her prestige converts poorly into permanent security. She's quietly saving toward a Guild Charter or a small property deed of her own — a "real" Landed future that doesn't evaporate the day the Company decides she's finished.
- **Equipment & Upkeep:** Tailored clothing indistinguishable from genuine nobility, a slender Company-issue High-Steel rapier disguised with ornamental fittings, and a leather dossier of current **Writs of Voice** — the exact limits of what she's authorized to agree to for whichever client currently retains her.

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** Secure a long-term, high-status retainer — ideally with a Duchy rather than a mere guild-master — that would cement both her reputation and her financial security before age or a single bad contract ends her career.
- **The Core Fear:** A client engagement going wrong in a way that gets blamed on her rather than them. Despite her rank, she is still fundamentally hired help, and a Landed client's word will always outweigh hers in any dispute.
- **Moral Compromises:** Once personally oversaw a duel she privately believed was a wealthy client using the formal rules to legally kill a weaker rival under color of law. Said nothing — refusing the assignment would have ended her career for the sake of a stranger she couldn't have saved anyway.

## 📜 Backstory & Current Role
Born in [[000 Atlas/The Sunken Ward|The Sunken Ward]] to a family of Corvus refugees, Vantry enlisted young and was noticed almost immediately by the High Captain who preceded [[High Captain Marco|Marco]] — a man who saw something unusual in her poise and quick tongue and personally arranged years of etiquette, rhetoric, and dueling instruction most Company soldiers never receive. She became the first, and still one of very few, locally-raised Envoys to reach the top of the specialist track. She now trains the handful of soldiers being considered to follow her, and personally vets every prospective Second Contract client alongside Marco's final sign-off.

She finds Duke Tythius De Vonce's standing refusal to retain an Envoy both insulting and — privately — something she respects more than she'd ever say out loud.
