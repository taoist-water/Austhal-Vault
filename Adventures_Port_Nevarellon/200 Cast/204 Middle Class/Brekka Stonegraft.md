# Character: Brekka Stonegraft
#cast/active #status/draft

> "I stamp a shaft when I would send my own crew down it. That's the whole of the job. The Duke would like it to be more complicated." — Brekka Stonegraft

> "She's never once been wrong. That's what makes her so expensive." — Duke Brennus Scutarius

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Brekka Stonegraft. *Stonegraft* is the lineage-word, not a surname: her line have surveyed shafts for nine generations. In the passes she is "the Stamp".
- **Current Occupation:** Master-surveyor of the **Deepgraft** mines in the [[Duchy of Stonereach]]. No shaft opens without her stamp.
- **Social Class / Standing:** A subject of [[Ubaraz Kingdom|Ubaraz]], working in Stonereach under the Ubaraz contract. On the coast's rolls she is nobody at all, and in Deepgraft she is the most important person in the valley.
- **Primary Residence:** The surveyor's house at the Deepgraft pit-head, and the shafts themselves.
- **Affiliations:** The Ubaraz contract crews, and her own line.

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** Dwarf, 141. Compact, grey-braided, and tireless.
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** Three fingers on the left hand, lost to a cable-run sixty years ago. She has never complained, and she still points with the stumps.
- **Financial Status:** Paid by Ubaraz, not by the Duke, which is the point of her.
- **Equipment & Upkeep:** The stamp itself — iron, very old, her line's mark — plus a sounding-hammer, a plumb-line and the shaft-books.

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** Keep the deep drift at Deepgraft closed.
- **The Core Fear:** That the Duke finds a human surveyor willing to stamp what she will not.
- **Moral Compromises:**
  - **The Notch.** She knows the Sun-Iron goes out over the Notch to the [[The Iron-Anchor Syndicate|Iron-Anchor]]. It is not her business, so she has never made it her business.
  - **What she knows.** She reports to Ubaraz everything she finds, and to the Duke what the contract requires.

## 📜 Backstory & Current Role
Brekka came over the Long Graft from Ubaraz eighty years ago, before the Corvus road died, and stayed. Her mother surveyed Deepgraft before her. The human crews trust her stamp more than they trust the Duke's coin, because nobody who went down a Stonegraft shaft has died of the shaft.

**The drift.** Deepgraft's richest seam runs down and west, under the range, and the Duke wants it opened. Brekka went down to its face last year, put her hand on the rock, and came up and refused the stamp. She has told the Duke the ground is unsound, and that is true. She has not told him what the rock felt like, and she will not.

**Why she is holding.** The crews will not go down an unstamped shaft, and every human surveyor in Stonereach was trained by her line. Brennus can wait, or he can break the oldest arrangement in the passes. So far he is waiting.

> **GM-facing.** The deep drift is heading toward the seam: the same heat-scarred fracturing that opened under Corvus in 8 A.A. and runs north-east under the Inner Sea to the Broken Spires (see [[The Inner Sea]]). The rock at the drift-face is warm, and it is glassy. Brekka knows what that means, because Ubaraz has known since 8 A.A. what it looks like. It is why Ubaraz's gates are shut. Ubaraz engineers cut the Corvus deep works that broke into the temple; some of them joined the Undercourt; and it was a Ubaraz engineer, one of the Seven, who brought the mountain down on the door (see [[Duchy of Corvus]]). Brekka's line has never forgotten any of it.

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 2** — she holds the mines between the seats, by expertise rather than by right.
- **Chokepoint:** the stamp. No shaft at Deepgraft opens without it.
- **Enforcement:** the crews' trust, and nine generations of never being wrong.
- **Geographic anchor:** the Deepgraft pit-head.
- **Bypass:** a human surveyor willing to stamp the drift — and a crew willing to go down it.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is what she knows and keeps.
  - **The mote** is that no one has ever died in a shaft she stamped.
