# Character: High Captain Marco
#cast/active #status/solid

> "Five thrones carved the coast from the wild. Four remain standing, one rules an ossuary, and all of them watch the northern sky with fear." — High Captain Marco

> "The High Captain doesn't sleep. He just changes which window he's staring out of." — Unknown Company sentry

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Marco Ferranti / "High Captain Marco" / "The Northern Watcher" (a nickname worn half as respect, half as mockery, by his own junior officers)
- **Current Occupation:** High Captain of [[The Golden Company|The Golden Company]]; senior field commander of the Port Nevarellon garrison.
- **Social Class / Standing:** Landed by Commission — the Company's founding charter is recognized under the Ducal Accord as equivalent to a Guild Charter, granting sworn officers full Landed legal standing without requiring personal property.
- **Primary Residence:** A spare command suite within **The Sovereign's Gate**, [[The Great Anchor Basin|The Great Anchor Basin]].
- **Affiliations:** The Golden Company (High Captain); answers, at a remove, to the Company's Governing Charter overseas.

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** 54 years old. Lean and still capable in a fight, but visibly worn thin by decades of campaign life followed by a decade of administrative war he can't win with a sword.
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** Chronic, severe insomnia. He sleeps in broken two-hour stretches and has for years — a habit that started as battlefield vigilance and calcified into something closer to a compulsion. His staff quietly track which of his window-shutters are open at night to gauge his mood.
- **Financial Status:** Comfortable by Company officer standards, but he reinvests most of his personal wealth into paying independent scouts and informants along the [[The Jagged Spine|Jagged Spine]] passes — an expense the Council refuses to fund from the Contract Sum, which he considers a dangerous economy.
- **Equipment & Upkeep:** Standard Company High-Steel officer's kit and a well-worn dueling saber he rarely draws anymore. Keeps a private map-room at the Sovereign's Gate tracking reported activity near the Corvus Scar, updated obsessively and shown to almost no one.

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** Secure Council authorization and funding for expanded scouting patrols toward the Spine — a request denied every season as an unnecessary expense against a threat nobody else believes is imminent.
- **The Core Fear:** That when whatever is stirring north of the Corvus Scar finally resurfaces, the Contract — built to police merchants and put down riots — will prove structurally unable to respond in time, and his warnings will have been ignored until it was too late to matter.
- **Moral Compromises:** Enforces the Council's Edicts with lethal, undiscriminating precision against Un-Landed civilians, because maintaining visible order is the price of the funding and legitimacy he needs for his real priority. Has also quietly declined to chase down several suspected [[The Cobalt Feather Syndicate|Cobalt Feather]] forgeries — to him, hunting smugglers is a distraction from the only threat he considers actually serious.

## 📜 Backstory & Current Role
Marco is not a Civil War veteran — he's second-generation Company, born to an officer family already serving the Contract, and transferred to the Port Nevarellon garrison roughly two decades ago as a rising captain. He was personally mentored by his predecessor as High Captain, a man who first identified a young dockworker's daughter named Isolde Vantry as Envoy material and began building the modern Envoy Corps. Marco inherited both the command and the project a decade ago, and expanded it — not for prestige, but because he saw clear strategic value in having Company-trained specialists embedded inside noble households: eyes and ears he could never otherwise place in the High Quarter.

He respects Duke Tythius De Vonce more than he'll admit and resents that the feeling isn't returned. Where Tythius sees an occupying foreign garrison, Marco sees a Duke too proud to share intelligence about the one threat that could actually unmake both their houses.
