# Character: Bond-Master Tallis Gorrius
#cast/active #status/draft

> "Everything is taken in its turn. The pans take their turn of you, and then you're done. I didn't make the tide." — Tallis Gorrius

> "Fewest dead of any pans on the coast. He'll tell you that himself. He'll tell you why, too, if you ask him: a dead man's a debt written off." — a Brinewick Tongue

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Tallis Gorrius, born Tallis Gorr / "the Bond-Master" / "Gorr" (behind his back, in the bond-rows)
- **Current Occupation:** Bond-Master of the **Brinewick** pans for the Bonds book of [[Valerius Family|House Valerius]]
- **Social Class / Standing:** Landed by deed, bought nineteen years ago. He Latinised the name the same year and kept the given name: **Tallis** is a Tidespoken devotional, and he will not scrub it (see [[Reference - Name Tables]]).
- **Primary Residence:** The bond-master's house above the Brinewick pans: stone, warm, and better furnished than anything in Saltmere outside the Table Hall.
- **Affiliations:** The Bonds book, which employs him. He is not of the blood and holds no shares.

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** Human, 46. Heavy, well fed, and unhurried.
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** The Docks cough from his childhood, which has never left him. Hands that still crack in the cold, though he has not worked salt in twenty years.
- **Financial Status:** Comfortable, and paid by results.
- **Equipment & Upkeep:** A good coat, a bond-ledger, and the key to the pan-store.

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** Make the yield at the next Salt-Draw without the extra squeeze Duke Junia wants. A squeezed pan dies, and a dead pan pays nothing.
- **The Core Fear:** Going back. His father died on a debtors' hulk off the Foundry Slips, owing less than Tallis now spends on wine in a year.
- **Moral Compromises:** None that he feels.
  - **He never closes a bond.** Bonds are not built to close; he did not build them, and he does not pretend otherwise.
  - **He keeps the pans alive** because the dead are a loss. He has done the sums, and his pans bury fewer than any on the coast.

## 📜 Backstory & Current Role
Tallis was born in [[The Muddy Docks]] to a Tidespoken family, and he still keeps the faith. He gives the Mother's share and he names his dead at Thalass's Count. He took the teaching that *everything is taken in its turn* and found it fitted the pans exactly.

He came to Brinewick as a clerk, made himself indispensable to the Bonds book, bought his deed at twenty-seven, and has run the largest bond-worked pans on the coast for a decade. He is very good at it. The Table thinks of him the way it thinks of a good galley.

**The Crock.** Every pan-gang keeps a crock. Each worker drops a pinch of every wage into it — a few coppers a turn — and at Salt-Draw the gangs pool their crocks and buy out one bond, chosen by lot. One worker walks free each year. Tallis allows it. He has worked out that it costs him one worker a year and buys him eleven months of quiet, and he calls it the cheapest thing he owns.

**The Dry Pans.** When a runaway heads north into the old pans, Tallis sends no one after them. *The pans take their turn*, he says. If he is asked whether he believes what the bond-rows whisper about the woman with the cup, he changes the subject (see [[Duchy of Valerius]]).

> **GM-facing.** He is not losing. He is the counterweight to [[Castellan Senna Cade]] and [[Reeve Colm Hollick]]: a Layer 2 holder who is competent, rational and entirely comfortable, and who sleeps well.

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 2** — he holds the pans between the seats, on the Table's behalf.
- **Chokepoint:** the bonds of Brinewick, and the pan-store key.
- **Enforcement:** the Debt-Bond, lodged in the Register; and the pan-store, which feeds or doesn't.
- **Geographic anchor:** the Brinewick pans.
- **Bypass:** running for the beaches to boil free salt; an Arbiter who reads a bond closely; the Crock.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is every bond in Brinewick.
  - **The mote** is the Crock, which belongs to the pan-workers, not to him. One name a year is read out in Salt-Draw Square, and the whole of Brinewick goes to hear it.
