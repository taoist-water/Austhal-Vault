# Character: Sergeant Wren Cobb
#cast/active #status/solid

> "She wears the gold cloak, but she still knows which docks flood first. That's worth more to us than any patrol schedule." — A Muddy Docks resident, unnamed

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Wren Cobb
- **Current Occupation:** Sergeant, [[The Golden Company|The Golden Company]] (Standing Garrison Track); assigned to the Muddy Docks patrol rotation.
- **Social Class / Standing:** Un-Landed-born; Landed by Commission only for the duration of her enlistment — a status that lapses instantly if she's ever discharged or deserts.
- **Primary Residence:** Company barracks housing near the Basin. Her mother and younger sibling still live in [[The Muddy Docks|The Muddy Docks]].
- **Affiliations:** The Golden Company (Sergeant, Rank and File).

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** 28 years old. Dock-hardened, physically capable, moves like someone who spent years hauling cargo before she ever held a halberd.
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** Two fingers on her left hand were badly crushed in a stevedore accident before she enlisted; they never fully straightened, and she's learned to compensate without comment.
- **Financial Status:** Sends roughly a third of her 3 sp daily wage back to her family in the Docks. Owns almost nothing beyond her kit.
- **Equipment & Upkeep:** Standard Company-issue gear, kept in meticulous condition — good inspection scores are the only reliable path to promotion she has.

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** Get reassigned somewhere she won't be ordered to personally collect Vagrancy Tolls from people she grew up with.
- **The Core Fear:** Being ordered to arrest or hurt someone she knows by name, with no legal way to refuse the order.
- **Moral Compromises:** Has quietly let recognized faces through toll checkpoints without paying, logging it as "insufficient specie, warned and released" instead of reporting it correctly — a small mercy that would end her career, and possibly worse, if anyone ever audited the pattern.

## 📜 Backstory & Current Role
Cobb enlisted young, specifically to get her family off a starvation wage. She's good at the job — good enough to make Sergeant — and sits squarely inside the exact structural fault line [[High Captain Marco|High Captain Marco]] worries about at the institutional level: a soldier whose loyalty to the cot and the coin is real, but who hasn't stopped being one of the people she's ordered to police.

- **Chain of command:** Reports up through Company officers to [[High Captain Marco|High Captain Marco]]
- **Represents:** The Golden Company's "Recruitment Fault Line"
- **Family:** Unnamed mother and younger sibling, [[The Muddy Docks|The Muddy Docks]] — open hook for future NPCs
