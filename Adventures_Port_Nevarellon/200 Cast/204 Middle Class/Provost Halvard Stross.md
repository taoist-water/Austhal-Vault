# Character: Provost Halvard Stross
#cast/active #status/solid

> "Stross doesn't raise his voice. He doesn't have to. He just writes your name down." — Common saying among the Blue-Cloak Watch

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Halvard Stross
- **Current Occupation:** Provost of [[The Golden Company|The Golden Company]]; oversees the conduct and discipline of the [[Faction - The Civic Constabulary (The Coppers)|Civic Constabulary]] citywide.
- **Social Class / Standing:** Landed by Commission.
- **Primary Residence:** A spare office-residence near the Trade Plazas' magistrate buildings, kept deliberately close to where formal complaints and court records are filed.
- **Affiliations:** The Golden Company (Provost); answers directly to [[High Captain Marco|High Captain Marco]].

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** 61 years old. Rigid posture, methodical movements, unnervingly patient.
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** Significant hearing loss in his left ear, the legacy of an old alchemical detonation during his early campaign years. He leans in and reads lips more than he'd ever admit — a habit that unintentionally makes him seem even more intimidating during questioning.
- **Financial Status:** Comfortable, cautious, and unshowy. Saves methodically; spends on almost nothing beyond his ledgers and a single well-kept arming sword.
- **Equipment & Upkeep:** A plain, impeccably maintained High-Steel arming sword he has drawn perhaps twice in the last decade. Carries a private ledger — not official Company record — cross-referencing patterns of Constabulary bribery, missed arrests, and unexplained wealth.

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** Build an airtight, comprehensive case against systemic Constabulary corruption — particularly Captain Elias "Half-Step" Vance's Toll-House — without triggering a political embarrassment that implicates the Company for having tolerated it this long.
- **The Core Fear:** That the corruption, once fully traced, reaches high enough — a Council patron, or worse, a fellow Company officer — that exposing it becomes more dangerous to the city's stability than the corruption itself.
- **Moral Compromises:** Has known about Vance's selective enforcement for well over a year and deliberately sat on it, holding out for a larger, more useful case rather than an isolated dismissal. His patience is a cost paid by whoever the Coppers fail to protect while he waits.

## 📜 Backstory & Current Role
Stross rose through the Company on a reputation for total incorruptibility, which made him the obvious choice when the Provost post needed filling after a predecessor was quietly found to be taking bribes of his own. He is respected, feared, and almost entirely without friends inside the Company — nobody wants to be seen too near him doing anything that might end up in the ledger.
