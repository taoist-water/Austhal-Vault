# Character: Verrine Sallow
#cast/active #status/solid

> "Twelve hundred halberds and every one of them eats what she sells. Marco commands the Golden Company. Sallow feeds it. Ask yourself which of those is the harder thing to replace." — Provost Halvard Stross

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Verrine Sallow, born **Vess Sallow**. *The Victualler*, *the Nine-Year Woman*. Signs instruments as **Sallow of the Contract Seat**
- **Current Occupation:** Councillor of Five — **The Contract Seat**. Principal victualler to the [[The Golden Company]] under standing supply contract
- **Social Class / Standing:** Landed by purchase, nine years. The newest and youngest councillor by a wide margin
- **Primary Residence:** A modern, aggressively expensive house at the outer edge of [[The High Quarter]], on a street that was warehouses within living memory
- **Affiliations:** [[Council of Five]], the Victuallers' and Provisioners' interest, [[The Golden Company]] (commercial, not sworn)

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** Human, 39. Hard, fast, chronically under-slept. Runs a supply operation and a Council seat simultaneously and is visibly burning the candle from both ends
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** A badly-healed left collarbone from a warehouse fall at twenty-two — it aches in wet weather and she cannot raise that arm above her head, which she has arranged her entire public bearing to conceal. Early-stage grain-lung from a decade of standing in her own storehouses
- **Financial Status:** Enormously solvent and dangerously concentrated. Somewhere between sixty and seventy percent of her income is the Golden Company supply contract. If the Contract Sum is ever late, she is the first person in Port Nevarellon to find out, and the first ruined
- **Equipment & Upkeep:** A tally-frame she still uses herself. A Golden Writ purchased the day she was enfranchised, wax intact, worn openly at every session — a thing the older councillors find gauche and she does entirely on purpose

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** Get a written legal opinion on whether the 99-Year Contract obligates the Company to answer an invasion executed in the name of **Tuwal Ghorun** — a polity no coastal register lists under that name. She has asked three advocates. Two declined. She has not told the Council she is asking
- **The Core Fear:** That the answer is *no*, and that she is the only person who will have known it in advance. She does not fear ruin so much as she fears being the one who saw it and could not make four other people care in time
- **Moral Compromises:** She got her deed by feeding an occupying army, and the coin that bought it came from the Contract Sum, which comes from the Vagrancy Tolls, which come from the Docks she was born in. She has never pretended otherwise. When it was thrown at her in chamber in her first year she agreed, in full, on the record, and moved to the next item — which is the moment the older councillors stopped treating her as a novelty

## 📜 Backstory & Current Role
Vess Sallow was a warehouse clerk's daughter who noticed, at nineteen, that twelve hundred soldiers require eleven tons of food a month and that nobody had organised it properly. She spent fifteen years making herself unavoidable to the Company's quartermasters and then bought a deed with the proceeds.

**She Latinised backwards, and it was deliberate.** Convention on the coast is to Latinise the *surname* on climbing — *Cobb* becomes *Cobbius*. Sallow changed only her given name and left the family name exactly as it was, which the High Quarter reads as either an error or an insult and which she has never clarified. Her mother is still alive in [[The Muddy Docks]] and still called Sallow.

She holds the **Contract Seat**: the Golden Company relationship, the annual Contract Sum, defence expenditure, and all dealings with [[High Captain Marco]]. She got it because she was already the Company's stomach, and the other four could not construct an argument against the person who already held the leverage. **She did not get the Long Seat, which she wanted, and she has not forgotten who blocked it.**

**She is the only councillor who has read the Contract in full, recently, with an advocate.** It is 41 rings from expiry and she has begun to suspect that the document everyone treats as the city's foundation has holes in it wide enough to lose a war through.
