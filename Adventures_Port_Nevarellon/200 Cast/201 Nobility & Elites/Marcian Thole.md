# Character: Marcian Thole
#cast/active #status/solid

> "He is the only one of the five who has ever been paid by the day. It has not made him kind. It has made him impossible to lie to about timber." — Suse Kellard, rope-walk foreman

## 📊 Vital Statistics
- **Full Name / Aliases:** Marcian Thole. *The Deaf Man*, *Old Keel*. Signs instruments as **Thole of the Harbour Seat**
- **Current Occupation:** Councillor of Five — **The Harbour Seat**. Master of the Thole Yards
- **Social Class / Standing:** Landed. Fourth-generation shipwright dynasty; enfranchised by keel ownership rather than by capital
- **Primary Residence:** A converted sail-loft above the Thole Yards in [[The Foundry Slips]] — the only councillor who does not live in [[The High Quarter]]
- **Affiliations:** [[Council of Five]], the Shipwrights' Guild, [[The Great Anchor Basin]]

## ⚖️ Realism & Physicality
- **Age & Vitality:** Human, 58. Broad, stooped, and visibly worn out. He looks a decade older than Lucia Marrenhal despite being fourteen years her senior in a way that reads as *labour*, not age
- **Physical Flaws / Limitations:** Substantially deaf from thirty years of caulking hammers in enclosed hulls — he reads lips and turns his good ear like a man aiming it. Three fingers on his left hand set wrong after a spar crushed them. He cannot hear a whispered aside in the chamber, which means he cannot hear the deals being made across him, and everyone knows it
- **Financial Status:** Asset-rich and cash-poor, which on the Council is a form of poverty. His wealth is timber, slipways, and four deep-water keels. He has been land-poor twice and mortgaged the yards to survive both. Marrenhal holds one of those notes
- **Equipment & Upkeep:** A shipwright's slick and a folding rule he still carries out of habit. His Golden Writ is thirty years old, the wax reseated four times, the crossguard wire green with verdigris. He has never drawn the sword

## 🧠 Psychology & Drive
- **Immediate Goal:** Force a survey of the curtain wall at the Foundry Slips onto the agenda for a fourth time. It has been deferred three times because defence funding prioritises the Basin and High Quarter, and Thole is the only councillor whose own workforce lives behind the bad section
- **The Core Fear:** **Year 99.** He has 41 rings of the Contract Bell left and he has done the arithmetic: he will not live to see it, his children will be old, and his grandchildren will inherit a city with no army, no militia tradition, and no memory of how to raise one. He is the only councillor treating this as a problem rather than a curiosity
- **Moral Compromises:** He has voted for the Vagrancy Tolls every year of his tenure. His justification is that his yards employ four hundred Un-Landed at Guild rates and that the alternative is not a fairer city but a poorer one — an argument he has repeated so often he has stopped hearing it, which is the one thing his deafness is not responsible for

## 📜 Backstory & Current Role
The Tholes have built hulls in Port Nevarellon since before the Civil War, and stayed out of it — the yards took royal contracts and merchant contracts in the same year and were careful never to be caught having an opinion. That caution is the family inheritance. Marcian took the Harbour Seat at 41 when his predecessor died mid-term, and won the by-election because the Basin's registered keel-owners trusted a man who could still tell rot from stain by smell.

He holds the **Harbour Seat**: Basin cargo tariffs, deep-water registration, the Reckoning of Keels, and the harbour defence budget. It is the seat that produces the most revenue and the least influence, because tariff schedules are technical and nobody contests what they cannot follow. Thole is aware he is tolerated as the man who does the arithmetic.

**His long game is a militia.** Not stated aloud, because a Landed citizen proposing an armed body outside the Golden Company is proposing something that looks, from certain angles, exactly like raising a private force — the precise thing the 99-Year Contract exists to prevent. He has instead been quietly expanding the Yards' fire-and-flood crews, drilling them, and classifying the drills as insurance compliance. It is a militia. It is four hundred people. It is not called that on any instrument, and Marrenhal worked out what he was doing two years ago and has said nothing, for reasons he has not been able to establish.
