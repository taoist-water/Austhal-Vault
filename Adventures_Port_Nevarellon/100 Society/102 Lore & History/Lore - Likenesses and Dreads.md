# Lore: Likenesses and Dreads
#lore/creatures #lore/cosmology #status/draft

> "She sat with him all night, my lady. Nobody let her in. In the morning he was gone and there were feathers on the sill." — a hospice porter, House of Mercy

> **GM reference.** Ground truth, except where marked as what people say. What the coast actually knows is visitations, saints' sightings, deathbed stories and the things people swear they saw in a mirror.

---

## 🌗 What they are
Every Great Named was split at the Shattering into a hoped-for face in the High Reach, a feared shape in the Undertow, and the current that runs between them (see [[Cosmology - The Celestial Graveyard and The war of Creation#🌗 The Three Shares|The Three Shares]]). A Share is not a person and does not walk. But where enough of it gathers in one place on the Disk, it takes the only shape it has: **the mortal shape the Naming gave it**.
- **A Likeness** is a Reach share in mortal shape: the hope, standing in the room.
- **A Dread** is an Undertow share in mortal shape: the fear, standing in the room.

Neither is the Creator, and neither is a god. A Likeness or a Dread is a shape made out of hope or fear, as thin as the feeling that gathered it, wanting what its Share wants and nothing else.

**Where they gather.** Where a Run surfaces (see [[Cosmology - The Tideways]]); at the Low Moons; and wherever many mortals hope or fear the same thing at the same time — a deathbed, a plague pit, a doomed post, a sinking ship, a courtroom waiting on a verdict. They are often present at the Going-Out, which is why so many people on the coast have seen someone sitting with the dying who was not there before.

**What they look like.** The Share's shape, worn in the local face. A Likeness of the Loving Mother looks like the mother the people in the room remember. A Dread of the List looks like the clerk who once came for their rent.

**How long they last.** Until the feeling that gathered them disperses: the patient dies or recovers, the ship goes down or makes harbour, the verdict is given. Or until they are given what they want. They can be fought, and they can be driven off. They cannot be killed, because there is nothing there to kill; a Likeness beaten in one sickroom is gathering in another before morning.

**What people say:** visitations. Saints' sightings. The Grey Lady on the hospice stair, the drowned man at the bottom of the harbour steps, the stranger at the gaming table who never loses. Every cult has its sightings, and every cult is sure they are its saint.

> **GM-facing — the saints.** When a priest sees Senecus at the pass, or a Tongue sees Thalass rise out of a calm sea, what they see is a Likeness wearing a saint's face. The saint is the door, the cults teach, and the Domain is the room. A Likeness is the room putting on the door's face to be recognised.

## 🕯️ The fourteen
| Share | Likeness or Dread | Appears as | Where it gathers | What it wants |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Loving Mother** | Likeness | A woman weeping gold at the bedside, in whatever face the patient loves best | Sickrooms, hospices, the House of Mercy | To be loved, and to be let close. The patient feels better. Ask the family in ten years |
| **The Appetite** | Dread | A nursing figure too thin to stand, cradling something | Plague pits, famine, anywhere the dying are crowded | To feed |
| **The Rest** | Likeness | A hooded grave-keeper; feathers on the sill afterwards | Gentle deaths; the Quiet Hour | Nothing. It sits with the dying, and the fear goes out of the room |
| **The Hungry Grave** | Dread | A digger in a grave too deep for the body | Mass graves, the Scar's edge, unburied dead | The dead kept in the ground. It hates the Long Carry |
| **The Brine Mother** | Likeness | A woman walking out of the surf | The Sea-Wall, the drowned's Count, a storm the Tongues called | For you to let go of what you are holding |
| **The Drowning Deep** | Dread | A drowned sailor beckoning from just under the surface | Wrecks, the harbour steps at night, leviathan water | For you to come down |
| **The Held Line** | Likeness | A soldier who takes the post beside you | Doomed posts, the Relief, Adder's Gate in winter | The post held, whatever it costs |
| **The Engine** | Dread | A smith at a forge that is never out, making something with too many edges | Forges, the war-fields, Hollowforge at night | Something built to kill |
| **The Enduring** | Likeness | A frost-rimed elder at the door on a winter night | The passes, Pilgrim's Notch, Bare-Teeth Night | For you to last until morning |
| **The Frost That Stops** | Dread | A still figure in a still room; whoever it looks at slows | Blizzards, the high Spine, anyone freezing alone | Everything to stop where it stands |
| **The Judge** | Likeness | A robed figure in the gallery when a true verdict is given | The Plumb Court, a sworn oath, a reckoning | The true measure, given and heard |
| **The List** | Dread | A clerk reading names aloud. Each name read is marked | Debtors' courts, the hulks, a ledger opened at night | Every name read, and every one found wanting |
| **The Mask** | Likeness | A smiling stranger at the gaming table who never loses | Gambling dens, cons, the Crooked Tally | For you to believe your own coin |
| **The Mocking Voice** | Dread | A laughing figure in the mirror, telling the truth | Mirrors, still water, the moment the con fails | For you to hear it |

**Good and evil are still not the axis.** The Rest is a Likeness and asks for nothing; the Loving Mother is a Likeness and is a mouth. The Mocking Voice is a Dread and has never once lied.

## 🔥 The Nameless: Elementals
What was never Named has no shape that mortals gave it, and never takes one.
- **What they are:** raw, unshaped essence gathered hard enough to move: a fire that walks, a storm that turns to follow, a fall of stone that does not stop falling. Nobody named it, so it has no face, no Share and no want. It does what essence does when there is enough of it in one place.
- **Where they gather:** where unanchored wrack spills or burns; on the war-ground, where the bone still bleeds; on the bad nights of the Low Moons; and wherever an Arcanist's working goes badly enough wrong.
- **Why they are worse in one way:** a Likeness or a Dread wants something, and anything that wants something can be bargained with, or given it, or denied it. An Elemental wants nothing. There is no feeling to disperse it and no price to pay it off. It stops when the essence is spent.
- **What people say:** firestorms, ball-lightning in the Spine passes, the fire on the war-fields that would not go out. Most of the coast's Elementals are never recognised as anything but very bad weather.

## 🧭 What they are not
- **Not fey.** The fey quickened out of essence in still water and belong to no Share (see [[Lore - Hags]]).
- **Not the War-Made.** The War-Made were made, with purposes; Likenesses and Dreads are Shares, with wants (see [[Lore - The War-Made]]).
- **Not ghosts.** A ghost is a soul the Unmoving kept from the Going-Out. A Likeness of the Rest at a deathbed is there to help it go.

> **The Mote:** no one on the coast stands a post alone. However bad the night at Adder's Gate gets, there is always someone else on the wall at the worst hour, and the Iron Horizon is right that he looks like Senecus.
