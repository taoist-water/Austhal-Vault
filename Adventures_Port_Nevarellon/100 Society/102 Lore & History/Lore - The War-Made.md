# Lore: The War-Made
#lore/creatures #lore/cosmology #status/draft

> "My grandmother said orcs were made to kill us. My mother said that was a lie. I asked the orc. He said his grandmother said the same about us." — a Sunken Ward dock-boss

> **GM reference.** Ground truth, except where marked as what people say. What the coast actually knows is old songs, the Iron Horizon's maxims, sailors' stories and the bones in the war-fields.

---

## ⚔️ What the War-Made are
In the last years of the War of Creation, both sides made things (see [[Cosmology - The Celestial Graveyard and The war of Creation]]). The Weaponizers made **weapons**, to excise individuation by killing everything that had it. The Alliance made **carriers**, to keep the mortals alive long enough to finish what they had started. Every making was shaped from essence and given a **purpose**, and a purpose is the one part of a making that does not die with its maker.

The War-Made come in two kinds, and the difference is the whole of their story:
- **Peoples.** Makings that bred true. Each generation took a little less of its purpose into it, until there was none left. The monstrous peoples inherited their makers' bodies and none of their convictions. A people is a people.
- **Instruments.** Makings that never bred: single, or few, or built to last. An instrument still carries its purpose, exactly as it was given, in a war that ended before the coast was settled. Most are broken, buried or sunk. Some are not.

**The Quiet made nothing.** It wanted nothing from the mortals and nothing from the war. What it might have made, grave-keepers like Vael did instead (see [[The Cult of the Ashen Veil]]).

## 🔨 The Weaponizers' works
### The Bastion: the Engine's makings
The Bastion was the Creator of thresholds and limits, of where one thing stops and the next begins, and it designed many of the living weapons (see [[The Cult of the Iron Horizon]]).
- **The monstrous peoples.** Orcs, made to break a line; goblins, made to get under it; giants and ogres, made to hold high ground no army could take back. All of them bred true, and none of them is a weapon now. They are peoples, and what the coast believes about their nature is a prejudice with a very old pedigree. Half-orcs on the coast, like [[Maccorrack]], are descended from both sides of the same war.
- **The siege-chimeras.** The Bastion built by joining: a body is a set of thresholds, and it knew exactly where each part could be made to stop and the next begin. Chimeras are bodies made of several animals at once, seamed where the parts meet. They did not breed true. The ones still alive are very old and very few, and every seam on them still knows its purpose: **break a wall**.

### The Unmoving: things made to stop the world
The Unmoving wanted the Sphere frozen as it had been, and every mortal stopped where they stood (see [[The Cult of the Rime-Fang]]).
- **The winter-drake.** Made to make the north stand still. Kaelen killed it and ate it, and it slowed her past feeling. Drakes are the Unmoving's apex instruments; the one in the high Spine was the last anybody knows of.
- **Frost-trolls.** A lesser making, and the reason a troll's wounds close: the Unmoving made them to stay the same shape. Cut one and it returns to what it was. Trolls bred, but badly, and today they are beasts rather than a people.
- **The northern-spires wyvern** that [[Captain Haren Twarde]] killed was an apex predator saturated with unanchored essence from the Undertow. Whether a Weaponizer made it or the Undertow fed it after, its hide still pulls an echo of the beast up from below.

### The Plumb: the measurers
The Plumb held the true line of every thing, and kept the Purge's list of which beings had strayed past restoring (see [[The Cult of the Zenith]]).
- **The measurers** were made to find whatever had strayed from its line and put it back, or end it. They are instruments of wrack and straight edges, tall, plumb-true, and faceless, because nothing the Plumb made was meant to be looked at, only to look. A measurer does not attack what is true to its line. It attacks what has strayed: the risen dead, the Undertow-made, the hag-worked, flesh forced out of its shape. By its measure, every mortal has strayed.
- Most measurers stand inert in old ruins and deep works, still taking the measure of the room. They wake when something strays near enough. A Bog-Hag's customer walking past one is enough.

### The vast ones
Some instruments were as vast as their makers. When the Sphere shattered they were too heavy with their purpose to settle anywhere but down, and they sank into the Undertow and held their shape there. The ground around each one became **a Wreck** (see [[Cosmology - The Tideways]]).
- **What people say:** the pious call them devils and their country the Hells. A devil, the coast says, keeps its word, and only to the letter.
- **What they are:** instruments still carrying out their orders. A vast one bargains because its purpose binds it, and a pact is only an order looking for a way to be carried out. **Malaphar** is one of them. So is whatever came through at Corvus, and so are the winged things that guard the Fourth's circle of fire (see [[Reference - The Wondrous Markets]]).

## 🛶 The Alliance's works: carriers, not weapons
The Alliance gods gambled on the mortals; they did not need to win the war by killing, only to keep the mortals alive long enough. So they made things that **carry, hide, feed and hold**. Their war ended, and their makings were left with nothing to carry. None of that made them kind.

### The Ebb: the leviathans
The Ebb was the Creator of the going-out. Its makings were **carriers across water**: vast, patient things made to take mortals out of the Purge's reach, holding whole peoples in their bellies and in the hollows of their backs, across seas the Weaponizers could not follow (see [[The Tidespoken Clergy]]).
- **After the war** there was nothing left to carry, and the carriers went down into the Drowning Deep, the Ebb's Undertow share, and soured there.
- **The leviathans** that drift up from the abyssal trenches beyond the Shield Atolls are what came back. They still carry. They take a ship down the way they once took a people away, and the Oakhaven fleets that hunt them have no idea they are killing the coast's oldest rescuers (see [[The Inner Sea]]).

### The Mother: the nurses
The Mother fed on those who loved her, and the war gave her more selves to feed on than ever (see [[The Cult of the Weeping Martyr]]).
- **The nurses** were made to keep wounded mortals alive: to find them on the field, hold them, feed them and keep them breathing until they could fight again. A nurse takes years and vigour from what it holds, and gives it back as life — the same trade as the Weepers' golden tears.
- **Now** a nurse holds whatever it finds. A traveller lost in the wrong place can be kept alive by one for years, warm, fed and never let go, while it takes everything they have left.
- **What people say:** folk-tales on the coast have a kind old woman in the wood who takes in the lost and never lets them leave. The Weepers tell the same story with a better ending.

### The Two-Faced: the doubles
The Two-Faced fought on both sides. Its makings did too.
- **The doubles** were perfect reflections of one soldier each, sent to take that soldier's place in the line — and then to break it, or hold it, depending on which side had asked. A double wears its original's face, voice, scars and memories.
- **After the war,** the doubles that survived went on wearing someone. A double that has lived someone's life long enough believes it is them, and so does everyone who knows it. Somewhere on the coast there are families with one at the table.
- **What people say:** changeling stories, and the old rule never to let a stranger see you in a mirror before they have told you their name. The glass-readers (see [[Lore - Arcana]]) would very much like to meet one.

## 🧭 What the War-Made are not
- **Fey** are not made. They quickened in still water, and they had no part in the war (see [[Lore - Hags]]).
- **Wretches** are not made either. A Wretch is what a place keeps (see [[Lore - Wretches]]).
- **Likenesses and Dreads** are not makings but Shares, taking the shapes mortals gave them (see [[Lore - Likenesses and Dreads]]).

> **The Mote:** the leviathans were made to carry people to safety, and the orcs were made to kill them. One of those two is now a people, with families, debts and a dock-boss who will hire you on a bad day. Purpose wears out. Given long enough, it wears out in everyone.
