# Cosmology: The Tideways
#lore/cosmology #status/draft

> "Went down forty fathom on the Spires and came up short a name. My sister says I had one. I believe her." — scratched inside a diving bell recovered off the Broken Spires

> **Ground truth — GM-facing.** No one in Austhal knows this whole. What survives in the world is divers' stories, cult teaching, the Undercourt's ruin and a few things that came back wrong. This note is the map behind them, written to be travelled. For what the layers are and how they formed, see [[Cosmology - The Great Fracture]]. For the Named, their Shares and the passage of the dead, see [[Cosmology - The Celestial Graveyard and The war of Creation]].

## 🧭 The Shape
The Tideways are one continuous body, not separate worlds: the **High Reach** above, the **Slack Water** (the Disk) in the middle, the **Undertow** below. Nothing sits on a border, because there are no borders. There is only depth.

```text
              [ THE OUTER DARK — the Void; the moons drift here ]
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   THE HIGH REACH     the far Heights ........ the Reaches of the Shares
                      the shallows of the Reach (where crossings land)
                              ↑         ↑         ↑
                             Runs: the Domain currents, top to bottom
   THE SLACK WATER    the Disk — seams, still water, war-ground, breaches
                              ↓         ↓         ↓
                      the shallows of the Undertow
   THE UNDERTOW       the Deeps of the Shares .... the Wrecks
                      the far bottom (no one has found it)
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
              [ THE OUTER DARK — beneath the Disk, past the shelf ]
```

- **The shallows.** Each layer is thinnest where it lies nearest the Disk. A crossing almost always lands in the shallows, and most of what comes up or down to the Disk comes from them. The shallows of the Reach are bright and unsettled; the shallows of the Undertow are cold and full of things that have not quite let go.
- **The countries of the Shares.** Each Great Named left a Reach in the High Reach and a Deep in the Undertow (see *The Three Shares*). These are the nearest things the Tideways have to lands: the Mother's Reach is warm, lit and full of the loved; the Plumb's Deep is where the List is kept, and every name on it is read aloud forever.
- **The Runs.** A Domain current runs through every layer between its Reach and its Deep. A Run is the one dependable road in the Tideways: follow the Ebb's Run down from the Sea-Wall and it will take you to the Drowning Deep, because that is where it goes. The Runs are also the road of the dead (see *The Going-Out*).
- **The open layers.** Between the Shares lies essence pulled by hope or fear that took no named shape. The dead who hoped for nothing in particular and feared nothing in particular drift here. It is the largest part of the Tideways, and the least mapped.
- **The Wrecks.** The things the Weaponizers made for the war sank with the Undertow and did not dissolve. Where one of them lies, the Undertow takes its shape: a Wreck is a country built around a war-made thing that still has its orders. The thing that came through at Corvus came from a Wreck. So did whatever stands in the Fourth's circle of fire.
- **The far Heights and the far bottom.** Nobody has reached either. The Ashen Veil's teaching that the dead "go up" imagines a top. The Undertow, the sailors say, has no floor: it just never lets go.

## 🌑 The Outer Dark
Around and beneath the whole of the Tideways is **the Void**: absolute nothingness, in which the Disk drifts. The Bestiary's Void-Touched come from it, the Void-Currents near the easternmost Shield Atolls pull toward it where the ocean shelf drops away, and **the moons drift in it**, dead and glowing.
- **It is not a layer.** The Tideways sort everything by hope and fear. The Void sorts nothing, holds nothing and keeps nothing.
- **It is the only place where the old death still holds.** A soul lost into the Outer Dark goes to no Share and no layer. It ceases to exist, the way every mortal did before the Shattering. This is the one fate the mortals murdered their makers to escape, and it is still out there, past the edge.
- **The moons** hang in it like hulls in a fog. At the Low Moons they hang low enough that the old stories talk about climbing to one. No story says what is there, or who came back.

## 🕳️ Where the Layers Come Close
The Disk is the Slack Water, but it is not evenly still. In some places the layers above or below lie close under the ground, and a crossing is possible, or a thing can come through on its own.
- **Seams.** Where a layer runs close under the rock. The coast knows one: the seam the Corvus temple was built over, which runs north-east to **the Broken Spires** and shows the same glassy, heat-scarred fracture at both ends. Stonereach's deep drift is now heading toward it (see [[Brekka Stonegraft]]).
- **Still water.** Where essence pools and quickens: marsh, tarn, sheltered reef, the drowned edges of old forests. It is the fey's ground, and the reason a hag can uproot only at the Low Moons. Still water opens sideways rather than up or down: it lies closest to the open layers.
- **War-ground.** Where the bone of the War of Creation lies thickest and still bleeds — the Longbarrow fields, the deep works under the mountains. War-ground lies close to the Wrecks.
- **Where a Run surfaces.** Each Domain current runs strongest in a few places on the Disk, and the cults found most of them long ago without knowing what they had found: the moving water past the Sea-Wall, the burial grounds beyond the landward wall, the held pass. A priest standing where their Run surfaces stands on the road.
- **Breaches.** Holes. Something forced the layers apart and they did not close. Corvus is one, and the mountain came down on it. The Fourth's circle of five fire columns is another, and the city stayed standing around it (see [[Reference - The Wondrous Markets]]). A breach needs no craft to cross. That is the problem.
- **Unbroken places.** Rare pockets where a piece of the old Sphere survived the Shattering whole. Inside one, the old cycle still turns: nothing decays, nothing begins and nothing ends, and a day goes round and comes back the same. Getting in can be easy. Getting out means making something *end*, in a place with no endings in it.

## ⚓ Crossing
The cosmology already says how: a mortal can cross *"if they can find a way to shift their own matter to match that layer — though doing so without a stabilizing anchor is usually fatal."* So a crossing needs two things, and the coast already handles both without knowing it.

- **Ballast** shifts you to the layer. Wrack is essence that settled as matter, and it carries its layer with it: **black wrack** weighs a body toward the Undertow, and **pale wrack** lifts it toward the High Reach. The deeper the crossing, the more ballast it takes, and the longer you carry it the more of its layer it puts into you. Ballast is why the Cobalt Feather's shard trade has buyers who will not say what they want it for.
- **An anchor** keeps you yourself. **A mortal's anchor is their name** — and not the name alone, but the name held by others: said, written, remembered. A priest crosses on a saint's name as well as their own, as they cast on one. A traveller whose name is in the grave-books, the Register or the Count is held more firmly than one whose name nobody keeps.
  - **The hag's Price "A Name"** takes exactly this. Anyone who paid it cannot be anchored, and cannot safely cross (see [[Lore - Hags]]).
- **The Low Moons are a spring tide.** When the moons hang low, everything essence-touched loosens — wrack, Bindings, a hag's roots, and the layers themselves. Every crossing is easier on those nights, and so is everything coming the other way.
- **The road of the dead.** The Going-Out crosses every soul without ballast or anchor. A living mortal can follow it — down the Ebb's Run with nothing in their hands, through the Plumb's measure, to the turn — but the passage was not built to bring anyone back. The Bastion's line holds against it. Only the Two-Faced has ever got anyone out the other way.

## ⚠️ The Two Dangers
The layers are dangerous in opposite directions.
- **The Undertow keeps you.** It does not drag you down all at once; it just never lets go once it has you. A traveller who stays too long grows heavier, colder and slower to want to leave, and the black wrack they carried for ballast stops being something they carry.
- **The High Reach remakes you.** The Reach is built out of mortal hope, and it does to a traveller what hope does: it makes them into what someone wished they were. Stay too long and you come back as the person your mother wanted, or the saint your congregation needed, and you will agree with them about it.
- **The open layers lose you.** Nothing there has a shape to hold onto, and a traveller who lets go of their anchor in the open layers is simply not found again.

## 🧳 Who Goes
Almost no one, and never for good reasons.
- **The Seven** went down into Corvus Spire in 8 A.A. Hallam Drey held the stair. Nobody knows how far the others went.
- **Divers on the Broken Spires** go down for the glassy fracture-stone and come up short of things.
- **The Eldest Tongue** walks into the sea at the end, and the Ebb's Run takes them (see [[The Tidespoken Clergy]]).
- **Namers** — Demonology's practitioners — reach down by naming, and the Undercourt reached furthest (see [[Lore - Arcana]]).
- **Hags** move only through the Slack Water, at the Low Moons, between kinds of still ground.
- **Whoever bound Tuwal Ghorun** knew how to hold a Share in the Slack Water. The Binders may still know (see [[The Twelgorn Kingdom]]).

## 🗺️ What This Is For
The shapes an adventure into the Tideways can take, built only from what is already here:
- **Go after a soul** before the line, before the Mother, before the measure. Or after it, into the Share's country where it came to rest.
- **Get back what the Ebb took.** The ferry's fare is whatever the dead held. Some of what it took was never theirs to give.
- **Walk a Run.** A priest of the Domain is the natural guide, and the one most at risk.
- **Enter a Wreck,** to bargain with, rob or end the war-made thing at its heart, which still obeys orders from a war that ended before the coast was settled.
- **Break out of an Unbroken place,** or into one, for something that has not aged since the Sphere.
- **Close a breach.** Nobody has. Corvus was the attempt, and the mountain was the cost.

> **The mote:** you are anchored by being remembered. Every name the grave-keepers write down, every name read out on the Sea-Wall, is a rope someone could climb back on.
