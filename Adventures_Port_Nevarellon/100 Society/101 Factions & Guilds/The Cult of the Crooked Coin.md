# Faction: The Cult of the Crooked Coin
#faction/underground #lore/religion #status/draft

> "Never believe your own coin." — the Crooked Coin's first teaching, and its least practised

> "Rook laughed at the empire's law and the empire's treasury fell down. Tell me again which of us is the fool." — [[Lidda Shoon]]

---

## 🏛️ Overview
- **Domain:** Trickery (see [[Religion - The Pagan Pantheon and the Faith Domains]]).
- **Paragon:** **Rook's Folly, the Grinning Prophet.**
- **Type:** An underground faith with no temple and no clergy. Every devotee is their own priest, and the oldest liar in the room leads the prayer.
- **In Port Nevarellon:** the Docks, the shadows of the Basin, and the [[The Cobalt Feather Syndicate|Cobalt Feather]], whose best people are its most devout. A Crooked Coin name is a credential in the Docks and a liability in the High Quarter (see [[Reference - Name Tables]]).
- **Abroad:** **the Struck City** — the Fourth, across the Broken Ocean, where the Coin is not a cult but half a government (see [[Reference - The Wondrous Markets]]).
- **Trappings:** loaded-dice amulets and mismatched clothes. Prayers are murmured riddles, jokes about authority, and the luck turning.

---

## 🪙 What the Faithful Believe
- **Rook.** Rook was no holy man. He was a cynic and a smuggler in the old empire — the empire the coast's founding families fled — who saw that its laws were a joke and counterfeited its treasury into bankruptcy. The empire never recovered. Its nobles took ship across the Broken Ocean and became the Dukes of the Whispering Coast. **The faithful say the coast exists because Rook laughed.**
- **Trickery is the poor man's weapon.** Every system is rigged; the Coin teaches you to rig it back. Forgery, a loaded die, a creditor talked out of his own ledger — these are sacraments, small redistributions of a world that cheats.
- **Rook still laughs.** Devotees hear laughter at their best moments, when the con lands. They take it for the Grinning Prophet laughing at authority alongside them.
- **"Never believe your own coin."** Fool anyone you like, but never yourself. Its anti-parable is **Marten**, the commoner who believed his own claim to be king and was crucified at the crossroads for it (see [[Reference - Name Tables]]). The faithful quote it constantly.
- **The great quarrel.** Rook against Aurelius the Architect — the forger against the law. The [[The Cult of the Zenith|Zenith]]'s *Meditations* were the old empire's law before they were the coast's, so this is the same war Rook started, still fought in wax and ink.

## 🎲 Practice
- **The Crooked Tally.** A festival on which debts are "forgiven" if the creditor can be tricked out of the ledger. Most years it frees no one. Some years it frees a bondsman, and the Docks talk of nothing else.
- **Coin-in-the-Mud.** Coins thrown into the Docks mud; the children keep what they find.
- **The loaded die.** Every devotee carries one and never uses it at the table. It is a reminder that the world is loaded too.
- **Confession by joke.** A devotee who has been caught tells it as a story against themselves in a tavern, and the room laughs. That is the whole of the Coin's absolution.

---

> **GM-facing — what the Coin really is.**
> **The Two-Faced.** The Domain of Trickery is the remains of the Creator of **reflection**. In the Sphere it showed every being itself. When individuation came, mortals hated what it showed them; in the Naming they called it the Liar, and the Naming made it so. With an ego of its own, it could not bear what it saw in *itself* either, so it lied — to others first, and then to itself, which is the lie that matters. What it refused to see split away and kept the old function: the truth, delivered only as mockery. **Two faces bound like a coin:** the **Mask**, which lies, flatters and promises; and the **Mocking Voice**, which tells the truth and laughs while it does it. In the War of Creation it fought on **both sides** (see [[Cosmology - The Celestial Graveyard and The war of Creation]]).
> **What the current does.** It does not help you fool other people. It makes **the lie you tell yourself** briefly true — the die lands, the creditor forgets, the guard looks away, because you believed hard enough. Then the bill comes due as **disappointment**: the luck turns, and it always collects. Nobody swindles their way past the pain; they only defer it.
> **The laughter** the faithful hear is the Mocking Voice, and it is not laughing at authority. It is laughing at them, and it is telling them the truth: *you are lying to yourself; you want to be a prophet.*
> **Rook's Folly** was Rook's own. He believed he was beating the empire; the current was using him, and he was its greatest dupe. The faithful have been grinning at the wrong joke for centuries — and their first teaching, *never believe your own coin*, is the one true thing the cult says.
> **The mote.** Disappointment is also the way out. A devotee who finally sees through their own lie is free of the current — and for the first time sees clearly what everyone else is lying about. The coast has a handful of these people. They make very good investigators and very poor company.

## 💰 Logistics & Economics (The Realism Anchor)
- **Income:** none as a body. The Coin owns nothing and collects nothing. Its wealth is whatever its faithful happen to be carrying.
- **Expenses:** none — which is its strength. There is no house to seize and no treasury to forge.
- **Assets:** the Cobalt Feather's craft (see the Blue Pens), and the Struck City.

---

## ⚖️ Leverage & Power Dynamics
- **The Zenith's oldest enemy.** The Cobalt Feather manufactures legitimacy and the Zenith authenticates it. When the Zenith struck the Fourth from the Register, it handed the remains of Rook's empire to Rook's own faithful. Rook's Folly acquired a capital, and Aurelius signed the deed.
- **The Cobalt Feather** is the Coin's sharpest blade in Port Nevarellon. Not every Feather is devout, but the Blue Pens nearly all are.
- **The Struck City.** There the Coin governs beside the city's ruler. It is a cult of thieves running a city that has no paper existence (see [[Reference - The Wondrous Markets]]).
- **Vulnerabilities:** No structure, so no defence; a devotee caught is a devotee alone. And the current collects — sooner or later every lucky streak in the Docks ends badly.

---

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 3** in Port Nevarellon; **Layer 1** in the Struck City.
- **Chokepoint:** trust — the forged instrument and the believed lie.
- **Enforcement:** none. A cult with no house has nothing to enforce with, only stories told against yourself in taverns.
- **Geographic anchor:** none in the city, deliberately. Abroad, the Struck City.
- **Bypass:** the Zenith's Register; disillusionment.
- **Cost & Mote:** The cost is the bill the current always presents. The mote is Coin-in-the-Mud — and the rare devotee who hears the laughter for what it is and walks out clear-eyed.

## 🎲 Rules Flags (for when the ruleset is integrated)
*Not carried over. These need checking against the Iron & Marrow ruleset.*
- **"Localized distortions of luck"** (the Religion note): how a Crooked Coin prayer bends a roll or an outcome.
- **The deferred cost:** luck borrowed is repaid as a later reversal. Is that a tracked debt, a GM tool, or narrative only?
- **The Mocking Voice:** can a character recognise the laughter for what it is, and with what check?
- **Disillusionment:** a devotee freed of the current — a state with mechanical weight (seeing through deception) or purely narrative?
- **The Zenith's Binding Oath:** does an oath sworn over the Register hold against someone the Two-Faced is helping to lie to themselves?
