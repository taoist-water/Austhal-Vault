# Faction: The Cult of the Rime-Fang
#faction/legal #lore/religion #status/draft

> "Eat what you kill and you'll live the winter. Give it away and someone else will too. Kaelen did the first so you'd never have to." — a Fang-mother at Sleetfall

> "They took us in at the Notch. Dried us, fed us, never asked a name. The keeper didn't smile once." — a carter on the Long Graft

---

## 🏛️ Overview
- **Domain:** Winter & Wilds (see [[Religion - The Pagan Pantheon and the Faith Domains]]).
- **Paragon:** **Kaelen the Survivor.**
- **Type:** A hill faith with no temple and no priesthood. The rites are kept by the **Fang-mothers**, the eldest women of each household, as Kaelen kept them for hers. There is no head of the faith above the hearth.
- **Where it is kept:**
  - **The Stonereach passes** — Sleetfall and the snowbound villages, and the shrine at **Pilgrim's Notch** on the Long Graft (see [[Duchy of Stonereach]]).
  - **The upper Sisters**, among De Vonce's miners, who are snowed in through Deepcold (see [[Duchy of De Vonce]]).
  - **The Scar-Holders.** Some took it up in the ash, where civilisation has already failed. The Quiet Steading keeps Bare-Teeth Night (see [[Duchy of Corvus]]).
  - **In the city, almost nowhere.** It is the faith of people the city never sees.
- **Trappings:** heavy white wolf pelts, frosted breath. Prayers manifest as a freezing, howling wind and ice.

---

## 🐺 What the Faithful Believe
- **Kaelen** was a matriarch from the deepest winters of the north. She hunted a **winter-drake** with an iron spear and her bare teeth, and lived. Then she outlived the winter, her clan and her children. *The Survivor* is not a compliment, and the Fang-mothers do not say it like one.
- **The rule of the hunt.** A hunter never eats her own kill. The Fang-mothers say Kaelen ate hers, and gave the rule so that nobody would have to be the Survivor again.
- **The cold is not cruel.** It is only there. The Wilds do not hate you and will not spare you, so you survive them together or not at all.
- **Where Kaelen came from:** "the north", over the Spine, the Fang-mothers say. Nobody on the coast has crossed it to check.

## ❄️ Practice
- **The Rime Hunt** — the Winter Moons hunt. The kill is given away — to the snowed-in households, the widows, the Notch's larder — and never eaten by the hunter.
- **Bare-Teeth Night** — the coming of age: one night outdoors in the Winter Moons with nothing. The Fang-mother waits at the door until dawn. Nearly all come back.
- **Pilgrim's Notch** — the shrine on the Long Graft, kept by the faithful in turns. Free shelter, no toll and no questions, for anyone: dwarf, carter, deserter. The lintel over its door is a jaw-bone too big for any beast alive, and the faithful say it is the drake's.
- **The Sleet Vigil** — the northern coast's folk custom of keeping a light burning for anyone still on the road. It is not a Rime-Fang rite, but the Fang-mothers say it was theirs first. Stonereach's storm-right grew from the same ground.

---

> **GM-facing — what answers.**
> **The Unmoving.** The Domain of Winter & Wilds is the remains of the Creator of **stillness and constancy**: in the Sphere it kept each thing the same through the turning of the cycle. In the War of Creation it was the purest of the Purge — it wanted the Sphere frozen as it had been, and every mortal stopped where they stood (see [[Cosmology - The Celestial Graveyard and The war of Creation]]).
> **The winter-drake** was its weapon, made to make the north stand still. Kaelen killed it — that part is true. Then, starving in the deep winter, she **ate it**, and the drake's flesh was unanchored wrack (see [[Cosmology - The Great Fracture]]).
> **What it did to her.** It slowed her. She stopped needing to eat, stopped feeling the cold, and her wounds closed over like ice. Over the years she stopped feeling anything for anyone. She outlived her clan without grieving it. The rule of the hunt is hers, made when she understood what had happened to her. It was the only thing she ever gave them on purpose.
> **What the current does now** is a little of what the drake did to Kaelen. A devotee in the cold slows: hunger waits, the cold bites less, and a wound frosts over and holds. It does not lengthen life — that is the Mother's trade, not this one (see [[The Cult of the Weeping Martyr]]).
> **The cost:** it slows their warmth too — their feeling for other people. An old Fang-mother is calm in a way that frightens her grandchildren. The faithful call it hardness. It is the Unmoving.
> **The mote: the rites outlast the feeling.** The Fang-mothers give the kill away, keep the Notch open and wait at the door on Bare-Teeth Night long after they have stopped feeling why. The carter they dried out at the Notch does not know the difference. Kaelen built a faith that does the right thing when its faithful can no longer feel it.
> **Kaelen's people.** The Rime-Fang is the one faith the settlers found on the coast rather than brought. Kaelen's people lived in the high Spine long before the settlement; the old families of Sleetfall descend from them, much married into since.
> **The jaw at the Notch** is war-bone, anchored and inert. It is only bone now, and the cold at the Notch is only the mountain.
> **The Tidespoken** would call the Unmoving the refusal of the tide if they knew of it (see [[The Tidespoken Clergy]]). They do not go into the passes.

## 💰 Logistics & Economics (The Realism Anchor)
- **Income:** none as a body.
- **Expenses:** the Notch's fire and larder, kept by the Rime Hunt's kills and the firewood carters leave behind them.
- **Assets:** Pilgrim's Notch. It stands on Stonereach ground, and no Duke of Stonereach has ever tolled it.

---

## ⚖️ Leverage & Power Dynamics
- **The passes owe it.** Everyone who has crossed the Long Graft in winter has slept at the Notch or knows someone who did — Ubaraz's carters included.
- **The Iron Horizon** keeps the same keeps: the soldiers pray to Senecus and their mothers pray to Kaelen (see [[The Cult of the Iron Horizon]]).
- **House Scutarius** lets it be. The Notch saves the Duke's road its dead, and costs him nothing.
- **Vulnerabilities:** no coin, no clergy and no voice in the city. And the Fang-mothers' hardness: the young go down to the coast and do not come back.

---

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 3** in the villages, with one foothold on **Layer 2**: the Notch, the only free roof on the Long Graft.
- **Chokepoint:** shelter in winter.
- **Enforcement:** the hearth; the Fang-mothers; the cold itself.
- **Geographic anchor:** Pilgrim's Notch; Sleetfall.
- **Bypass:** the toll-keeps' rooms, for those who can pay; dying on the road.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is the Fang-mothers' warmth, slowed out of them a winter at a time.
  - **The mote** is the Notch's open door, and a faith that keeps giving the kill away.

## 🎲 Rules Flags (for when the ruleset is integrated)
*Not carried over.*
- **The slowing:** resistance to cold, hunger and exposure for a devotee who invokes Kaelen; wounds that frost over and hold.
- **The cost:** the loss of warmth — an emotional or social cost over time. Is it tracked, a GM call, or narrative only?
- **Ice and wind** as prayer manifestations.
- **Faith-casting** under the Religion note's *Faith protects the mind; invoke the saint's deed* rule.
- **Eating unanchored wrack:** what it does to anyone else fool or desperate enough to try.
