# Faction: House Valerius
#faction/legal #lore/politics #status/draft

> "My father wore the coronet thirty years. The Table took it back in an afternoon, by a show of shares. He still sits at the Table. He still votes against me. That is what a house is, here." — Duke Junia Valerius

> "Every other duke on this coast is a man with a sword. Ours is a woman with a ledger, and she can be dismissed. Tell me which of them you'd rather owe." — a Trade Plazas banker

---

## 🏛️ Overview
- **Type:** A ducal house run as a syndicate. The coast calls it a syndicate with a coronet, and the house takes it as a compliment.
- **Headquarters:** [[Saltmere]], the seat of the [[Duchy of Valerius]].
- **Current Leader:** **Duke Junia Valerius**, chosen by the Table in 47 A.A.
- **Primary Influence:** coast-wide, through credit. The house's bond-houses hold paper on half the Iron Court, on House Hordeus, and on a good part of [[Port Nevarellon]]. One of the banking houses on the Trade Plazas is a Valerius house in all but name (see [[The Trade Plazas]]).

---

## 🪙 The Shares & the Table
- **The four books.** The house holds the duchy as four books of shares: **the Pans**, **the Vines**, **the Fleet** and **the Bonds**.
  - Every adult of the blood holds shares, inherited through either parent.
  - Shares may be sold only to another of the blood, and only with the Table's leave.
  - A spouse who marries in holds none. A child of the blood who marries out keeps theirs, and so do their children.
- **The Table.** The shareholders sit once a year, in the week of Salt-Draw, at Saltmere. They vote by share, not by head. They hear the year's yield, declare the dividend, and decide who wears the coronet.
- **The coronet is an office.** The Table chooses the Duke, and by two-thirds of the shares it can take the coronet back. The house uses the title *Duke* whoever holds it, because it is an office, not a person.
- **It is all lodged.** Every share, every bond and every appointment is lodged with the Council and counter-copied in the Register (see [[The Cult of the Zenith]]). To Valerius, a thing not lodged did not happen.

**Who holds Valerius shares.** Because shares follow the blood out of the house, Valerius owns a piece of its neighbours:
- **[[Duchy of De Vonce#Eldrick De Vonce, the heir|Eldrick De Vonce]]** holds his mother Cassia's shares. He votes them by proxy, draws the dividend, and hates both.
- **Faustina's children** with Lucan Hordeus hold shares through her — the next heirs of Aerthos (see [[Duchy of Aerthos]]).

## 👥 Notable Members
- **Duke Junia Valerius** — 52. She ran the Bonds book for fifteen years before the Table gave her the coronet. Cold, exact, and fair by the ledger, which is the only fairness she recognises. She has no children; the coronet is not inherited, so she has never needed any. She is the only duke on the coast who can be dismissed, and the only one who behaves like it.
- **Gratian Valerius** — 79. Junia's father, Cassia De Vonce's brother, and Duke from 17 A.A. until the Table took the coronet back in 47 A.A.
  - **Why he lost it.** After his sister died he lent heavily to the De Vonce knights, out of family feeling, and the loans went bad.
  - **Now** he sits at the Table on his own shares and votes against his daughter on every motion, and he has a faction.
- **Faustina Valerius** — 39. Wife of [[Duchy of Aerthos#🏰 House Hordeus|Lucan Hordeus]], heir of Aerthos, and a shareholder in the Bonds book: the book that holds the Hordeus bonds. She lives at Millhaven, and she reads her husband's letters before he sends them.
- **[[Bond-Master Tallis Gorrius]]** — not of the blood. He runs the Brinewick pans for the Bonds book, and runs them well.
- **The Envoy** — a Golden Company Envoy, retained at Saltmere as a standing client (see [[The Golden Company]]).

## 💰 Logistics & Economics (The Realism Anchor)
- **Sources of Income:**
  - The Pans: salt.
  - The Vines: wine.
  - The Fleet: coastal shipping and cured fish.
  - The Bonds: credit and Debt-Bonds, and the interest on everybody's.
- **Expenses & Upkeep:** the pan-workers' board, charged back against their wages; the fleet; the Envoy; Palefast; the dividend.
- **Primary Assets:** the pans at Saltmere and Brinewick; the galleys on the Meridian; and the paper — a strongroom at Saltmere full of other people's obligations.

## ⚖️ Leverage & Power Dynamics
- **Political Leverage:** everyone owes it money. The four standing Duchies need the Council's market, and the Council needs Valerius salt and credit.
- **Monopoly / Unique Value:** lawful salt on the coast, and the only duchy with an exit by sea.
- **Vulnerabilities:**
  - **It is owed more than the coast can pay.** If the Iron Court or Aerthos defaults, the house must foreclose on knights and dukes' sons, and it cannot.
  - The Table can remove its Duke.
  - **Every lodged appointment is an instrument.** A cousin who lost at the Table can petition the Court of Nullity to void the Duke's appointment. The Court can never confirm her (see [[The Cult of the Zenith]]). Gratian has thought about it.

> **GM-facing.** Junia's crisis is coming at the next Salt-Draw. Gratian's faction will move to remove her unless she calls in the Hordeus bonds. Calling them in would force Aerthos to sell Grainlock, and Grainlock has already been sold down to service those same bonds (see [[Duchy of Aerthos]]). The Bonds book is lending against grain that is not there. Junia half-suspects. Faustina knows.

> **GM hook — losses at sea.** Every year the Fleet book records one galley lost in the Winter Moons. It is never lost. It sails east past the Shield Atolls with salt for the Fourth, the Struck City, and comes back with whatever the Fourth pays in (see [[Reference - The Wondrous Markets]]). The trade is uninsurable and unlodged. It is the one thing the house does not write down, which tells you what it is worth to them.

## ⚡ Internal Friction & Conflict
- **Internal Factions:** Junia's majority against Gratian's faction, at every Table.
- **External Rivals:** the free-salt smugglers; the wreckers at Coldspar; the Wyvern Tail and Grey Water crews on the Meridian; and anyone with an Arbiter willing to read a bond closely.
- **Public Perception:** trusted by the Council, envied by the other Duchies, and hated in the pans. In the High Quarter they are the only dukes anyone invites to dinner twice.

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 1** — the house is the Ducal power of Valerius.
- **Chokepoint:** credit and salt.
- **Enforcement:** debt, lodged in the Register; and the Table, against its own.
- **Geographic anchor:** the strongroom at Saltmere.
- **Bypass:** default; the Court of Nullity; free salt.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is the pans, and everyone on a Valerius bond.
  - **The mote** is the Crock (see [[Bond-Master Tallis Gorrius]]). It is also the fact that a house this hard still answers to its own members: a Valerius Duke can be dismissed, and one was.
