# Faction: Council of Five
#faction/Legal #status/solid

> "They are afraid of producing a king. So they built a government where four people must agree before anything can happen, and then they were astonished when nothing did." — Master Cartographer Vaelen

## 🏛️ Overview
- **Type:** Merchant oligarchy / elected governing board of a sovereign Free City-State
- **Headquarters:** [[Port Nevarellon]] — the Council Chamber above [[The Trade Plazas]]
- **Current Leader:** **None, deliberately.** The **Chair** rotates annually and is drawn *by lot* on First Keel. The Chair sets the order of business and has **no casting vote**. The lot is drawn in public because the alternative is a permanent head of state, and the merchants who wrote the Accord had just paid a great deal of money to remove one
- **Primary Influence:** City-wide, plus the northern estuaries of the [[Silted Marshes]] (nominal)

## 🗳️ The Franchise — Who Actually Elects Them
The Council is genuinely elected, and the election is genuinely a sham, and both facts live in the same clause: **the franchise is the deed.**

A **Landed** citizen — anyone holding registered title under [[Law - The Council's Edicts]] — holds one vote per seat. An **Un-Landed** citizen holds none. There is no property threshold beyond the deed itself and no requirement of wealth, birth, or guild membership. The system is, on its own terms, open: buy a deed, get a vote.

A deed costs more than an unskilled laborer earns in a lifetime of days they will not live to work.

This is why Port Nevarellon can call itself a free city without anyone lying. The gate is unlocked and priced out of reach, exactly like the toll into [[The High Quarter]]. It also means the **Register of the Landed** — the roll of who holds title — *is* the electoral roll, which makes the councillor who administers it the most quietly dangerous person in the city. See [[Lucia Marrenhal]].

## 💰 Logistics & Economics (The Realism Anchor)
- **Sources of Income:** Basin cargo tariffs; the Vagrancy Tolls; the Guild-Tax; a mandatory cut of every **Golden Writ** registered; harbour and keel registration fees under the Reckoning of Keels; the Lastwater water price
- **Expenses & Upkeep:** The annual **Contract Sum** to [[The Golden Company]] — the single largest line by a wide margin and effectively non-negotiable; the [[Faction - The Civic Constabulary (The Coppers)|Blue-Cloak Watch]] as a cheap auxiliary; maintenance of the Sea-Wall, the Tide-Wards, the limestone curtain wall and the cisterns; the [[Tythius De Vonce|Spine Aqueduct]] payments to De Vonce
- **Primary Assets:** The Sovereign's Gate; the cisterns and the aqueduct's city terminus; the Register of the Landed; and the 99-Year Contract itself — an instrument with **41 years left to run**

## ⚖️ Leverage & Power Dynamics
- **Political Leverage:** The only body that can issue title, and therefore the only body that can make a person legally a person
- **Monopoly / Unique Value:** The 99-Year Contract with the Golden Company. Complete control of the port city — the gateway for all commerce across the Whispering Coast and beyond. Port Nevarellon is known internationally as one of the **3 Wondrous Markets**
- **Vulnerabilities:**
  - Losing safe trade routes weakens the revenue base, risks a missed Contract Sum, and costs them the Company's protection — the failure mode the whole structure is built around
  - **The Contract expires in 41 years and there is no succession plan.** Only [[Marcian Thole]] treats this as urgent
  - **The Unwritten Day.** The 365th day belongs to no turn, and no instrument specifying a turn can fall due on it. Four attempts to close it have failed
  - **The Tuwal Ghorun identity void.** Southern instruments name a polity no coastal register lists. It cannot be resolved, and one councillor is actively ensuring it never is

## 🔒 The Four-of-Five Rule — Why Nothing Ever Changes
Routine business passes on a simple majority. But any **Instrument of the Whole** — a new Edict, an alteration to the Contract, a treaty, a change to the Register, or the recognition of any territorial claim — requires **four of five**.

It was written into the Accord by five merchant families who had just watched a king abuse unchecked power and who trusted each other only marginally more than they had trusted him. The purpose was to make it impossible for any three houses to combine and rule the other two.

**The consequence is that any two councillors can block anything that matters, indefinitely, without ever making an argument.** This is not a flaw the Council is working around. It is the load-bearing reason the Vagrancy Tolls have never been repealed, the curtain wall at [[The Foundry Slips]] has never been surveyed, the Scar-Holders have never been recognised, and the Unwritten Day is still standing after four attempts by the most capable administrator in the city.

Fifty-eight years of deadlock is not a failure of these five people. It is the machine working exactly as designed by people who feared tyranny more than they feared paralysis.

## ⚡ Internal Friction & Conflict
- **Internal Factions:** No stable blocs — the four-of-five rule makes permanent alliances useless and shifting ones essential. The durable oppositions are **Marrenhal vs. Kress** on the Unwritten Day (nineteen years, immaculately courteous, entirely unresolved) and **Sallow vs. Vantry** over the Long Seat, which Vantry blocked and has never explained
- **External Rivals:** [[The Five Duchies of the Whispering Coast]] (particularly De Vonce, via the aqueduct); [[The Iron-Anchor Syndicate]]; [[The Cobalt Feather Syndicate]]; the Cult of the Zenith, whose Court of Nullity can void a Council instrument and can never validate one
- **Public Perception:** A necessary evil to the Landed, an occupying government to the Un-Landed, and *shopkeepers playing with stolen crowns* to the Duchies. The Toll Amnesty is the only day of the year the Council is cheered, and it is cheering for something the Council could not legally prevent

## 👥 Notable Members
- [[Marcian Thole]] — *The Harbour Seat.* Tariffs, keel registration, harbour defence. Human, 58. Deaf shipwright, four generations of hulls, quietly drilling four hundred people he does not call a militia
- [[Lucia Marrenhal]] — *The Writ Seat.* The Register of the Landed, deeds, Golden Writs, the franchise. Human, 44. Precise rather than cruel, going blind, and the only person who can see every crack at once
- [[Ottavian Kress]] — *The Water Seat.* Cisterns, Lastwater, the Spine Aqueduct. Halfling, 51. The Docks' voice, sold by the hour, and delivered more often than anyone else offers
- [[Verrine Sallow]] — *The Contract Seat.* The Golden Company, the Contract Sum, defence. Human, 39. Feeds twelve hundred halberds; the only councillor who has read the Contract to the end
- [[Palla Vantry]] — *The Long Seat.* Foreign trade, the sea-lanes, the south. Elf, 214. Elected continuously for eighty years, has claimed nothing, and cannot be confirmed or removed by any court that exists

## 🪑 The Sixth Chair That Isn't
**No dwarf has ever held a seat**, despite [[Ubaraz Kingdom]] being among the city's oldest and largest trading partners and despite dwarven structural engineering holding up half of Stonereach. The reason is procedural rather than stated: the franchise requires a deed registered in Port Nevarellon, and Ubaraz houses trade through factors rather than holding city title. It is a live and growing grievance, it has never been raised as an Instrument of the Whole, and under the four-of-five rule it never will be.
