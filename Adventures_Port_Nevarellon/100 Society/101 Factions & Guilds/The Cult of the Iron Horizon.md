# Faction: The Cult of the Iron Horizon
#faction/legal #lore/religion #status/draft

> "Hold the gate. That's the whole of the maxim. Nobody ever tells you what the gate's for." — [[Castellan Senna Cade]]

> "A soldier who doubts is already half-routed. The Saint does not ask you to win. He asks you to stay." — first maxim of Maxim Night

---

## 🏛️ Overview
- **Domain:** Strategy (see [[Religion - The Pagan Pantheon and the Faith Domains]]).
- **Paragon:** **Saint Senecus the Unyielding.**
- **Type:** A soldiers' faith with no temple and no priesthood. Every garrison has a **Maxim-Keeper**, usually the oldest sergeant, who leads the rites. There is no head of the faith above the garrison.
- **Where it is kept:**
  - **The De Vonce officers and the Iron Court** — every knight keeps the Held Pass vigil before his Blooding (see [[Duchy of De Vonce]]).
  - **The border levy** at Adder's Gate.
  - **Every keep in Stonereach**, each of which claims Senecus's pass as its own (see [[Duchy of Stonereach]]).
  - **Families who give soldiers.** In the city, the Senecus devotional names — *Senna*, *Senec*, *Senric* — mark them. [[High Arbiter Sevrin Kalder|Kalder]]'s parents kept the faith and expected a soldier.
- **Trappings:** polished iron shields, clean-cut discipline. Prayers are short, practical tactical maxims.

---

## ⚔️ What the Faithful Believe
- **Saint Senecus** was a military philosopher who held a doomed mountain pass against a horde of monstrous races, and died holding it. The pass did not fall while he lived, and that was the whole victory.
- **The Horizon** is the line you are given to hold. Victory is not survival. It is staying at your post, whatever the odds, until relieved or dead.
- **Doubt is the enemy's first weapon.** The faith's prayers do not ask for courage. They ask for certainty, and they get it.
- **Where the pass was.** Every keep in the Stonereach passes says Senecus died in its own pass. The old maxim-books say the pass was "at the edge of the world", which settles nothing.

---

## 🛡️ Practice
- **The Held Pass** — an overnight vigil for Saint Senecus, with no fire and no speech. Every knight of the Iron Court keeps one before his Blooding, and so does every Maxim-Keeper before taking the office.
- **Maxim Night** — the recitation of the tactical maxims, used as officer initiation. A new officer recites the maxims he has learned and receives one more, his own, from the Maxim-Keeper. He never repeats it to anyone.
- **The Relief** — when a soldier dies at a post, the next soldier to stand it says the dead one's maxim aloud, once, if anyone knows it.
- **Hallam Drey** of the Seven kept the faith (see [[Duchy of Corvus]]). The border levy says he went down into Corvus Spire because someone had to hold the stair.

> **GM-facing — what answers.**
> **The Bastion.** The Domain of Strategy is the remains of the Creator of **thresholds and limits**: in the Sphere it held the shape of things, where one thing stopped and the next began. The Naming gave it a general's face, and it took the face seriously. In the War of Creation it fought for the Purge, and it **designed many of the living weapons** — the ancestors of the monstrous races (see [[Cosmology - The Celestial Graveyard and The war of Creation]]).
> **The irony** is that the Iron Horizon prays to the remains of the maker of the very horde Senecus died holding against.
> **What the current does.** It removes doubt. A devotee who calls on Senecus is steadier, clearer and harder to break. **The cost is that they cannot break even when they should.** The current does not distinguish a post worth holding from one that should be abandoned. Iron Horizon garrisons die at positions any sane commander would have pulled back from, and the faithful call it glory.
> **Senecus** was real, and older than the coast. His pass was on the old empire's frontier, across the Broken Ocean, and the faith came over with the settlers (see [[History - The Broken Crown of Austhal]]). Every Stonereach keep is wrong.
> **The mote** is that sometimes the post *is* worth holding: a ford with the last clean water before the ash, or a stair down to a door that must not open. Senna Cade holds the first. Hallam Drey held the second.

## 💰 Logistics & Economics (The Realism Anchor)
- **Income:** none as a body. Garrisons keep their own rites.
- **Expenses:** the vigil-shield every new Maxim-Keeper is given, paid for by the garrison.
- **Assets:** the maxim-books, kept by each garrison and never copied out.

---

## ⚖️ Leverage & Power Dynamics
- **De Vonce's officer corps** is the faith's backbone. The Duke does not keep it himself, but he has never promoted a knight who did not.
- **The Golden Company** has its own discipline and no use for the Saint, so the Horizon is the faith of the coast's *own* soldiers. That matters to the levies more than they would say.
- **The Zenith** puts up with it. Aurelius orders the world; Senecus only holds a line in it.
- **Vulnerabilities:** a faith that cannot retreat. An Iron Horizon commander is predictable, and an enemy who knows it can make him die where it chooses.

---

## 🧱 Layer & the Five Fields
**Layer 3**, carried by Layer 1's soldiers.
- **Chokepoint:** certainty under orders.
- **Enforcement:** the garrison; shame; the Relief.
- **Geographic anchor:** none — the faith is wherever a post is held. Adder's Gate and the Stonereach keeps come closest.
- **Bypass:** desertion; the Golden Company's pay.
- **Cost & Mote:**
  - **The cost** is the garrisons that should have pulled back.
  - **The mote** is the ones that held what was worth holding.

## 🎲 Rules Flags (for when the ruleset is integrated)
*Not carried over.*
- **The removed doubt:** resistance to fear, rout or morale effects for a devotee who invokes Senecus.
- **The cost:** an inability to retreat or disengage under the current. Is it a condition, a GM call, or narrative only?
- **Faith-casting** under the Religion note's *Faith protects the mind; invoke the saint's deed* rule.
