# Lore: Lycanthropes

> "The howls stopped an hour before it found us. That's when Pike said we should've kept moving." — a Longbarrow ploughman

> **GM reference.** Ground truth, except where marked as what people say. What the coast actually knows is the howling, the bites, the silver, and what Longbarrow will not talk about.

---

## 🐺 What people say
- **The barrow-wolves.** Longbarrow, among the war-fields of [[Duchy of De Vonce|De Vonce]], has had them for longer than anyone can count back: neighbours who change once a turn, on the night the Pale Sister turns, and hunt the war-fields below the old barrows until dawn.
- **A bite passes it.** Not every bite, but enough. Longbarrow does not talk about what it does with the bitten.
- **Silver bites them,** the hunters say, because silver is the Pale Sister's metal. Cold iron does nothing, and a hunter learned that the hard way.
- **At the Low Moons** they change every night, and do not change back until the moons have risen.
- **Between turns, a barrow-wolf is a neighbour,** and remembers everything.

---

## 🩸 What they are
> **GM-facing — the first, and the price.**
> **The first** was a Longbarrow man, generations before the Accord. He went into the drowned margin of the Iron Wood, below the Sisters, and asked a **Briar-Hag** to make him something that would never be prey again (see [[Lore - Hags]]). She agreed; a hag always agrees. The price he chose was **something not yet his**.
> **What was not yet his** was everyone he would ever wound. The change copies itself into whatever it bites, and that is the price still being collected, long after the hag uprooted and the man died. **The curse is not a hag's work. It is the bill for one.**
> **Why cold iron does nothing:** only the first was hag-worked, and a little fey for it. The rest were never touched by a hag; they are only paying. Silver works on all of them, and why is still open.
> **What the change takes.** The first change took the first man's reason, and every change after takes the same: what hunts on the turning night has no Wits and no Will. The person comes back in the morning, and remembers. The Bestiary's **Barrow-Fang** is one of them, on its turning night.
> **Not everyone Longbarrow calls bitten was.** Some of them are the bread (see [[Duchy of De Vonce]], the war-bone).

---

> **The Mote:** Longbarrow's families lock their own in the root-cellar on the turning night and sit up with them, on the other side of the door, until dawn. Most nights that is enough.
