SEAMS FOUND WHILE DRAWING

  1. The Millhaven spur leaves the Road too late. The Lay of the Coast sketch runs the spur west from Castle Iron-Spire. But Iron-Spire is 3–4 days out and Millhaven only 2–3, and the Aerthos note has the spur running west along the river as a towpath. The spur has to leave the Road just outside the city, so that is how it is drawn here.
  2. Valerius cannot quite reach the Scar's mouth. The Valerius note says the mouth touches Valerius's inland edge. The Five Duchies note says the Road skirts the mouth on its seaward side and runs through De Vonce the whole way. Both can't be true at the mouth itself. Drawn: a strip of De Vonce carries the Road between the two. The simplest fix is that Valerius meets the valley's northern lip, above where the Road climbs into Thraw's Pass. Its bond-gangs cutting Blackcopse don't need a shared border.
  3. Three rivers have no stated course. The Adderwater is a legal border and has a ford at Bittern's Ford, but no note says where it runs to. The Blackrun and the Colding only say where they start. Here the Adderwater joins the Colding to the sea, and the Blackrun drains into Lake Ashen, which is my choice.

HOW PLACES WERE PUT DOWN

  • Stated: the note gives a direction from a named place, such as "below Adder's Gate", "north of Brinewick" or "at the mouth of the marshes". Placed exactly as written.
  • From travel time: the note gives days by a named mode. Distance is taken from the distances note's paces: horse 25–35 miles a day, coastal galley 50–80, open sail 100–150, marsh skiff 5–10 straight-line.
  • Inferred: the vault gives only the duchy and what the place does. I placed it where its trade or role makes sense: smelters under the Sisters, the toll station on the river, fords on the water. These are the ones to move freely.

Nothing on this chart is written back into the vault. If you want any placement made canon, or a seam fixed in a note, I'll bring it to you as a diff first.
