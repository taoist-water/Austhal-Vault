# Iron & Marrow — Three Novice Player Characters

All three built using *The Marrow*'s Step 1–5 chargen (4 DP Attributes / 8 DP Skills / 2 Feats / Armor + Weapons), cross-checked against Iron Core, Hardware, Metal Meet Flesh, and Manipulating the Void for legality and math.

---

## 1. Krivva "Two-Coin" Skarn — Half-Orc Female, Faith Caster (Trickery Domain)

*"The gods don't love you. They just haven't decided you're worth the effort of hating yet. Might as well roll the dice."*

### Vital Statistics
- **Species:** Half-Orc
- **Size:** Standard | **Move:** 30 ft / 6 squares
- **Attributes:** Prowess 0 | Reflex 1 | Wits 0 | Will 3
- **Skills:** Faith 3 | Influence 2 | Stealth 1 | Thievery 1 | Acrobatics 1
- **Wound Threshold:** 5 *(4 base + 0 Prowess + 1 Leather Armor)*
- **Stress Limit:** 7 *(4 base + 0 Wits + 3 Will)*
- **Wound Slots:** 3 | **Momentum Bank:** 5 (max)
- **Inventory Slots:** 8 *(8 + 0 Prowess)*

### Species Traits (Half-Orc)
- **Blood Frenzy:** Suffering a Wound immediately clears 1 Dissonant Stress.
- **Menacing:** Advantage on Will + Influence checks intimidating anyone smaller/weaker.
- **Outcast (Drawback):** Disadvantage on social checks with civilized strangers who don't know her.

### Feats
- **Divine Conduit — The Covenant, Domain of Trickery.** Grants a Holy Symbol, the **Fickle Fate** Domain Tag (an ally rolls a Fumble → spend 1 Stress as a Reaction to downgrade it to a standard failure), and the 3 Novice Miracles below.
- **Battlefield Orator** *(Influence +2)*: Action in combat — choose one: an ally clears 1d6 Stress, or an engaged enemy takes -2 to their next Defense roll.

### Equipment
- **Armor:** Leather (+1 Armor, Light)
- **Weapons/Shield (two 1H items):** Dagger (Power 0, Finesse, Concealable, Thrown, Sidearm) + Buckler (2 SV, **Nimble** — rolls Reflex instead of Prowess on Block, which matters since her Prowess is 0)
- **Holy Symbol** (granted by Divine Conduit)

### Miracles (Tithe of Will = 2d6 + Will + Faith = **2d6+6**, vs. TN 8 Novice)
- **Beginner's Luck** — 1 Locked Stress, self/ally touch, Activation/Free Action. Pass: target gains Advantage on their next Clash (within 1 min). Fail: as Pass + 1 Encroachment. Snake Eyes: Advantage still applies, but the Stress converts to 1 Wound.
- **The Crooked Coin** — 1 Locked Stress, Free Reaction (triggers when an enemy targets Krivva or an ally within Short Range). Pass: the attacker suffers Disadvantage on that Clash. Fail: as Pass + 1 Encroachment. Snake Eyes: convert to 1 Wound, Encroachment resets to 0.
- **Fool's Errand** — 1 Locked Stress, Medium Range, Activation, Scene duration. Pass: sensory illusion; in combat, a 3x3 Heavily Obscured zone (enemies striking through it suffer Disadvantage; targets inside get +2 Defense). Fail/Snake Eyes as above.

### Table Note
With Will 3 + Faith 3 (+6 total), her minimum possible roll on a Novice Tithe of Will is 2+6=8 — she **cannot Fail** a Novice Miracle; she can only hit Clean Channel or a natural Snake Eyes (double 1s), which bypasses the modifier entirely. Encroachment is a non-issue for her right now, but that math tightens at Adept (TN 10) and Master (TN 12), so don't build the character around "Faith never fails" long-term.

### Combat Math Quick-Ref
Strike 2d6+0 (not her job) | Dodge 2d6+2 | Block 2d6+1 (Reflex 1 via the Buckler's Nimble tag, no Block skill invested — she'll lose this roll more often than not; the Buckler's SV 2 then subtracts from any Impact before it's compared to her Wound Threshold of 5, which is where the shield actually earns its keep) | Tithe of Will 2d6+6

## 2. Ilyrean Dusk — Elf Male, Arcana Caster (Demonology and Void Magic)

*"I didn't summon it. I just introduced it to a door that was already open."*

### Vital Statistics
- **Species:** Elf
- **Size:** Standard | **Move:** 30 ft / 6 squares
- **Attributes:** Prowess 0 | Reflex 1 | Wits 3 | Will 0
- **Skills:** Arcana 3 | Notice 2 | Insight 1 | Stealth 1 | Acrobatics 1
- **Wound Threshold:** 4 *(4 base + 0 Prowess + 1 Leather - 1 Hollow-Boned)*
- **Stress Limit:** 7 *(4 base + 3 Wits + 0 Will)*
- **Wound Slots:** 3 | **Momentum Bank:** 5 (max)
- **Inventory Slots:** 8 *(8 + 0 Prowess)*

### Species Traits (Elf)
- **Fey Reflexes:** Advantage on Reflex checks vs. hazards, traps, AoE.
- **Trance:** Full rest benefits from 4 hours' meditation.
- **Hollow-Boned (Drawback):** -1 Wound Threshold (already applied above).

### Feats
- **Arcane Awakening** *(Paradigm: Demonology and Void Magic)*. Grants a Grimoire with 3 Novice spells (below). Paradigm spells benefit from Mastery: a Messy Success resolves as Clean.
- **Tactical Mind** *(Wits 2, Notice 2)*: Automatically starts every combat encounter with 1 Momentum banked.

### Equipment
- **Armor:** Leather (+1 Armor, Light — no Arcana penalty)
- **Weapons (two 1H items):** Grimoire (Repository — spend 1 Momentum to cast a Novice spell without Locking Stress, first round only) + Dagger (Finesse — uses Reflex, his better stat, as backup melee)

### Grimoire (Wits + Arcana = **2d6+6**)
- **Fear** *(Demonology/Void — Paradigm, gets Mastery)* — Arcane Clash vs. target's Will + Resolve, Short Range, Aggressor. Margin 1–2: target takes 2 Dissonant Stress and must flee at max speed next Activation. Margin 3+: as above, and Fodder-tier targets Rout instead of just fleeing.
- **Bolt** *(Common)* — Arcane Clash vs. Defense, Medium Range, Spell Power 2. Margin 1–2: Impact = Margin+1. Margin 3+: as above.
- **Arcane Protection** *(Common)* — Unopposed vs. TN 8 to raise, Sustain (Lock 1 Stress). Margin 3–4: hostile spells targeting him suffer Disadvantage. Margin 5+: also SV 2 vs. the next hostile spell's Impact. As a Reactor, he may Block using Wits+Arcana instead of a physical stat.

### Combat Math Quick-Ref
Arcane Clash/Manifestation 2d6+6 | Dagger Strike (Finesse, Reflex) 2d6+1 | Dodge 2d6+2

## 3. Roland Kest — Human Male, Melee (Sword & Shield)

*"I don't need to be faster than the axe. I just need to be standing between it and you."*

### Vital Statistics
- **Species:** Human
- **Size:** Standard | **Move:** 30 ft / 6 squares
- **Attributes:** Prowess 3 | Reflex 1 | Wits 0 | Will 0
- **Skills:** Melee 3 | Block 3 | Athletics 2 | Acrobatics 1
- **Wound Threshold:** 9 *(4 base + 3 Prowess + 2 Chainmail)*
- **Stress Limit:** 5 *(4 base + 0 Wits + 0 Will + 1 Indomitable Spirit)*
- **Wound Slots:** 3 | **Momentum Bank:** 4 *(Steady, Not Sharp — capped, not the standard 5)*
- **Inventory Slots:** 11 *(8 + 3 Prowess)*

### Species Traits (Human)
- **Adaptable:** +1 extra Skill Point at creation (already spent above — 9 total skill DP instead of 8).
- **Indomitable Spirit:** +1 Stress Limit (already applied).
- **Steady, Not Sharp (Drawback):** Momentum Bank capped at 4.

### Feats
- **Iron Grip** *(Melee +1 or Athletics +1)*: If a Clash ties and the weapons bind, automatically bank 1 Momentum.
- **Quick** *(Reflex 1)*: Rolls Activation Order (2d6+Reflex) with Advantage (3d6, keep highest two).

### Equipment
- **Armor:** Chainmail/Scale (+2 Armor, Medium, **Bulky** — -1 to Athletics/Stealth/Arcana; his effective Athletics is 2 on paper but plays at 1 while armored — intentional tradeoff, not an error)
- **Weapons/Shield (two 1H items):** Longsword (Power 3 one-handed, Versatile — would jump to Power 4 two-handed but then he loses the shield) + Kite/Round Shield (4 SV, **Cover** — Advantage on defense vs. ranged attacks)

### Combat Math Quick-Ref
Strike 2d6+6, Impact = Margin+3 | **Block 2d6+6** (Prowess 3 + Block 3 — a real contest, not a lock). If he loses the Block Clash, subtract the Kite Shield's SV 4 from the Impact before comparing to his Wound Threshold of 9 — between the high Threshold and the shield's mitigation, most hits that get through still land as a Dissonant Stress "glancing hit" rather than a Wound. | Parry 2d6+4 | Activation Order 2d6+1 with Advantage (Quick)

### Forward Look
Roland already meets the prerequisites for three strong Tier 2 (Milestone-unlocked) feats the moment you have spare DP: **The Vanguard/Shield Brother** (Prowess 2, Melee 2, Block 1 — shove an ally aside and eat the hit yourself), **Attrition Engine** (Prowess+2, Block+2 or Melee+2 — bank Momentum on every successful Block/Parry), and **Riposte** (Melee 2 — win a Parry, hit back automatically). Worth flagging now so his first Milestone Reward has an obvious home.

## Design Notes & Flags

**1. Demonology and Void Magic is Novice-thin.** Cross-checking *Manipulating the Void* against the Arcane Awakening feat's requirement ("3 Novice spells... from the Common list and/or your chosen Paradigm's list"), the Demonology/Void paradigm currently has exactly **one** Novice-tier spell (*Fear*). Its two signature pieces — *Flay the Veil* and *Zone of Apathy* — are both Master-tier. Compare Witch Magic and Hedge Craft, which has two Novice options (*Confusion*, *The Evil Eye*). Mechanically this isn't broken — Ilyrean fills out fine on the Common list — but it means a Novice Demonologist reads as "1/3 paradigm flavor, 2/3 generic Arcanist" until they're deep enough into Advancement to afford Master spells. If Demonology is meant to feel distinct at the table from session one, it may need one or two more Novice/Adept-tier entries. Flagging for a call, not fixing unilaterally.

**2. Paradigm Mastery may not actually do anything for Clash-resolution spells — this affects Fear directly.** Arcane Awakening defines Mastery as "a Messy Success (Margin 0–2) resolves as a Clean Success instead," which is the language of the four-tier **Margin of Manifestation** ladder (Fail/Messy/Clean/Exceptional) used by unopposed utility spells like *Arcane Protection*. But *Fear* — Ilyrean's one actual in-Paradigm pick — resolves as an **Arcane Clash** with its own two-tier Margin Scaler ("Margin 1–2" / "Margin 3+ Clean"), which never uses the word "Messy" at all. Read strictly, Mastery has no defined target to upgrade on a Clash spell, meaning the one spell chosen specifically *for* being in-Paradigm gets zero benefit from the feat that's supposed to reward that choice. Recommend an explicit ruling: for Clash-resolution spells, Paradigm Mastery upgrades a Margin 1–2 result to the Margin 3+ (Clean) tier, treating the lower successful band as that spell's "Messy" equivalent. This is a sign-off item — it changes the actual power level of every Clash spell in every Paradigm, not just Demonology.

**3. Chargen Step 5 doesn't touch the currency economy.** *The Marrow* Step 5 says "choose 1 Armor" and "choose weapons/shields" with no gold budget, while *Hardware* prices everything in sp and *Soothing the Soul*'s downtime economy assumes characters have some. I picked mid-tier Common/Scarce gear (Leather, Chainmail, Longsword, Kite Shield — nothing above 45 sp) rather than reaching for Rare/Legendary items, on the assumption Novices get a standard starting kit for free and buy nothing at creation. If that's not the intent, these loadouts may need trimming or the chargen doc needs an explicit "starting gold: X sp" line. Left Belt/Backpack consumable slots (rope, rations, torches) blank for your call on how that's handled.

**4. Feat tier discipline.** I restricted all six feat picks to Tier 1 (Blood and Rust) since Step 4 of chargen doesn't gate by tier explicitly, but Tier 2/3 headers plus the Advancement section's "Purchase a Feat: costs 3 DP" language strongly implies Tier 1 is the intended Novice pool and Tier 2/3 are Milestone-gated. Worth stating that explicitly in the chargen doc if it isn't already, since nothing currently stops a Novice from picking Riposte or The Vanguard/Shield Brother straight out of the gate.

**5. RESOLVED — Block/Shield Value math.** Original Block formula (`2D6 + Prowess + Block + Shield Value`) stacked Shield Value directly into the opposed roll alongside Prowess and Block skill, rather than mitigating damage after a loss the way Armor and Brace's +2 WT do. At Roland's Prowess 3/Block 3/Kite Shield 4, that produced `2d6+10` against Bestiary Strike totals in the `2d6+4` to `2d6+6` range — functionally unhittable by choice, with no accompanying cost, and it would only get worse once Advancement pushes attributes past the Novice +3 cap. Corrected per TheTao's direction, restoring the original intent:

> **Block:** 2D6 + Prowess + Block. If you win, no Impact. If you lose, subtract your Shield's Value from the Impact (minimum 0) before comparing it to your Wound Threshold.

This also surfaced a live inconsistency: *Iron World*'s trap-hazard section already described a third variant ("add Shield Value to Threshold if you lose") that predates neither the roll-stacking version nor this fix — that line needs to be reworded to match ("subtract Shield Value from Impact") so only one shield mechanic exists across the project.

**Bulwark redefined** (Tower Shield tag) to avoid reintroducing the same problem in miniature — moved off roll/Impact math entirely and onto positional control, tying into the Bestiary's existing Orc Line-Breaker *Unstoppable Mass* ability:

> **Bulwark:** The shield's mass lets you root yourself in place. While readied, you cannot be Shoved, knocked Prone, or forced out of your Threat Zone as a result of losing a Clash. Once per Scene, when you lose a Block Clash, you may spend 1 Momentum to reduce that Impact to 0 instead of applying your Shield Value.

**Open sign-off item — RESOLVED:** Precise and Armor-Piercing ignore only the target's worn **Armor Value** (the component folded into Wound Threshold), not Shield Value. A shield physically interposes and stops the blow; Precise/AP represents finding gaps in worn plating, not punching through an actively-held shield. Updated tag text:

> **Precise:** Ignores 1 point of the target's Armor Value (not Shield Value).
> **Armor-Piercing:** Ignores 2 points of the target's Armor Value (not Shield Value) when calculating Impact — twice Precise's ignore-1.
