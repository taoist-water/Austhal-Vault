### Arcane Protection (Spell)

The air around the target thickens into a dull, shimmering haze, dampening the resonance of hostile sorcery.

- Level: Novice
    
- Stress Cost: 1 Stress
    
- Target: Self
    
- Action type: Reactor, Act
    
- Duration:  whilst at least 1 stress remains locked/2+Arcana rounds.
    

Effect:

- The Aegis: Hostile spells or supernatural effects targeting the protected character suffer Disadvantage on the casting roll (roll 3d6, keep the lowest two).
    
- Impact Mitigation: If cast as a reactor action: Roll a Block using WIts + arcana. Win and receive no impact. Lose and resolve the impact with an [SV 2] modifier.
    

Special Interactions:

- High Resonance (+1 Stress): the caster can lock 1 extra stress so the protection is reinforced. The penalty to hostile powers increases to a flat -4 penalty (replacing Disadvantage), and the [SV]  increases to 4.

### Bolt (Spell)

A concentrated bolt of raw energy—fire, lightning, or force—streaks from the caster's hand toward a single foe.

- Level: Novice
    
- Stress Cost: 1 Stress
    
- Target: One character within LOS, Medium Range
    
- Action type: Act (Aggressor Action: 2D6 + Wits + Arcana)
    
- Effect:
    

- Impact: On a success the bolt strikes the target. The attack has a base Impact Power of 2.
    

- Special Interactions:
    

- High Resonance (+1 Stress): The caster can push the spell’s intensity, increasing the Impact Power to 4 for this casting.
    
- Bolt Barrage (+1 Stress): The caster fires an additional bolt at a second target within range for each additional Stress taken.

### Blast (Spell)

The caster hurls a ball of energy that explodes on impact, catching multiple foes in its radius.

- Level: Adept
    
- Stress Cost: 3 Stress
    
- Paradigm: Elementalist
    
- Target: A point within LOS (Large Blast Radius), Medium
    
- Action type: Act (Aggressor Action: 2D6 + Wits + Arcana)
    
- Effect:
    

- Explosion: All characters within 10ft of the point of impact must make a reactor action  
    
- Concussion: Those who fail suffer an Impact Power of 3. This damage is applied directly to their Wound Threshold [T].
    

- Special Interactions:
    

- High Resonance (+2 Stress): The blast is so intense it ignores the first 2 points of Armor on all targets caught in the radius.

### Healing (Spell)

The caster knits flesh and mends bone through sheer spiritual or arcane will.

- Level: Novice
    
- Stress Cost: 3 Stress
    
- Target: Touch
    
- Action type: Act (2d6 + Will + Faith)
    
- Effect:
    

- Mend Flesh: A successful casting immediately clears 1 Wound slot from the target.
    
- Stabilize: If the target is Incapacitated, a success stabilizes them and prevents further death checks.
    

- Special Interactions:
    

- High Resonance (Success with Margin 4+): The target also clears 2 Stress as the magical energy refreshes their spirit.
    
- Golden Rule: A character cannot benefit from more than one magical healing effect per Scene.

### Boost/Lower Trait (Spell)

The caster alters the target's fundamental capabilities, either heightening their reflexes or sapping their strength.

- Level: Novice
    
- Paradigm: Transmutation
    
- Stress Cost: 2 Stress
    
- Target: One character within LOS, Short Range.
    
- Duration: 2+Arcana rounds.
    
- Action type: Act (2d6 + Wits + Arcana V Will + Resolve, if unwilling)
    
- Effect:
    

- The Shift: Choose one Attribute (Prowess, Reflex, Wits, or Will). The target gains a +1 bonus (Boost) or a -1 penalty (Lower) to all checks using that Attribute.
    

- Special Interactions:
    

- High Resonance (+2 Stress): The magnitude of the shift increases to +/- 2.
    
- Stacking: A character can only have one Boost or Lower effect active on them at a time. A second casting replaces the first.

- Level: Master

### Puppet

The caster seizes control of the target's motor functions, turning a foe into a literal marionette.

### Resurrection

The most profound and taxing of all spells; the caster reaches into the void to pull a soul back to its broken vessel.

- Level: Master
    
- Stress Cost: 30 Stress
    
- Target: Touch (Character deceased within 24 hours)
    
- Action type: Act (Requires a full hour of ritual)
    
- Effect:
    

- The Great Recall: If the casting check succeeds, the target returns to life with 3 Wounds and Maximum Stress.
    
- The Price: Because the Stress cost (30) will almost certainly exceed any caster's Stress Limit, the caster will suffer massive Wounds and likely fall into a coma or die to complete the spell.
    

- Special Interactions:
    

- Soul Scar: The resurrected character loses 1 point of permanent Will (spiritual weight) as the price of their return.
