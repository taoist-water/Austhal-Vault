This is a very smart pivot. You’ve recognized a classic game design trap: building an economy with too many faucets (Threat generation) and not enough sinks (Threat spending).

If a Priest is using _The Reprieve_ to hand the GM Threat during a fight against standard Fodder or Grunts (who lack heavy Threat abilities), that Threat just sits there doing nothing, which breaks the "Certainty and Sacrifice" loop. The sacrifice must _always_ hurt.

To fix this, we need to build a **Universal Threat Menu** for the GM. This menu gives the GM ways to weaponize Threat against the players globally—targeting their positioning, their resources, or their environment—even if there isn't a Boss on the board.

After we lock that in, we will tackle the **Domain Spells piecemeal**. I will give you a fully fleshed-out suite for the **Domain of Strategy** today, keeping my output focused and detailed so we don't trip your token limits.

### Part 1: The Universal GM Threat Menu (For `Tools for the Nameless.md.txt`)

This needs to go directly into the GM Tools document under the "Threat Scale" section. It provides concrete, mathematically balanced levers for the GM to pull.

**Add this section to the GM Tools:**

> ### The Universal Threat Spends
> 
> The GM is not limited to using Threat solely on monster stat blocks. Threat is the currency of the Void pushing back against the players. The GM may spend Threat from their pool at any time to trigger the following Universal effects:
> 
> **1 Threat: The Complication (Friction)**
> 
> - **Equipment Malfunction:** An unopposed task suddenly becomes messy. A lockpick snaps, a torch gutters out, or a rope frays. Introduce an immediate **Disadvantage** to a player's next non-combat roll.
>     
> - **The Slipping Footing:** A piece of normal terrain suddenly becomes _Difficult Terrain_ right as a player moves through it, halving their movement for that turn.
>     
> 
> **2 Threat: The Attrition Tax (Resource Drain)**
> 
> - **Dread Resonance:** The atmosphere of the dungeon thickens unnaturally. The GM instantly inflicts **1 Dissonant Stress** on up to two players of their choice. (This is a brutal counter to a Priest generating Threat to clear their own stress).
>     
> - **Momentum Bleed:** The enemy seizes the narrative upper hand. The GM immediately drains **1 Progress Momentum** from the party's collective pool.
>     
> 
> **3 Threat: The Tactical Shift (Battlefield Control)**
> 
> - **Reinforcements:** The sound of battle draws attention. At the start of the next round, 1 Grunt or 1d3 Fodder enemies enter the engagement map from an edge.
>     
> - **Environmental Collapse:** A physical barrier in the room gives way. The GM targets one piece of _Full/Partial Cover_ and destroys it, or drops the ceiling to create a 3x3 square zone of _Heavily Obscured_ dust/smoke.
>     
> 
> **5 Threat: The Crisis (Absolute Devastation)**
> 
> - **The Void's Mandate:** The GM interrupts the normal Activation Order. A chosen enemy (usually an Elite or Boss) immediately takes a full, out-of-sequence Activation.
>     

**Designer's Note on this Menu:** Notice how these specific spends directly attack the core pillars we just finished polishing: Stress, Momentum, and the Environment (Cover/Obscuration). This keeps the whole system singing in harmony.

### Part 2: Domain Fleshing Out (Piecemeal, Part 1)

Let's tackle **The Domain of Strategy (The Cult of the Iron Horizon)**.

- **Paragon:** Saint Senecus the Unyielding
    
- **Theme:** Action economy, positioning, and unyielding defense. Strategy spells do not deal damage; they bend the rules of combat time and space to ensure the party fights with terrifying, mechanized efficiency.
    

Here is a complete progression (Novice, Apprentice, Master) for this Domain. (You already have _Unbroken Line_ from our previous session, so I am integrating it here as the baseline).

#### 1. Unbroken Line (Novice Miracle)

- **Cost:** 1 Locked Stress
    
- **Action Type:** Free Reaction (Declared on an ally's turn)
    
- **The Effect:** The Priest invokes Saint Senecus, transferring supreme tactical clarity. The target ally immediately gains the benefits of the **Regroup** action (clearing Ablaze/Anchored conditions, or dropping normal Dissonant Stress) _without_ spending their turn, allowing them to remain fully on the offensive.
    

#### 2. The Iron Phalanx (Novice Miracle)

- **Cost:** 1 Locked Stress
    
- **Action Type:** Free Reaction (Declared when an ally is targeted by a Strike)
    
- **The Effect:** The Priest physically projects the concept of a shield wall through the Void. The target ally immediately gains the benefits of the **Brace** action (reducing incoming Impact by 2) against the triggering attack, without having to spend their own Activation to do so.
    

#### 3. Senecus’s March (Adept Miracle)

- **Cost:** 2 Locked Stress
    
- **Action Type:** Activation
    
- **The Effect:** The Priest hammers their weapon against their shield (or the floor), dictating the rhythm of the battlefield. Up to 3 allies of the Priest's choice may immediately move up to their Move Value. This movement represents supernatural tactical synchronization—**it does not provoke Engagement attacks** from leaving enemy Threat Zones.
    
- _Designer Note: This is an incredible tool to instantly break a party out of a bad choke point or set up massive Flanking Advantage._
    

#### 4. The Commander’s Mandate (Master Miracle)

- **Cost:** 4 Locked Stress
    
- **Action Type:** Activation
    
- **The Effect:** The Priest speaks with the absolute, booming authority of the Iron Horizon, bending the flow of time itself. The Priest targets one ally. That ally immediately takes a **full, secondary Activation** (Move + Action) out of the normal initiative sequence. This does not consume the ally's normal turn for the round.
    
- _Designer Note: 4 Locked Stress is an agonizing cost that will likely push a Priest to their breaking point, but granting your heavy hitter a second full turn to wipe an Elite off the board is the ultimate manifestation of "Strategy."_
