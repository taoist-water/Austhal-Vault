# Steel and Splinters: Weapon Conversion
*Converting the classic Simple/Martial/Exotic weapon tables into Iron & Marrow terms.*

See chat for the full conversion methodology (Power derivation, crit range/multiplier → Precise/Inertia mapping, new tags, dropped categories). Iron & Marrow doesn't gate weapons behind a proficiency tax the way d20 does — any character with the relevant Melee/Ranged skill can pick any of these up — so the Simple/Martial/Exotic distinction is dropped entirely in favor of organizing by Specialization Group, the way the rest of *Hardware*'s Weapons table already does.

**New Tags:** Trip, Disarm, Set, Double, Repeating, Entangling, Armor-Piercing (formalized — it already appeared on Light Crossbow/Heavy Arbalest/Estoc but was never actually defined in the Weapon Tags list).

**New Specialization Groups:** Flails, Thrown.

________________________________________________________________________

## New Weapon Tags

- **Trip:** As an Aggressor Strike with this weapon, you may forgo Impact on a win to instead knock the target Prone.
- **Disarm:** As an Aggressor Strike with this weapon, you may forgo Impact on a win to force the target to pass a Prowess check vs TN 8 or drop what they're holding into an adjacent square.
- **Set:** If this weapon is readied and an enemy voluntarily moves into your Threat Zone, your Strike against them gains a Charge's +2 Clash bonus and tie-break — without the -2 Reactor penalty a real Charge imposes on you.
- **Double:** This two-handed weapon has two striking ends, each with its own Power (listed X/Y). It functions as a built-in Twin-Blade Stance: spend 1 Momentum on a won Clash to immediately follow up with the second Power value as Impact + 1 Stress, without needing a separate Sidearm weapon in your off hand.
- **Repeating:** Holds multiple shots internally; does not require the Reload action between individual shots. Once the magazine is empty, reloading it fully requires a full Action.
- **Entangling:** As an Aggressor action, forgo Impact on a win to instead apply the Anchored condition to the target (identical to the Entangle spell's effect).
- **Armor-Piercing:** Ignores 2 points of the target's Armor Value when calculating Impact.

## Not Converted (and why)

- **All loose ammunition** (arrows, three separate bolt varieties, sling bullets, blowgun darts) — already fully abstracted by the Community Supply Die. Pricing these individually would undo that abstraction.
- **Shield (light/heavy) as a weapon, Spiked Shield** — the existing Shields already have SV, and offensive shield use is already covered by the Shove action plus the Shield Spikes add-on already in the core Armour section.
- **Spiked Armor as a weapon** — this is the Armor Spikes add-on already in the core Armour section, not a separate weapon entry.
- **Composite Shortbow/Longbow** — mechanically identical to the base weapon with the Balanced Quality tag applied (+100% cost, reroll a natural 1 once per scene). Not a separate catalog line.
- **The "monk" tag** — d20 uses this to gate weapons behind the Monk class's unarmed-strike progression, which Iron & Marrow has no equivalent subsystem for. Dropped silently rather than forcing a translation that doesn't map to anything.

## Open Question for You

This is a much bigger addition to the core Weapons table than the two armor tiers were — ~35 new weapons plus two new Specialization Groups (Flails, Thrown) and seven new tags. Given the precedent we set with Armor, I'd want your sign-off before folding this into *Hardware* proper rather than assuming it. A few ways to handle it:

1. **Merge all of it in** — full weapons roster, matching the Armor precedent.
2. **Merge a curated subset** — you pick which ones actually add something to your table, skip the long tail (e.g., keep Rapier/Scimitar/Flail/Trident/Whip, skip Urgosh/Ranseur/Gnome Hooked Hammer-tier exotica unless you specifically want that flavor available).
3. **Leave it standalone** as a GM-optional supplement, not merged into core.
