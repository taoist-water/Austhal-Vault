# \[cm-05\] Rest and Recuperation

## The Breather

A desperate pause in the dark to bind wounds and steady racing hearts.

- **The Mechanics:** Taking a Breather requires 30 uninterrupted in-game minutes. Completing it clears all standard Dissonant Stress, resetting the Death Spiral.
    
- **The Cost:** The adrenaline fades; all participating players immediately drain their **Momentum Bank** to 0. It does not heal physical Wounds and cannot clear Locked Stress.

## Long Rest:
a period of downtime, lasting at least typically 8 hours, during which a character does nothing more strenuous than eating, drinking, uninterrupted sleeping.
