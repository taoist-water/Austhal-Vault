````markdown
# District: The High Quarter
#location/district #status/draft

> "Five coppers to walk up the hill. The view's free, once you've paid for the walk." — Muddy Docks saying

---

## 🏙️ District Profile
- **Settlement:** [[Port Nevarellon]]
- **Position:** The top of the northern limestone cliffs, at the seaward end of the bay. The [[The Great Anchor Basin|Basin]] lies in the deep water below; the [[The Trade Plazas|Trade Plazas]] fall away inland.
- **Social Stratum:** Old nobility, Council patrons and the Zenith's clergy. Landed almost to a household.
- **Architecture & Infrastructure:** The finest limestone in the city — walled houses, walled gardens, and almost no timber. With no sun-side to build toward, the streets climb in terraces cut for drainage. The main avenues are lit by [[The Brine-Glow Lanterns|Brine-Glow lanterns]].
- **Population:** ~2,500, most of them servants who live in.

---

## ⚖️ Law, Order & Safety
- **Guarding Presence:** The heaviest in the city. Company patrols run day and night. The [[Faction - The Civic Constabulary (The Coppers)|Blue-Cloak Watch]] at the toll-gate look professional, because Company officers are watching them.
- **The Toll:** 5 cp to enter from below — more than a dockhand earns in a day, which is the point of it. Once a year, on the **Toll Amnesty**, anyone may walk in free. Most don't.
- **Local Customs:** Register B names, and nobody asks where yours came from if you Latinised well. A Crooked Coin devotional name here is a liability.
- **Crime Level:** White-collar. Forged instruments, bought magistrates, and the [[The Cobalt Feather Syndicate|Cobalt Feather]]'s rogue alchemists buying god-shards behind good doors.

---

## 🥖 Economy & Logistics
- **Water:** The city's cisterns lie beneath the Quarter. They collect the rain, and the [[Tythius De Vonce|Spine Aqueduct]] ends at the Quarter's landward edge. Every year on **Lastwater** the cisterns are measured in public and the price of water is set. The water sold in the Docks by the barrel is carted down from here.
- **Sanitation:** Subterranean flush-vaults that vent past the eastern cliffs, into the sea and not into the harbour.
- **Access down:** Stairs and a single cart-road down the cliff to the Basin. The Quarter's warehouse edge is creeping downhill: [[Verrine Sallow]]'s house stands on a street that was warehouses within living memory.

---

## 📍 Key Locations & Sub-Locations
- **The Old Seat** — the King's palace, on the crown of the cliffs. The Golden Company stormed it at 0 A.A., and a folk hero slew the last King inside it. **It has stood empty ever since.** The Council would not sit in it, the Company will not garrison it, and no house can take title to it, because a deed to the royal seat would be a claim to the crown. It is left to moss, exactly as the Council's toast wishes. *(Its name avoids "King" — the King-Ban covers place names.)*
- **The Plumb Court** — the [[The Cult of the Zenith|Zenith]]'s windowless limestone hall around the Register vault. It has never burned. [[High Arbiter Sevrin Kalder|Kalder]] keeps cloister quarters here, and [[Keeper Merrit Dray|Dray]] lives forty feet from the vault.
- **The Guild Chapter-House** — on the seaward edge, with the observatory and instrument room on its roof (see [[The Guild of Alchemists]]).
- **The Vantry house** — the oldest continuously occupied private residence in the city. It is [[Palla Vantry]]'s.
- **[[Lucia Marrenhal]]'s house** — deliberately the plainest frontage on its street.
- **The cisterns** — vaulted, cold, and tended by Un-Landed cistern-men, who see everything and tell no one.

---

## 👥 Power Players
- **Local Authority:** The Council, in practice — three of the five councillors live here ([[Lucia Marrenhal|Marrenhal]], [[Palla Vantry|Vantry]] and [[Verrine Sallow|Sallow]]). Kress and Thole pointedly do not.
- **Dominant Factions:** [[The Cult of the Zenith]]; [[The Golden Company]]; the Council's patron houses.

---

## 🧱 Layer & Mote
**Layer 1 ground.** Two of the city's Layer 1 chokepoints are physically here: the Register and the water.
- **The mote:** the Toll Amnesty — one day a year when the hill belongs to everyone. The Council calls it mercy. It is crowd management. It is still theirs.

> **GM-facing.** The Old Seat is the one building in the city nobody can own. If the [[The Twelgorn Kingdom|Unaging Heir]] ever comes home, this is where they will walk.

---

- The cistern-men: who employs them — the Water Seat, or a contractor?
````

````markdown
# District: The Trade Plazas
#location/district #status/draft

> "Up the hill they buy the law. Down the mud they break it. Here we just sell it by the yard." — Artisan Quarter saying

---

## 🏙️ District Profile
- **Settlement:** [[Port Nevarellon]]
- **Position:** The rising ground between [[The High Quarter]]'s cliffs and the bay head, inland of [[The Great Anchor Basin|the Basin]]. It borders [[The Muddy Docks]] at the toll-gate. The [[The Cult of the Zenith|Plumb Court]] is a twenty-minute walk uphill.
- **Social Stratum:** The middling city — merchants, masters, clerks, journeymen and servants. The only district where Landed and Un-Landed live on the same streets.
- **Population:** ~8,000.
- **Three neighbourhoods:**
  - **The Plazas proper** — cobbled squares, banking houses and guild-halls.
  - **The Merchant Quarter** — merchants' houses, behind the toll-gate from the Docks.
  - **The Artisan Quarter** — workshops, small masters and journeymen.

---

## ⚖️ Law, Order & Safety
- **The Toll:** 1 cp to cross from the Docks into the Merchant Quarter — a third of a day's wage, just to reach a better market.
- **Guarding Presence:** The Company patrols the Plazas. The Watch hold the toll-gates and look professional there. In the Artisan Quarter the Watch is thin after dark, and the guilds' own search does much of the policing.
- **Crime Level:** Corruption at the top, petty theft and forged stamps at the bottom.

---

## 📍 The Plazas Proper
- **The Council Chamber** — above the Plazas, by design: the Council would not sit in the Old Seat.
- **The High Courts and the magistrates' buildings** — commercial and criminal matters inside the city, for the Landed only. [[Provost Halvard Stross]] keeps an office-residence beside them.
- **The Council clerk-house** — where the turn-roll is published.
- **The guild-halls** — the "legal guilds" of the city (see [[Framework - The Guilds of Port Nevarellon]]).
- **The banking houses**, and the upscale markets.
- **The Harbour Master's tower** — stone, near the Plazas' seaward edge. The Harbour Master stays barricaded in it.
- **The Gull Market** — the spring trade season opens here, and the city's population doubles.

---

## 🏠 The Merchant Quarter
Factors, small Landed merchants and new money, in houses that are larger than they are old. It sits directly behind the toll-gate from the Docks, which is why the gate is called the Merchant Quarter gate.
- **[[Ottavian Kress]]'s house** — large, loud and badly kept, on the Merchant Quarter side of the toll-gate. Pointedly not the High Quarter, and pointedly not the Docks.

---

## 🔨 The Artisan Quarter
**The working middle of the city**, and until now the part of it nobody had written.
- **Who lives here:** masters on their shop charters, with their journeymen and apprentices lodged above the shop. Clerks, servants, smiths at small forges, and widows keeping a dead master's bench under the guild box.
- **What happens here:** most of the city's legal retail. Stamp-rent counters, where the Docks' carvers and fishers pay for a master's wax, line the streets nearest the toll-gate.
- **What it looks like:** timber-framed upper storeys on stone ground floors. Shop signs with freshly Latinised names, which the High Quarter finds hilarious.
- **[[Alfric Danniken]]'s townhouse** — modest and well staffed, kept for "immediate business."

---

## 👥 Power Players
- **Local Authority:** The Council (the Chamber) and the High Courts; in the Artisan Quarter, in practice, the guild-masters.
- **Dominant Factions:** [[Council of Five]]; the city guilds; [[The Golden Company]] (patrols); [[The Cobalt Feather Syndicate]] (forged stamps and instruments).

---

## 🧱 Layer & Mote
The Plazas are **Layer 1** — the Council, the High Courts and the turn-roll are all here. The Artisan Quarter is **Layer 3**, and it is where the guild system is actually lived.
- **The mote:** this is the one district where people climb. A journeyman can make master, and a master's name can go up over a door. It does not happen often, but it happens here and nowhere else.

---

## ❓ Open Questions
````

````markdown
# District: The Foundry Slips
#location/district #status/draft

> "In the Slips you can tell a man's trade by his cough." — Foundry Slips saying

---

## 🏙️ District Profile
- **Settlement:** [[Port Nevarellon]]
- **Position:** The south shore of the bay — flat, gently shelving ground, which is what a slipway needs. It runs from [[The Sunken Ward]] at the bay head toward the harbour mouth, and it is backed by the landward curtain wall at its weakest.
- **Social Stratum:** Working — skilled and unskilled yard labour, and their families. [[Marcian Thole]] is the only councillor who lives here.
- **Architecture & Infrastructure:** Slipways, sheds, timber row-houses, and smoke. Coal-fired refineries and fish-curing smokehouses hang a grey ceiling over the district on still days.
- **Population:** ~8,000 resident, and more by day: much of [[The Sunken Ward|Cinder Row]] walks in each morning to work and walks home to sleep.

---

## ⚖️ Law, Order & Safety
- **Guarding Presence:** Thin. The Watch keeps toll-houses at the edges. The Company's defence money goes to the Basin and the High Quarter, which is why the wall here has never been surveyed.
- **The Yards' crews:** what order the Slips have comes mostly from the **Thole Yards' fire-and-flood crews** — four hundred people, drilled, and classified as insurance compliance. It is a militia, and it is not called one on any instrument.
- **Crime Level:** Moderate — theft from the yards, fights at the pits, and the [[Silas Bane|Young Bloods]] recruiting.

---

## 🥖 Economy & Logistics
- **The yards:** the [[Framework - The Guilds of Port Nevarellon|Shipwrights' Guild]]'s ground. They run on De Vonce iron and timber, and Iron-Burl from [[Divtown]].
- **The smokehouses:** cured fish, one of the city's two chief exports. The fish-smokers' lungs go before they are forty.
- **The refineries:** coal-fired, licensed by [[The Guild of Alchemists]].
- **Hiring:** the **Boot-Change**, the annual hiring day, is held here: the whole year decided in a morning.

---

## 📍 Key Locations & Sub-Locations
- **The Thole Yards** — four generations of hulls, and four hundred Un-Landed at Guild rates. Thole lives in the converted sail-loft above them. [[Lucia Marrenhal]] holds the mortgage and has never mentioned it.
- **The rope-walk** — a shed a quarter-mile long, where rigging is laid. Suse Kellard runs it on a Shipwrights' stamp, the only woman in the Slips with one.
- **[[The Rusty Anchor Foundry]]** — a working foundry with fighting pits beneath, and [[Silas Bane]]'s guarded suite above them.
- **The weak wall** — the curtain wall's poorest stretch. Thole has tried three times to get it surveyed, and his fourth attempt is coming.
- **The debtors' hulks** — old hulls moored just off the Slips. The debt-prisons are afloat: each dawn the debtors are rowed ashore to work the yards, and each dusk they are rowed back. Most of them are working off a Debt-Bond that will never close (see [[Law - The Council's Edicts]]).
- **Beyond the wall, downwind:** the [[The Guild of Alchemists|Guild]]'s nitre-yards, and the city's burial grounds and paupers' pits, where the Ashen Veil's **Pauper's Procession** ends — the free mass burial of the Sunken Ward's unclaimed dead.

---

## 👥 Power Players
- **Local Authority:** [[Marcian Thole]], in all but name.
- **Dominant Factions:** the Shipwrights' Guild; [[The Guild of Alchemists]] (refineries and nitre-yards); [[The Iron-Anchor Syndicate]]'s Young Bloods at the Rusty Anchor.

---

## 🧱 Layer & Mote
**Layer 3**, with one Layer 1 weakness built into it: the wall. If the city is ever taken from the land, it will be taken here.
- **The mote:** when fire comes — and in a district of coal, pitch and smokehouses it comes every season — the Yards' crews turn out for every house on the street, not only the Yards'. Nobody pays them to.

---

## ❓ Open Questions
- Who owns the debtors' hulks — the Council, a contractor, or the yards that use the labour?
- Does Silas know what Thole's crews really are?

## 🔗 Connected Notes
[[Port Nevarellon]] · [[Marcian Thole]] · [[Silas Bane]] · [[The Sunken Ward]] · [[Framework - The Guilds of Port Nevarellon]] · [[The Guild of Alchemists]]
````
